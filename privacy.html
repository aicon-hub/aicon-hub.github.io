Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

AiCoN is a free guide to useful tech and opportunities. This page explains what the AiCoN site and Android app do with your information, in plain words.

No account, no sign-in

You do not need an account, email or phone number to read AiCoN or use the app. We do not ask for your name, contacts, location or files.

What stays on your device

The app stores a few things only on your phone, so features work:

  • Your settings: theme, text size and reminder preference.
  • Reading insights: your streak, reading time and daily goal progress.
  • Guides you opened, used to shape the For You tab.

This data never leaves your phone. There is no AiCoN server that receives it, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Analytics and ads

The AiCoN app has no analytics, advertising trackers or profiling.

On the website, article pages load Google Analytics (gtag.js from Google) to count visits and see which guides are read. Google processes that usage data under its own privacy policy. The home page and guide index do not load it. We do not run ads and we do not sell data.

What the app connects to

  • The app downloads the guide index and article pages from aicon-hub.github.io so you can read them.
  • If you turn on deadline reminders, the check runs on your phone. No data is sent anywhere.
  • The update check reads the public version file on aicon-hub.github.io.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under its own privacy policy.

External links

Our guides link to official websites and apps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control what they collect. Check their policies before signing up or sharing data with them.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email aicon4tech@gmail.com or join the AiCoN Telegram channel.
