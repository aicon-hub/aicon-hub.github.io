# Security policy

Thanks for helping keep AiCoN and its readers safe. This policy covers
the AiCoN website (aicon-hub.github.io) and the AiCoN Android app.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues through one of AiCoN's official public
channels:

- Email: aicon4tech@proton.me (backup: aicon4tech@gmail.com)
- Telegram: https://t.me/AiCoN4tech

Please include:

- A clear description of the issue and its possible impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The affected page, feature, or app version
- Your suggested severity, if you have one

Please do not open a public GitHub issue for a vulnerability. Give us a
reasonable chance to investigate and fix before any public disclosure.

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days.
- After investigating, we will tell you whether the report is accepted,
  needs more detail, or is out of scope, and what happens next.
- If a fix is needed, we will tell you when it ships.

AiCoN is a small independent project. We cannot promise a bounty or a
reward, but we are happy to credit reporters in a release note if they
want that.

## Scope

In scope:

- The AiCoN website and its generated pages, service worker, and app
  shell (app mode)
- The AiCoN Android app distributed from this site
- The build and publish scripts in this repository

Out of scope:

- Third-party sites, services, and images we link to or embed
- Issues that only affect outdated app versions (please update first)
- Content corrections or editorial disagreements (use the normal
  contact channels instead)
- Denial-of-service attempts, spam, and social engineering of readers

The current live site and the latest app version are the supported
versions; older versions are not maintained separately.

## Researcher safety

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, avoid accessing
or modifying other people's data, and do not disrupt the service, we
will not pursue or support legal action against your research.
