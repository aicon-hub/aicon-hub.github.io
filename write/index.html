Write for AiCoN

AiCoN now publishes reader contributors. You bring the topic - we check the facts and publish the guide under your name, with your own author profile.

How it works

  1. Email your post. Send everything to aicon4tech@gmail.com: your topic and the full content, your photo, and your public links - GitHub, Instagram, LinkedIn, any other social profiles, and your blog if you have one.
  2. We verify and publish. AiCoN fact-checks every claim against official sources, edits for clarity, and publishes the guide under your name with your own author profile: photo, bio and your links.

Ready to write?

One email is all it takes. Send your content, photo and links - we reply on the same thread.

Email aicon4tech@gmail.com

What we publish

  • Official sources only: product pages, government notices, university announcements.
  • No referral or affiliate links.
  • No piracy, cracks or grey-area downloads.
  • Clear, sharp screenshots - never blurry ones.
  • Plain language. Short sentences. Steps a first-time reader can follow.

What you get

Your own author profile on AiCoN (photo, bio and your public links), a byline on every guide you write, and your work in front of readers across Kerala and India.
