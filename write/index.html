Write for AiCoN

AiCoN now publishes reader contributors. You bring the topic - we check the facts and publish the guide under your name, with your own author profile.

How it works

  1. Watch the WhatsApp channel. Contributor invitations and topic calls are announced on the AiCoN WhatsApp channel first. Join it to hear when a slot opens.
  2. Email your pitch. Send your topic and a short outline to aicon4tech@gmail.com. Add your name, a photo if you want one, and the public links for your author profile (blog, LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram).
  3. We verify and publish. AiCoN checks every claim against official sources, edits for clarity, and publishes the guide under your name with a byline linking to your author profile.

Ready to pitch?

Email your topic and outline. We reply on the same thread.

Email aicon4tech@gmail.com Join the WhatsApp channel

What we publish

  • Official sources only: product pages, government notices, university announcements.
  • No referral or affiliate links.
  • No piracy, cracks or grey-area downloads.
  • Clear, sharp screenshots - never blurry ones.
  • Plain language. Short sentences. Steps a first-time reader can follow.

What you get

Your own author profile on AiCoN (photo, bio and your public links), a byline on every guide you write, and your work in front of readers across Kerala and India.
