Typing.com: free touch typing lessons, tests and games

By AiCoN | Published 15 October 2026 | Sources checked 15 October 2026

Typing.com says its free edition includes the whole Learn to Type course of lessons, tests and games, paid for by ads.

What it is

Typing.com is a website that teaches touch typing. Its support page calls it an online typing tutor and digital literacy platform used by schools around the world. You practise with lessons, tests and games, and you do it in a browser. It is not an AI tool. We included it because it is a free learning resource from an official source.

Is it free?

Partly, and the free part is the typing course. Typing.com's support page says it has a free edition and a PLUS edition. It says the free edition carries "the whole of Learn to Type - its lessons, tests and games" and is supported by ads. PLUS is a paid monthly subscription. It removes the ads, keeps your data indefinitely and unlocks the rest of the curriculum, plus assignments, auto grading and a student monitor for classes. We did not find a PLUS price on the pages we read, because the site says it is shown at checkout in your own currency.

What you get in the free edition

  • Learn to Type: lessons from home row basics upward, typing tests and games.
  • Account optional: the privacy policy says that without an account the site uses a session cookie to track your performance, and that it is lost when you leave. An account lets you save and track progress. A student account needs a username and password, and an email is optional.
  • Teachers: the homepage says the teacher portal is free, with unlimited students, classes and teachers. Some classroom tools are listed as PLUS features on the support page.

What to watch out for

  • Ads. The free edition shows ads. The privacy policy says they are managed by Playwire LLC.
  • Other units are paid. Units such as Digital Citizenship, Coding and Test Prep appear on the homepage, but the support page says PLUS unlocks the rest of the curriculum beyond Learn to Type. Check what shows for your account before you plan around them.
  • Data is kept for 70 days on the free edition, per the support page, while PLUS keeps it indefinitely.
  • Children under 13. The privacy policy says they cannot create their own free student account without consent from a parent, guardian or teacher.
  • Website only. We found no mention of a phone app, and we did not look for one in app stores.

Privacy and data

  • The privacy policy says it will not sell, share or disclose the information it collects, except as the policy lists.
  • It says the free edition uses Google Analytics, which places a cookie and collects your IP address but not your name.
  • It says advertisers get aggregated, de-identified information about user groups and usage, and that Playwire may collect and use aggregated and anonymised data for advertising.
  • It says user data is stored and processed outside the European Union.
  • Parents can ask for their child's data to be deleted by contacting Typing.com.

India

  • We found no India restriction on the pages we read, and the support page says schools around the world use it. Availability from India is not stated, and we did not test it from India.
