Pay your KSEB electricity bill online: Quick Pay and web self service guide

By AiCoN | Published 30 October 2026 | Sources checked 30 October 2026

You can pay a KSEB energy charge bill from your phone without logging in. The KSEB Web Self Service portal (wss.kseb.in) has a Quick Pay page: enter your 13 digit consumer number and the mobile number registered with KSEB. We opened the portal on 30 October 2026 and read the page as listed. We did not enter a consumer number or make a payment.

What you need

  • Consumer number: the 13 digit number on your bill.
  • Registered mobile number: Quick Pay needs a mobile number already registered with KSEB. The page has a link to register one if you have not.
  • Captcha: type the text shown in the image.

Conditions listed on the page

  • Quick Pay is for energy charge bills, annual ACD bills and LED bills only.
  • Installment bills can be paid only at the counter.
  • Rebate does not apply to advance amounts paid.
  • If you pay today, the due amount updates only the next day.
  • No success status after paying? Check again after 3 hours.
  • Payments are not allowed from 11:45 PM to 12:15 AM IST because of scheduled activities.
  • The page links to a list of payment options and transaction charges - check it before you pay.

Other services on the portal

  • Online services such as load change, tariff change and ownership change.
  • Register a complaint or service request, and track complaint status.
  • Register your mobile number or email to get alerts for new bills and supply interruptions.
  • New connection applications.
  • Helpline: 1912 or 0471-2555544, 24x7 (as shown on the portal).

Only pay on wss.kseb.in. KSEB's own page also points consumers to the National Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) to report cyber fraud.
