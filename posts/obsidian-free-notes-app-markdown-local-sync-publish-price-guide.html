Obsidian: a free notes app, with paid Sync and Publish add-ons

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

Obsidian is a notes app for your private thoughts, study notes and lesson plans. The official pricing page says it is free without limits and needs no sign-up. You pay only if you want its optional add-on services.

Quick facts

  • What it is: a notes app. The Obsidian home page calls it the free and flexible app for your private thoughts.
  • Price of the app: the pricing page says "Free without limits. No sign-up required."
  • Commercial licence: Rs 4,400 a year per user (about $50, at Rs 88 to the dollar). The pricing page says you are not required to pay it, even for use at work, but organisations are encouraged to.
  • Sync: $4 a month per user, billed annually (about Rs 350 a month). It syncs notes across devices and has end-to-end encryption and version history.
  • Publish: $8 a month per site, billed annually (about Rs 705 a month). It puts your notes on the web.
  • Catalyst: $25 one-time. It gives early access to beta versions and community badges.
  • Refunds: full refund within 7 days for Sync and Publish. Catalyst, Commercial and Credit are non-refundable, per the pricing page.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the Obsidian home page and pricing page on 9 November 2026. We did not install or use Obsidian.

Who is it for?

It suits students and teachers who want one place for study notes, reading notes, lesson plans or a personal journal. AiCoN already has a guide to Joplin, another free notes app. Obsidian is a different tool, so compare them before you pick one.

How to start

  1. Open obsidian.md and download the app for your computer. The home page offers more platforms under "More platforms".
  2. Create a vault, which is where your notes live. You do not need to sign up.
  3. Write a note. Use the official Help pages at obsidian.md/help if you want to learn linking and tags.

Free and paid

Everything above that costs money is optional. If you only write notes on one device, you do not need Sync. If you do not publish a website, you do not need Publish. We did not check for student or education discounts, so ask Obsidian before you pay.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not install or use Obsidian. We cannot say how easy it is for a new user.
  • Prices are in US dollars on the official page. Your bank may add a foreign-exchange fee.
  • Without Sync, your notes stay on the device where you made them. Back them up yourself.
  • Third-party plugins are made by other people, not by Obsidian. We did not check any plugin.
