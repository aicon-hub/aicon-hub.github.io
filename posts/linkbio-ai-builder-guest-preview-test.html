Link.bio AI builder: try a link-in-bio page before signing up

By AiCoN | Checked 18 November 2026

Link.bio's AI builder creates a preview page from a public website link or a short description. Its useful part is assembling a page with buttons, images and theme choices, rather than only writing a bio. We tested two guest builds without creating an account. Publishing was not checked by us.

What can you try free?

The official AI builder lets visitors generate a preview without signing up. Its FAQ says two guest builds per day, and our second build reached that limit. It says AI building is free on every Link.bio account and does not require a card. That is the provider's current statement, not a promise of permanent unlimited access or an audit of every feature.

To claim a username, edit in an account and save a live page, the site asks you to sign up. We inspected that entry page but did not submit an email or use Google sign-in. A preview is not a published page or a reserved username.

How to try the preview

  1. Open link.bio/ai-link-in-bio-generator. This is the clean official link, without a referral or campaign tag.
  2. Paste a public website link you have permission to use, or describe a fictional example. The provider says it accepts up to five links plus a short description.
  3. Choose Build my page and wait for the result. Do not enter private information just to try it.
  4. Compare the bio and theme choices. Check every generated button and its destination before using the page.
  5. Stop at the preview if you do not want an account. Claim starts a signup route; do not assume it is just a download button.

What worked in our test?

First, we described a fictional handmade-notebook studio called Paper Moon in Kochi, without real business accounts, contact details or links. It produced a page preview, three bio choices and three themes. Changing the theme changed the preview. It did not invent shop links in that test.

Second, we pasted the URL of Link.bio's own fictional Brasa Café demonstration website into the real builder. It generated a fresh preview with photos and buttons for a menu, booking, coffee beans and visiting information, plus three bio and theme choices. The button destinations pointed to pages under that sample website. We inspected those destinations, but did not make a booking or purchase.

The second result displayed 8.5 seconds on the site's timer and then said the daily guest allowance was exhausted. This is one observed result, not a speed guarantee. Clicking a built-in example also showed a prebuilt sample; that is separate from the two actual builds above.

Where this is useful

A creator, student club, shop or small business can quickly compare a page layout before deciding whether to create an account. A text chatbot can help write a bio, but this tool puts the bio, link buttons and a theme into a page preview together.

It is less useful if you already have a working link page and only need a sentence rewritten. We did not compare it against competing builders, test a live business site, verify imported social profiles or complete publishing.

Check the privacy trade-off

This is not an offline or on-device-only tool. Link.bio's privacy policy says typed text, information from pasted public links and small image previews are sent to OpenAI for suggestions. It says OpenAI does not use this business-service data for training, but may retain it for up to 30 days for abuse detection.

The policy says unclaimed guest previews and their pictures are kept for one day and then deleted unless claimed. It counts guest tries by IP address for a day. For some social profiles, it uses a separate public-profile scraping provider. These are provider disclosures, not independently audited guarantees.

Use only links and pictures you are allowed to reuse. Review names, claims, photos, buttons and destinations before saving anything live. Do not paste private chats, documents, passwords or personal identifiers.

India availability and test limits

Our check establishes that the public guest generator worked in this browser session. We did not verify an India-based account, regional signup availability or successful publishing. The fictional Kochi wording tested a local business description, not location-specific access.

No account was created, no email inbox was opened, no payment or card was requested during the guest tests, and no page was claimed or published. We did not grant file, contact or social-account access. The provider lists dashboard languages as English, Spanish and Portuguese; Malayalam output was not checked by us.
