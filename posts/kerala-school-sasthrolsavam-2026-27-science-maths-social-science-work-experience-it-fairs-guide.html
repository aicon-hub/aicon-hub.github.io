Kerala School Sasthrolsavam 2026-27: the official KITE portal, results and what to ask your school

By AiCoN | Published 26 October 2026 | Sources checked 26 October 2026

The official Kerala School Sasthrolsavam portal run by KITE (Kerala Infrastructure and Technology for Education) now carries the 2026-27 season. When we checked on 26 October 2026, its home page showed "School Registration Started", and at least one sub-district results page (Thaliparamba South) already had results posted. This guide covers what the portal offers, what the five fairs include and where to look for results. It does not give a fair calendar: we found no state-wide schedule on the official portal, so ask your school for local dates.

At a glance

  • What: the Kerala school fair system (Science, Maths, Social Science, Work Experience and IT), with its official portal at mela.kite.kerala.gov.in
  • Who: school students in Kerala. The portal offers a school login that uses the school's Sampoorna username and password; we found no public student registration
  • Portal status: "School Registration Started" on the 2026 home page; the 2026 menu lists Sub District Results, District Sasthramela, Special School Sasthramela and VHSE Expo 2026
  • Results: sub-district results pages are public and need no login. Thaliparamba South had results posted when we checked; other sub-districts may not yet
  • Dates: not published on the official portal as a state-wide calendar when we checked. Confirm with your school or the local education office
  • Where: mela.kite.kerala.gov.in (official KITE portal)

The five fairs and what students present

From the item overview on the official 2026 home page, checked 26 October 2026 (the portal lists examples and the lists are not exhaustive):

  • Science: collections, charts, short experiments, still models, working models and research-type projects
  • Maths: number charts, geometric charts, still models, puzzles, magazines, games and pure construction work
  • Social Science: collections, working models, still models, speeches, quizzes, atlas making and local history writing
  • Work Experience: practical craft and product work, for example incense-stick making, reed and bamboo products, book binding and coconut-shell products
  • IT: IT quiz, digital painting, Malayalam typing, Scratch programming and animation

How students take part

Checked on the portal on 26 October 2026. We did not log in to anything; this describes what the public pages show.

  1. The portal's login is for schools. The home page tip says to use the Sampoorna username and password for school login. The public pages have no individual student sign-up, so students should go through their school.
  2. The Instructions/Downloads page lists a Sasthrolsavam manual, a user guide for schools, a user guide for sub-district and sample application forms for each fair. The manual link on that page is a 2025 file (sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf), so it describes an earlier season and is not a statement of 2026-27 rules.
  3. For 2026-27 rules, item lists and dates, ask your class teacher or the school's Sasthrolsavam coordinator.
  4. Results appear on the portal's results pages. The sub-district results page for Thaliparamba South shows overall school points and links for Science, Maths, Social Science, Work Experience and IT results, by LP, UP, HS and HSS/VHSE section.

What we could not confirm

  • Fair dates. The official portal did not show a state-wide schedule for 2026-27. We are not listing district or sub-district dates, because we could not confirm them on an official source. They are set locally, so confirm with your school or the education office.
  • The 2026 circular. The portal's "Circular" link for 2026 points to a file that returns a 404 error (not found) when we checked, so it could not be read.
  • Whether your school's sub-district has started. The portal shows registration started and some results posted. That does not mean every sub-district has held its mela or posted results.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official portal? The portal is on the kerala.gov.in domain and its footer says it is designed and maintained by KITE.

Can I register my child directly? The public pages show no individual registration. The login is for schools, so ask your school.

Where do I see results? On the portal's Sub District Results pages, which opened without a login when we checked. A blank or missing page can simply mean that level has not published yet.

Which classes are covered? The results pages group results into LP, UP, HS and HSS/VHSE sections, and some fairs (such as IT) list only UP, HS and HSS/VHSE. Eligibility for each item comes from the manual and your school.

Source: KITE mela portal, 2026 home, its Instructions/Downloads page and the Thaliparamba South sub-district results page, checked live on 26 October 2026. This is an AiCoN explainer, not a publication of KITE.
