CBSE board exams 2027: date sheet for Class 10 and 12, starting 16 February

By AiCoN | Published 7 November 2026 | Sources checked 7 November 2026

CBSE has released the 2027 board exam date sheet 101 days before the first paper. Class 10 papers run from 16 February to 9 March 2027. Class 12 papers run from 16 February to 9 April 2027.

What did CBSE announce?

The Controller of Examinations issued the date sheet circular on 6 November 2026 (CBSE/Date Sheet/X-XII/2026-27). It says the exams begin on Tuesday 16 February 2027, and all papers start at 10:30 am IST.

Class 10 students will appear in 83 subjects and Class 12 students in 121 subjects. CBSE says over 26.5 lakh Class 10 and over 19.8 lakh Class 12 students have registered.

Class 10 key dates

  • Mathematics (Standard and Basic): Wednesday 17 February
  • Science: Monday 22 February
  • Computer Applications, Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence: Wednesday 24 February
  • English (Communicative and Language and Literature): Friday 26 February
  • Malayalam and other regional languages listed by CBSE: Saturday 27 February
  • Hindi Course A and B: Wednesday 3 March
  • Social Science: Monday 8 March
  • Last day: Tuesday 9 March

Class 12 key dates

  • Physics: Tuesday 16 February
  • Accountancy: Tuesday 23 February
  • Chemistry: Saturday 27 February
  • English Core and Elective: Friday 5 March
  • Mathematics and Applied Mathematics: Tuesday 9 March
  • Economics: Tuesday 16 March
  • Political Science: Friday 19 March
  • Biology: Thursday 25 March
  • History: Saturday 27 March
  • Computer Science and Informatics Practices: Wednesday 31 March
  • Business Studies: Thursday 1 April
  • Last day: Friday 9 April

These are the main subjects only. The official PDF lists every subject code, including skill subjects and languages.

Why CBSE says the dates are set this way

The circular says the schedule leaves gaps between subjects that students commonly take together, takes the tentative dates of major entrance exams into account, and checks that no two subjects chosen by the same student fall on one day. More than 40,000 subject combinations were considered.

What students and parents should do

  1. Open the date sheet PDF on cbse.gov.in and find your own subject codes.
  2. Check the admit card when your school issues it. CBSE says to follow the instructions printed there.
  3. Note that CBSE says no communication devices are allowed inside the examination centre.
  4. Ask your school about practical and internal assessment dates. This guide does not list them.

What AiCoN could not check

  • Practical exam dates. News reports mention 1 January to 13 February 2027, but we did not find them in the date sheet PDF, so check your school notice.
  • Any later change. CBSE says important instructions will be issued separately.
