Tinkercad: free browser 3D design for beginners

By AiCoN | Published 16 October 2026 | Sources checked 16 October 2026

Autodesk lists Tinkercad as free beginner 3D design in the browser, for educational and personal projects. You need an account, and Autodesk says it is generally not for commercial use.

What it is

Tinkercad is a browser-based 3D design app from Autodesk. Its own site calls it "a free, easy-to-use app for 3D design, electronics, and coding". Autodesk describes it as "a beginner-friendly, browser-based CAD tool" with a drag-and-drop interface, aimed at people new to 3D design or 3D printing. It is not an AI tool. We included it as a free creative learning tool.

Is it free?

Yes, per Autodesk. Autodesk lists Tinkercad on its free CAD software page and says it is "designed for educational and personal projects". That page also says Autodesk's free CAD tools are "generally intended for non-commercial use". If you plan to sell what you make or use it for a business, check Autodesk's terms first. We did not find a price or paid plan for Tinkercad on the pages we read.

What you can do with it

  • Design in 3D with basic shapes: Autodesk says you can "experiment with basic shapes and concepts without needing advanced design skills".
  • Use it in a browser: there is nothing to install. You need an account to save work.
  • 3D design, electronics and coding: the three areas named on Tinkercad's own site. We did not open the editor, so we cannot say how each one works.
  • Move on later: Autodesk says beginners can start with Tinkercad and later move to its professional tools, Fusion and AutoCAD.

What to watch out for

  • It is a beginner tool. Autodesk says Tinkercad "lacks the advanced features needed for complex designs".
  • Needs an account and internet. It runs in the browser.
  • The site has no Hindi. The language list on the Tinkercad home page covers English, Czech, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Hungarian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Hindi is not on it.
  • Check the date on the terms. The Tinkercad terms page on Autodesk's site says "Last Updated: December 2019".

Privacy and data

  • Age: the terms say a person aged 13 or older can create their own account. A child under 13 can have an account only with a parent or guardian's approval, or as a student through a class set up by a teacher.
  • Your designs: the terms say Autodesk does not claim ownership of your content. By posting or sharing it you give Autodesk and its affiliates a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual licence to use it in connection with the service and its business, for example to promote the service. The terms say shared content "can and may be viewed worldwide", so do not put personal details in public designs.
  • Sensitive data: the terms say the storage features are not meant for card numbers, ID numbers, medical information and similar data.
  • The terms point to Autodesk's Privacy Statement and Children's Privacy Statement for how data is collected and used. We did not read those in full.

India

  • We did not find any page saying Tinkercad is blocked or limited in India, and we could not confirm availability for India on the pages we read. Try it in your browser. No rupee price is shown on the pages we read.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tinkercad free?

Yes, per Autodesk, for educational and personal projects. Autodesk says its free CAD tools are generally for non-commercial use.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Autodesk calls it browser-based.

Is it safe for kids?

The terms allow under-13 accounts only with parent or teacher approval. Read the children's privacy statement before signing a child up.
