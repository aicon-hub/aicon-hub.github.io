IndiaAI Fellowship 2026-27: up to Rs 2 lakh, eligibility and how to apply

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

Applications are open for the IndiaAI Fellowship 2026-27 under the IndiaAI Mission (MeitY). The official announcement offers up to ₹1 lakh for undergraduate and integrated dual degree students and up to ₹2 lakh for postgraduate students, with applications closing 30 November 2026 on the official fellowship portal.

Official artwork hosted by IndiaAI (indiaai.gov.in). Independent guide, not affiliated or sponsored. Artwork and trademarks belong to their owners.

This is an AiCoN explainer, not a government publication. Figures and dates below come from the official IndiaAI announcement and a Press Information Bureau release, checked 21 October 2026. Confirm final eligibility and dates on the official portal before applying. No application was submitted for this guide.

What the fellowship offers

IndiaAI's official announcement lists financial support of up to ₹1 lakh for undergraduate and integrated dual degree (IDD) students, and up to ₹2 lakh for postgraduate students. It also lists an additional ₹1 lakh of conference support for international presentations.

Under the IndiaAI Mission's FutureSkills pillar, the fellowship programme has been expanded to support 13,500 scholars: 8,000 undergraduates, 5,000 postgraduates and 500 PhD researchers. The Press Information Bureau says the fellowships are open to students from diverse disciplines including engineering, medicine, law, commerce, business and liberal arts, and that the expansion brings the IndiaAI Fellowship at par with PM Research Fellowships.

Who can apply

The official announcement states eligibility by semester, worded as follows:

  • 3-year UG: from the start of the 5th semester.
  • 4-year UG: from the start of the 7th semester.
  • 5-year integrated dual degree: from the start of the 9th semester.
  • PG: semesters 1, 2 and 3.

These are the announcement's own boundaries. The portal also carries an eligibility check, so verify your specific course and semester there before applying.

Deadline

The official announcement gives the application deadline as 30 November 2026. One national report cited 20 November 2026 instead; treat the date shown on the official fellowship portal as final, and apply early rather than on the last day.

How to apply: step by step

Checked on the live official pages on 21 October 2026. Applications run through the fellowship portal at fellowship.indiaai.gov.in, which IndiaAI says supports digital applications, progress tracking, mentorship and review.

  1. Open the official fellowship portal: fellowship.indiaai.gov.in (also linked from the IndiaAI FutureSkills page under "Guidelines and portal for IndiaAI Fellowship").
  2. Sign in to continue. The portal offers sign in with Gmail, LinkedIn or Email. The application form page is not public and opens only after sign in.
  3. After signing in, open the IndiaAI Mission fellowship form, fill in your details and upload the documents it asks for.
  4. Submit before 30 November 2026 and keep the confirmation for tracking.

Steps 3 and 4 happen inside a personal sign in. The form fields and document list were not tested beyond this point.

PhD applicants

The 13,500-scholar expansion includes 500 PhD researchers. The IndiaAI FutureSkills page separately links PhD fellowship guidelines and a proforma where institutes submit nominations, so PhD candidates should read that route with their institution rather than assume it matches the UG and PG portal flow.

Frequently asked questions

Is this only for engineering students?

No. The government release says students from diverse disciplines including engineering, medicine, law, commerce, business and liberal arts are covered.

What is the last date to apply?

30 November 2026 per the official announcement. Check the portal for the final word.

Can first-year PG students apply?

The announcement lists PG semesters 1, 2 and 3 as eligible.

Did AiCoN submit an application?

No. This is a researched guide, not a completed application or verified selection.
