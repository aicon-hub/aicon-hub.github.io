Envision: a free app that reads text and describes scenes

By AiCoN | 9 October 2026 | 5 min read

Envision is a phone app that reads text aloud and describes scenes with AI. Its support page says the app is completely free.

What it is

Envision is a smartphone app that uses the camera to read text aloud and describe what is around you. Its website says it reads text in over 60 languages, and that an AI assistant called Ask Envision lets you type or speak questions about a scanned text or a scene.

Is it free

Yes. Envision's support page says the iOS and Android app has been completely free since version 3.0, released on 25 July 2022. It also says you can choose to give a voluntary donation.

What it can do

  • Scan text, such as food labels, signs, bills, menus and books, and speak it out.
  • Describe a scene or objects with your phone camera.
  • Answer questions about text or an image through Ask Envision.

How to try it

  1. Install Envision from your phone's app store.
  2. Open Scan Text or Describe Scene.
  3. Point the camera, then use Ask Envision to ask a follow-up question.

Limits to know

  • AI descriptions can be wrong. Do not rely on them for medicines, safety signs or anything risky.
  • The Google Play listing resolves with India selected. We did not install the app or test Indian-language recognition. Check your device compatibility and try a non-sensitive sample first.
  • We did not test the app ourselves.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a subscription?

No, per Envision. The app has been free since version 3.0.

Is it only for blind or low-vision people?

Envision describes its purpose as accessibility, but anyone can use a reader that speaks text aloud.
