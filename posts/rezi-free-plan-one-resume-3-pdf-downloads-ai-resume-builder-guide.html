Rezi: free plan gives 1 resume and 3 PDF downloads, with AI checks

By AiCoN | Published 11 October 2026 | Sources checked 11 October 2026

Rezi is an AI resume builder. The free plan gives one resume, limited AI tools and three PDF downloads with no card, and Pro is $29 a month, about 2,810 rupees.

What it is

Rezi is an online resume builder. Its pricing page says it scores your resume on 23 key metrics for applicant tracking systems (ATS) and runs real-time content checks for things like filler words and weak or unquantified bullet points. Its AI features include an AI writer that rewrites bullet points and keyword targeting and analysis.

Is it free

Yes, with limits. Rezi's pricing page says "free to start, no card required". The Free plan is $0 a month: one resume, limited AI tools (such as Keyword Targeting and Analysis), up to three PDF downloads, and unlimited cover letters and resignation letters. The Pro plan is $29 a month (about ₹2,810 using an illustrative ₹96.8/USD conversion, not a verified Indian checkout price or current exchange-rate quote) with unlimited resumes, full AI tools, unlimited downloads and one free monthly expert resume review. The Lifetime plan is a one-time $149 (about ₹14,400). The Lifetime plan excludes the free monthly expert review. Rezi says Pro and Lifetime have a 100% refund on request within 30 days of purchase. Before paying, check the renewal schedule, cancellation route, final currency, taxes and card fees. We did not inspect checkout or test a refund.

Is it available in India

The privacy policy describes worldwide services, but that is not proof of India-specific signup, checkout or payment support. We did not create an account or test an Indian checkout.

How to try it

  1. Sign up for the free plan. No card is needed.
  2. Build one resume and use the free keyword and content checks.
  3. Download your PDF. You get three downloads, so check the resume before you export. The pricing page does not state a monthly reset for this Free-plan quota; do not assume it renews. Review every AI suggestion: do not invent skills, numbers, job titles or qualifications to match keywords. An ATS score is feedback, not a guarantee of passing screening or getting an interview.

Limits to know

  • The free plan allows one resume and three PDF downloads. Rezi does not say whether the free keyword tools cover every job description you try.
  • Rezi's privacy policy says it collects the content you put in your resume, including biographic information. You can ask for your data to be deleted by emailing support@rezi.io. The policy also describes closing an account from the dashboard with three confirmations and permanent deletion. Export your resume first; we did not test deletion or verify backup-retention timing.
  • AiCoN advice: a resume holds your phone number, address and work history. Share only what you are comfortable with, and read the privacy policy first.
  • Rezi's "62% of users land an interview" is its own marketing figure. We could not verify it, so do not rely on it.
  • The policy describes sharing with service vendors, employees/contractors and legal authorities, plus analytics and advertising cookies. It says account content is stored and may be hosted at a public URL; check sharing settings before publishing a resume. It separately says account-free resume creation does not store that resume on servers, but we did not verify that route. We found no explicit AI-training commitment on the checked legal page and make no no-training claim.
  • Pro and Lifetime are for personal, individual use. Commercial resume-writing or helping multiple clients requires Enterprise under the terms.
  • We did not test it ourselves.
