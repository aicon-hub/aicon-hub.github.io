Top Class Education Scholarship for SC students: full fees plus Rs 86,000, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | 24 October 2026 | 5 min read

The Central Sector Scholarship of Top Class Education for SC Students pays full tuition and non-refundable charges plus an academic allowance of Rs 86,000 in the first year and Rs 41,000 in later years. It is for SC first-year students at notified top institutions such as IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMSs and NLUs, with family income up to Rs 8 lakh. Applications close on the National Scholarship Portal on 31 October 2026.

What is it?

A central scholarship of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment for Scheduled Caste students studying beyond Class 12 in notified "top class" institutions. The notified list covers IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMSs, IIITs, NIFTs, NIDs, IHMs, National Law Universities and other central institutions, plus NAAC A++/A+ accredited institutions, NIRF top-100 institutions and Institutions of National Importance. The scholarship continues until you finish the course, subject to satisfactory performance.

Who can get it?

From the official scheme guidelines (applicable from 2025-26):

  • You belong to a Scheduled Caste and your family income from all sources is up to Rs 8 lakh a year. Self-employed parents need an income certificate from a revenue officer of at least Tehsildar rank; employed parents need one from their employer.
  • You are a first-year student in a full-time prescribed course at a notified institution, admitted through its general selection criteria. Second, third and fourth year students cannot get a fresh award.
  • Selection is on inter-se merit within your institution's allotted slots. Thirty percent of slots are reserved for eligible SC girl students.
  • No more than two siblings in a family can hold this scholarship; you give an affidavit at the institution.
  • The scholarship stops if you are not promoted (it resumes on promotion) and ends if you change institution.

How much does it pay?

  • Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges. For private-sector institutions there is a ceiling of Rs 2.00 lakh a year per student.
  • Academic allowance: Rs 86,000 in the first year and Rs 41,000 in every subsequent year, for living expenses, books and stationery, and a computer with accessories.
  • Both parts come by Direct Benefit Transfer to your bank account. You must deposit the fee with the institution within a month of receiving it.

Deadline: 31 October 2026

Checked on the live National Scholarship Portal on 24 October 2026. The scheme entry under All Scholarships shows the window open from 1 June 2026 with student applications closing 31 October 2026, defective-application and institute verification until 15 November 2026, and DNO/SNO/MNO verification until 30 November 2026. The guidelines also set 31 October as the standard annual application date. Do not confuse the student submission date with the later verification dates. No personal account was accessed.

How to apply on NSP: step by step

Checked on the live National Scholarship Portal on 24 October 2026.

  1. Open scholarships.gov.in (Academic Year 2026-27 is live). New users complete One Time Registration from the Students section: register your mobile number, then finish eKYC with the NSP OTR app and Aadhaar face authentication. Registration is free.
  2. Log in with your OTR ID and open a Fresh Application for Academic Year 2026-27. (Second-year and later scholars renewing use Renewal.)
  3. Choose the Central Sector Scholarship of Top Class Education for SC Students.
  4. Fill the form and upload the documents the guidelines list: income certificate, caste certificate, bank account details, admission rank and fee details.
  5. Submit before 31 October 2026. Your institution then verifies your application before it goes to the Ministry; track status in the same dashboard.

Steps inside your own NSP login are not shown as screenshots. Generating an OTR alone is not an application - you must still log in and submit the scholarship application.

Frequently asked questions

Which institutions count as top class?

Institutions notified by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment: IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMSs, IIITs, NIFTs, NIDs, IHMs, National Law Universities and other central institutions, plus NAAC A++/A+ institutions, NIRF top-100 institutions and Institutions of National Importance.

How much is the scholarship?

Full tuition and non-refundable charges (ceiling Rs 2 lakh a year for private institutions) plus an academic allowance of Rs 86,000 in the first year and Rs 41,000 in each later year.

I am in the second year. Can I apply fresh?

No. Fresh awards are for first-year students only; later years are renewals for existing scholars.

What if I am not promoted?

The scholarship is terminated while you are not promoted, and released again once you are promoted to the next class.
