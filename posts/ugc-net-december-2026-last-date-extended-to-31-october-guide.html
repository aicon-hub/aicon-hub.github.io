UGC-NET December 2026: last date extended to 31 October, no late fee

By AiCoN | Published 28 October 2026 | Sources checked 28 October 2026

The National Testing Agency has extended the last date to submit the UGC-NET December 2026 online application form. A public notice dated 27 October 2026 says candidates can now apply up to 31 October 2026, 11:50 PM, without any late fee, and pay the examination fee by the same time. Earlier, the form was to close on 28 October with a ₹500 late-fee window from 29 to 31 October. That late-fee window is replaced: applying by 31 October 11:50 PM now costs only the normal fee.

Quick facts

  • New last date: per the NTA public notice of 27 October 2026, the online application form for UGC-NET December 2026 can be submitted up to 31 October 2026, 11:50 PM. The last date for successful transaction of the examination fee is the same: 31 October 2026, 11:50 PM.
  • No late fee: the notice says the extension is without any late fee. The Information Bulletin had set a ₹500 late fee for submissions between 29 and 31 October; the extension notice removes that charge for applications completed by the new deadline.
  • Normal fee unchanged: ₹1,150 for General and unreserved candidates; ₹600 for General-EWS and OBC-NCL; ₹325 for SC, ST, Third Gender and PwD/PwBD candidates. Payment is online only, and bank or payment-gateway charges and GST are extra.
  • What else stays the same: the notice says all other terms in the Information Bulletin and earlier public notices remain unchanged. Per the bulletin updated on 16 October 2026, that means the correction window of 2 to 4 November 2026, exam city intimation on 23 November 2026, admit cards from 7 December 2026 and the computer-based exam on 14 to 18 December and 21 December 2026 across 87 subjects.
  • What the exam decides: eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor and admission to PhD programmes. It is also the application route for the NFSC, NFOBC and Maulana Azad national fellowships.
  • Scope: this extension is for UGC-NET December 2026 only. CSIR-UGC-NET December 2026 runs on its own schedule and is not covered by this notice.
  • NTA's advice: complete the application well before the revised deadline to avoid last-minute technical difficulties, and check nta.ac.in and ugcnet.nta.nic.in for updates.

How to apply before the new deadline

  1. Open ugcnet.nta.nic.in and use the UGC-NET Dec 2026 application form link under Candidate Activity.
  2. Register, fill the form and choose your subject and exam cities carefully - some choices cannot be changed later.
  3. Upload your photo and signature exactly as the Information Bulletin specifies.
  4. Pay the fee online before 11:50 PM on 31 October 2026.
  5. Download and keep the confirmation page after successful payment - an unpaid or half-finished form is not a submitted application.
