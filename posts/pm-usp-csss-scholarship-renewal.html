PM-USP CSSS scholarship: who gets Rs 12,000 a year and how to renew

By AiCoN | 6 October 2026 | 6 min read

PM-USP CSSS is a central scholarship for college and university students with high Class 12 marks and limited family income. Renewal for 2026-27 is on NSP. Both current public NSP pages now show renewal applications until 31 October 2026. Read the submission and verification dates separately.

What is it?

The PM-USP Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (CSSS) is a merit-cum-means scholarship of the Department of Higher Education. You apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP), scholarships.gov.in.

Who can get it? (fresh applicants)

From the official guidelines and FAQ:

  • You are above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in your stream from your Class 12 board.
  • You study a regular degree course. Correspondence or distance mode is not allowed.
  • Your college is recognised by AICTE or the regulator for your course.
  • Family income is up to Rs 4.5 lakh a year. An income certificate is needed only for fresh applicants.
  • The CSSS guidelines and FAQ say no other scholarship, fee waiver or reimbursement. NSP separately announces merit plus welfare combinations subject to scheme eligibility; do not assume that portal-wide statement overrides CSSS-specific restrictions.

How much?

From the guidelines: Rs 12,000 a year at graduation level for the first three years, and Rs 20,000 a year at post-graduation level. For technical courses such as B.Tech, the scholarship is up to graduation level only: Rs 12,000 a year for the first three years, and Rs 20,000 in the fourth year.

Renewal rules

To renew in each year of study you need at least 50 percent marks in the annual exam. The official guidelines and FAQ set attendance of at least 75 percent.

Renewal deadline: 31 October 2026

Checked on 7 October 2026: both the NSP homepage and exact PM-USP CSSS renewal entry on All Scholarships show student applications until 31 October 2026, institute/defective verification until 15 November and L2 verification until 30 November. The earlier 30 September versus 31 October discrepancy is no longer present in these public sources. Do not confuse submission with later verification dates. No personal account was accessed.

How to apply on NSP: step by step

Checked on the live National Scholarship Portal on 19 October 2026. NSP now uses One Time Registration (OTR) - do this first if you have never registered.

  1. Open scholarships.gov.in (Academic Year 2026-27 is live), click Students → Apply now, then Register. Registration is free - no fee is required.
  2. Read the OTR guidelines, tick the consent boxes and click Next. Register your active mobile number and verify it with the OTP; a reference number is sent to your mobile.
  3. Complete eKYC: install the NSP OTR app and the Aadhaar Face RD app on an Android phone and do face authentication with the reference number. Your OTR ID is then generated. Aadhaar is required; a minor without Aadhaar can register with a parent's or legal guardian's Aadhaar.
  4. Log in on NSP with your OTR ID, open the Renewal application for Academic Year 2026-27 and choose the PM-USP Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSSS). (Fresh applicants choose Fresh Application instead.)
  5. Fill the application form and upload the documents it lists.
  6. Submit before 31 October 2026. Track the application in the same dashboard; institute and district/state verification happen after you submit.

Steps 4-6 happen inside your own NSP login, so they are not shown as screenshots. Generating an OTR alone is not an application - you must still log in and submit the scholarship application.

Frequently asked questions

What is the income limit?

Rs 4.5 lakh a year, from the guidelines.

How much is the scholarship?

Rs 12,000 a year at graduation level and Rs 20,000 at post-graduation level.

What is the renewal deadline?

Both checked current public NSP sources show 31 October 2026 for student renewal submission. Later dates are verification deadlines.

Can I get it with another scholarship?

The checked CSSS guidelines prohibit another scholarship or fee reimbursement. NSP also has a broader merit/welfare announcement subject to scheme eligibility. Resolve any proposed combination with the scheme authority before relying on it.

I study B.Tech. What do I get?

Rs 12,000 a year for the first three years and Rs 20,000 in the fourth year.
