Julius: ask questions about your spreadsheet, with a free daily allowance

By AiCoN | Published 10 October 2026 | Sources checked 10 October 2026

Julius is a website where you upload a spreadsheet and ask questions about it in plain English. It has a Free plan with 25 credits a day, and Plus is approximately ₹1,940 a month (US$20), with Indian checkout pricing unverified.

What it is

Julius is a website where you upload a spreadsheet or data file, such as Excel or CSV, and ask questions in plain English. Julius says it can analyze data, run analyses, draw charts and give answers with citations. Its pricing page says it can also create reports, presentations and websites.

Is it free

Yes, with a daily limit. Julius lists a Free plan with daily refresh credits. Its credits help page says the Free plan gets 25 welcome credits one time, plus 25 credits each day. A simple message costs fewer credits than a full analysis, so a big task can use up a day's credits quickly. Paid Plus is approximately ₹1,940 a month (US$20), converted at ₹96.79 per US dollar. This is not a verified Indian checkout price; taxes and card conversion fees may differ.

How to try it

  1. Go to julius.ai and sign up free.
  2. Upload a small spreadsheet or CSV file.
  3. Ask a question, for example "Which month had the highest sales?"
  4. Read the answer and the chart. Check a few numbers by hand.

Check your data first

Use a copy of the file. Remove names, account numbers and other private fields, label each column and keep units clear. Ask Julius which rows it used and how it handled blanks. Compare totals against your spreadsheet before trusting a chart.

Limits to know

  • The free credits are small and the welcome credits do not renew. The current credits table lists 25 daily credits; a later FAQ paragraph on the same page mentions only the one-time 25 credits. The pricing page also confirms a daily allowance, but check the actual balance/reset in your account before planning a task.
  • Julius changed from a message limit to a credit system in 2026. An older Julius page still mentions 5 messages a month, so check the current limit in your account.
  • Julius says it does not train models on your data. Still, avoid uploading private records such as Aadhaar, bank or health data.
  • AI can get calculations wrong. Check important numbers yourself.
  • We found no official page on availability in India, and no restriction either.
  • We did not test it ourselves.
