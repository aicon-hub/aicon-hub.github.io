Penpot: an open source design tool with a $0 cloud plan

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

Penpot is an open source tool for designing screens, posters and prototypes with a team. Students can use it for UI projects and portfolios, and teachers for diagrams and visual material. Its pricing page lists a $0 cloud plan.

Quick facts

  • What it is: an open source design and prototyping platform, with a cloud version that Penpot hosts and a self-host option.
  • Price: the pricing page lists the cloud Professional plan at $0 per user per month, described as "Free and fully featured. No limits, no missing tools." All prices on the page are in US dollars.
  • Open source: the page says there is no vendor lock-in and that you own your designs and code.
  • Education: the page says non-profit, education and open-source discounts are available, with an application by email. The page we read does not say what the education discount is.
  • What we could not confirm: the pricing table lists storage figures and version history periods, but our text copy of the page did not show which figure belongs to which plan. Check the table on penpot.app/pricing for the storage limit.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the Penpot pricing page on 9 November 2026. We did not create an account or a design.

Who is it for?

Students and teachers who need a free tool to draw screens, diagrams or posters, and who want to work together in the browser. The Penpot FAQ also covers moving from Figma or Adobe XD.

How to start

  1. Open penpot.app and choose the cloud version.
  2. Read the pricing page first to see which plan is free.
  3. Start a file and invite your team.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not use Penpot, so we cannot say how it compares with other design tools.
  • Storage and version-history limits exist. Check them before you move a large project.
  • Penpot also sells paid plans for organisations. A free plan does not include those features, such as single sign-on.
  • Penpot is a design tool and does not teach design. Use the official guides or tutorials.

Sources: Penpot pricing. Independent guide by AiCoN - not affiliated with Penpot. Checked 9 November 2026. Details can change; check the official source before you act.
