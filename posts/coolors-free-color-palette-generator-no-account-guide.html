Coolors: free color palette generator, no account needed

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

Coolors is a palette generator from an independent developer in Italy: press the spacebar and it deals a five-color palette, free in the browser without an account. Here is what its pages say about free limits, Pro pricing, ads and data, and what our scripted test did.

Quick facts

  • What it does: builds five-color palettes in the browser. Press the spacebar for a new palette, lock the colors you like, adjust the rest, then copy or export. The site also lists an image color picker, gradient tools, a contrast checker and a palette visualizer.
  • Price: the Free plan costs $0 and shows ads. Pro is $3 per month billed yearly ($36, about ₹3,400) or $5 monthly (about ₹470), and a one-time "Forever" option showed $99 (about ₹9,350), marked down from $149. Prices read from the live pricing page on 21 October 2026; INR at about 94.5 per US dollar (21 October 2026 close).
  • Account: not needed to generate palettes - our scripted test used it signed out. Saving palettes needs a free sign-in; the free plan saves up to 10 palettes.
  • Made by: an independent developer named Fabrizio, "creator and maintainer of Coolors" per the pricing page. The owner and data controller is Coolors s.r.l. of Colleferro, Rome, Italy, per the privacy policy.
  • Platforms: the website works in any modern browser. Its download section lists an iOS app, a Chrome extension, a Figma plugin and an Adobe extension; no Android app is listed.
  • Best for: anyone picking colors for a poster, invite, website, slide deck, room or logo in seconds.

What it is

Coolors calls itself "The super fast color palettes generator" and says it is trusted by more than 8 million users. The generator shows five colors as full-height columns with their hex codes. Each spacebar press deals a fresh palette; you can lock a column to keep that color while the rest change. Finished palettes can be copied as hex codes or exported, and a separate Explore section surfaces community-made palettes - the homepage advertises "Explore 10M+ Palettes", while the pricing page lists "10K+ of ready color schemes" on the free plan and "10M+" on Pro.

What we tested

On 21 October 2026 we ran a scripted functional test in a desktop browser, signed out. Opening coolors.co/generate redirected to a palette URL and showed five hex codes (C0BDA5, CC978E, F39C6B, FF3864, 261447). We pressed the spacebar twice; each press replaced all five colors with new valid hex codes and updated the address bar with the new palette (first F4DBD8-BEA8A7-C09891-775144-2A0800, then 8C271E-ABA194-CFCBCA-D8DDDE-D9F7FA). In the same browser we opened the pricing page to read the rendered plan prices. This was a scripted functional test of one workflow, not a full product review; we did not create an account and did not test Pro features, the mobile app or the extensions.

Is it free?

Yes, with ads and limits, per the pricing page. The free plan includes palette generation up to 5 colors, 10K+ ready color schemes, up to 10 saved palettes, 1 project and 1 collection, up to 5 saved colors, picking colors from an image, limited collages and a basic public profile page.

Pro removes ads and raises the limits: palettes up to 10 colors, 10M+ ready schemes, unlimited saved palettes, projects, collections and colors, collages, a Pro profile, palette contrast checks and variations, saving images, gradients and fonts, color libraries, advanced export options, a private profile, custom logos on exported PDFs, all Palette Visualizer layouts, dark mode and Coolors AI with 3,000 credits a month. The rendered pricing page showed Pro at $3 per month billed yearly ($36) or $5 per month billed monthly, plus a one-time "Forever" payment of $99, marked down from $149.

Privacy and data

  • The privacy policy names Coolors s.r.l. (Colleferro, Rome, Italy) as owner and data controller.
  • Data types it lists include email address, first and last name, username, password and picture for registered users, plus trackers, usage data, device information, universally unique identifiers and advertising identifiers such as Google Advertiser ID or IDFA.
  • Cookies and other trackers are used to provide the service and for the further purposes described in its cookie policy.
  • The policy has dedicated sections for users in the United States and Brazil.
  • The free tier is ad-supported; the site asks ad-blocker users to allow ads or subscribe from $3 a month. Expect ad and analytics tracking on the free plan.

India

  • No region restriction appears on the pages we read, and the generator loaded normally in our test. We did not test from India itself.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars. At about 94.5 INR per dollar (21 October 2026 close): $5 is about ₹470, $36 about ₹3,400 and $99 about ₹9,350.

Good to know

  • Free palettes are capped at 5 colors; Pro raises that to 10.
  • Contrast checking on palettes and the Palette Visualizer's full layouts are listed as Pro features.
  • The AI features (Color Bot, Coolors AI credits) sit behind Pro.
  • No Android app is listed on the site; only iOS, Chrome, Figma and Adobe.
  • The company runs on an indie budget and says Pro subscriptions support the developer, with part going to nonprofits.

Frequently asked questions

Is Coolors free?

Yes. Generating palettes in the browser is free with ads. Pro removes ads and lifts the limits: $3 a month billed yearly ($36) or $5 billed monthly, or a $99 one-time Forever option, per the pricing page checked on 21 October 2026.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Our scripted test generated palettes without signing in. You need a free sign-in to save palettes (up to 10 on the free plan).

Is there an Android app?

None is listed on the site. Its download section lists an iOS app, a Chrome extension, a Figma plugin and an Adobe extension.

Who makes Coolors?

An independent developer named Fabrizio, described on the pricing page as the creator and maintainer. The owning company is Coolors s.r.l. of Italy, per the privacy policy.
