Wordle: free daily word game, six tries

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

Wordle gives you one new five-letter word puzzle every day: six guesses, colored tiles, done in a few minutes. It is free and needs no account. We tested it ourselves. Here is what its pages say and what we found.

Quick facts

  • What it does: one new 5-letter word puzzle every day. You get six guesses; tiles turn green (right letter, right spot), yellow (right letter, wrong spot) or grey.
  • Price: free. A short video ad plays before the game; a New York Times Games subscription removes the pre-game ads and unlocks an archive of over 10,000 puzzles, per the game's own page.
  • Platforms: any web browser, on phone or computer.
  • Best for: a two-minute daily brain break, and comparing scores with friends.

What it is

Wordle is the daily word game from The New York Times. Each puzzle is numbered and edited by Tracy Bennett, per the game page. The rules, from its own How to Play: "Guess the Wordle in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word." A new puzzle is released daily at midnight.

We tried it

This was a functional workflow test with scripted input: it checks that the game works start to finish, not whether our guesses were any good.

  • Opened Wordle on nytimes.com on 21 October 2026 without an account and pressed Play.
  • A short video ad played first; a "Continue to Wordle" option followed.
  • We typed a valid 5-letter guess and the tiles flipped, showing one letter correct and another present. The on-screen keyboard updated with the result.
  • No login was asked for at any point; the page offers a free NYT account only to save your stats.

Is it free?

Yes, the daily puzzle is free. The trade-off is the pre-game video ad we saw. The paid Games subscription removes those ads and opens the puzzle archive, per the page. You never need to pay to play today's puzzle.

Privacy and data

  • Playing without an account still runs on nytimes.com, so The New York Times privacy policy applies, including advertising cookies (we saw a video ad before play).
  • A free NYT account is optional and only saves your stats and streak across devices, per the game page.

Our advice

  • Start with a word full of common letters, like SLATE or CRANE, to rule letters out fast. This is our advice, not the Times'.
  • One puzzle a day is the whole point; the paid archive is for people who want more.

India

  • It is a website and works anywhere. Its page says a new puzzle is released daily at midnight, but does not say in which time zone, so we cannot give an India reset time.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wordle free?

Yes. Today's puzzle is free with a short ad before play. A subscription only removes ads and adds the archive.

Do I need an account?

No. We played without one. A free NYT account only saves your stats.

When does the new puzzle come?

Daily at midnight, per its page. The pages we read do not say which time zone.
