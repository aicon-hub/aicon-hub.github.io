GitHub Student Developer Pack: free Copilot, Pro account, domain and credits for students

By AiCoN | Published 19 October 2026 | Sources checked 19 October 2026

The GitHub Student Developer Pack gives verified students free access to professional developer tools that normally cost money: GitHub Copilot Student, a free GitHub Pro account, a free .me domain for a year, Heroku credits of $13 a month for 24 months, Microsoft Azure with no credit card, MongoDB Atlas credits, three months of DataCamp and more. If you are 13 or older and enrolled in a degree or diploma course, you can apply free.

@@COVER@@
Good to knowYou need a school-issued email address and/or official, dated proof of enrollment (GitHub lists both on its students page). Approval is not instant - GitHub reviews your proof, which can take days. Offers come from GitHub and its partners, and each partner's terms apply; the list changes, so check the pack page for the current offers before you count on one.

Who can apply

  • Students aged 13 or older
  • Enrolled in a degree or diploma-granting course of study
  • You need a school-issued email address and/or official, dated proof of enrollment
  • A free GitHub account

What you get (checked 19 October 2026)

  • GitHub Copilot Student: free for verified students - unlimited code completions plus an allowance of GitHub AI Credits, with limited chat and agent usage
  • Free GitHub Pro account for verified learners
  • Name.com: 1 year free .me domain registration + 1 SSL certificate free for 1 year
  • Heroku: $13/month credit for 24 months
  • Microsoft Azure: cloud services and learning resources with no credit card required (separate 13-17 age offer too)
  • MongoDB Atlas credits plus MongoDB University access, including a free certification valued at $150
  • DataCamp: 3 months free with your GitHub student account
  • Dozens more partner offers on the pack page - the list changes over time

How to apply: step by step

  1. Create a free GitHub account (or sign in to your existing one).
  2. Add your school-issued email to your GitHub account if you have one - it speeds up verification. Otherwise keep an official, dated proof of enrollment ready (student ID, enrollment letter or fee receipt with a date).
  1. Open education.github.com/pack and tap "Sign up for Student Developer Pack".
  1. Fill the application with your school details and upload proof if asked. GitHub reviews applications - approval can take a few days, and you may be asked to re-verify yearly.
  2. Once approved, open the pack page again and activate each offer (Copilot Student, domain, Heroku, Azure and others) from its card.
