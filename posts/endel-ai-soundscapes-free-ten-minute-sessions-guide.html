Endel: AI soundscapes in short free sessions

By AiCoN | 7 October 2026 | 5 min read

Endel creates adaptive soundscapes for focus, relaxation, sleep and movement. Its official help page offers selected free soundscapes in sessions of up to ten minutes. This is background audio, not a medical treatment or a guaranteed productivity boost.

What makes Endel an AI tool?

Endel's technology page describes an AI-powered node system that maps permitted inputs onto soundscape logic. Sound elements are designed by its sound team; the engine adapts the result as inputs change. This is not a text-to-song generator or a promise of entirely new music from a prompt.

Depending on your device and permissions, inputs can include time of day, weather, bedtime, movement and heart rate. The company says sound generation happens in real time, on-device. That technical description does not establish that every account, analytics or support function avoids cloud processing.

What is free?

Free soundscapesThe official help page lists Focus, Sleep, Relax and Move.
Session limitEach free session lasts up to ten minutes. The same page says you may start as many sessions as you like throughout the day.
PremiumLonger uninterrupted sessions and access to all soundscapes, scenarios and additional features require Premium.
TrialThe help page describes a seven-day yearly-plan trial, while general terms say trial duration can vary by offer. Read the actual offer and renewal date on your screen; a trial is not the same as the ongoing free tier.
IndiaApple's India listing and India lookup resolved the iPhone app as a free download with in-app purchases. Installation, Indian Android eligibility and individual feature access were not tested.
Paid INR priceNo single current tax-inclusive India subscription price was verified. Check your store/account's final price and billing period. No currency estimate is given here.

Names differ across official pages: the help page calls the movement soundscape Move, while the homepage uses Activity in its introductory player. Follow the options actually shown in your app. The exact free scope of each non-mobile platform was not established.

How to try a short session

  1. Open the official Endel website and follow its app link, or use the verified India iPhone listing.
  2. Check whether onboarding is showing an optional subscription or trial. Do not accept a paid offer just to assume the ten-minute free tier exists.
  3. Find an available free Focus, Sleep, Relax or Move soundscape and inspect any limit shown.
  4. Start a short session at a comfortable volume. Use it as background sound while doing a simple task or resting.
  5. Review permission prompts separately. Heart-rate, location and movement inputs depend on platform and consent; the guide does not require granting all of them.
  6. Compare whether you personally find it useful. Do not infer a health benefit from the fact that audio adapts.
  7. If upgrading, check total cost, renewal date and the correct cancellation route for the platform used to buy.

These are editorial steps based on official product descriptions, not a tested onboarding walkthrough. No account, listening session or subscription was started for this guide.

What this guide does not promise

Endel markets neuroscience-backed benefits and numerical focus/stress results. We did not review those studies here and do not repeat the performance numbers as independent evidence. No benefit, sleep improvement or medical outcome is guaranteed by this guide.

Background sound is not a diagnosis or treatment for insomnia, anxiety or attention difficulties. Seek qualified advice for persistent health concerns. Keep audio at a comfortable level and do not let headphones or sleep-oriented audio reduce awareness when driving or crossing roads.

Privacy and permission choices

Adaptive sound can use personal context. Check permissions before enabling location, health or activity integrations, and review the official privacy policy. On-device sound generation is a narrow technology claim, not a privacy certification of the whole app.

Features differ across mobile, web, watch and speaker versions. Do not assume a free mobile session or a sensor integration has identical availability everywhere. This guide covers the public app descriptions and verified India iPhone listing, not hardware purchase advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Endel entirely free?

No. Selected soundscapes have short free sessions; Premium provides longer access and additional features.

Is the ten-minute limit a daily allowance?

The official help says each session is up to ten minutes and allows repeated sessions throughout the day. It is not described as only ten minutes total per day.

Does it write songs?

The verified technology description is adaptive soundscape generation from designed elements and context, not prompt-based songwriting.

Can it cure insomnia?

No such claim is made here. This is optional background audio, not medical care.

No listening or hands-on app test is claimed. Features and limits were checked against official sources.
