Margadeepam Scholarship 2026-27: Rs 1,500 for Kerala minority students in class 1-8, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 18 October 2026 | Sources checked 18 October 2026

Scoped recheck 24 October 2026: actual public banner confirms 31 October at 5 PM. Corrected student self-registration to the school form/clerk/head route in current English guidelines. Added two-child limit, other-scholarship exclusion, Class 1 selection and document/account wording differences. Older PDF dates distinguished from extension; revised school-forwarding deadline not verified. No login or application tested. Emblem-bearing logo and old homepage screenshot removed from display; text-only.

Kerala's Minority Welfare Department has extended the Margadeepam Scholarship 2026-27 deadline to 31 October 2026, 5 PM. The scheme pays Rs 1,500 a year to minority community students studying in Class 1 to 8 in government or aided schools in Kerala, if the family income is up to Rs 2.5 lakh.

Good to knowThe current official guidelines use a school-led route: download and fill the application, give it to the school head, then the school clerk enters it and the head verifies and forwards it online. The public Register / Login menu showed Official Login only, not student or parent mobile registration. Contact your school early. Previous recipients can apply afresh for this academic year.

Who can apply

  • Minority community students (Muslim, Christian - all denominations, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi) who are Kerala residents studying in Kerala
  • Class 1 to Class 8, in government or aided schools
  • Annual family income up to Rs 2.5 lakh
  • 30% of scholarships are reserved for girls; if enough girls do not apply, boys are considered

At most two children from the same family can receive this scholarship; the school checks ration-card details and rejects a third application. Students receiving another scholarship are not eligible under these guidelines.

What you get

  • Rs 1,500 per year, given as a one-time payment

How selection works

  • Selection is by a point system out of 30: family income (up to 10 points), previous year's academic marks (up to 12), and extra points for orphans and children with disabilities (40% or more)
  • For Class 1, academic marks are not required; eligibility is based on income. Class 2 to 8 uses the previous academic year's grades. Disability and loss-of-parent categories carry separate points.
  • Not first-come, first-served

How to apply through your school

Public portal and official English/Malayalam guidelines compared on 24 October 2026. No school login or application submission tested.

  1. Open the official Margadeepam portal and download the 2026-27 application form. Ask your school head for help if needed.
  2. Fill accurate student, family, class, school and bank details and give the completed form to your school head with the required supporting records.
  3. The school clerk enters the details in Clerk Login; the school head checks, approves and forwards them through the portal. Students should get an opportunity to check the entered details. This is not a student self-registration route we verified.
  4. The current public announcement says documents need not be uploaded with applications: the school head keeps them safely at school. Documents include income and minority/community certificates, bank passbook, previous-year grade sheet and disability/death certificates where applicable. The English PDF still describes PDF files up to 200 KB; follow the current no-upload announcement and ask your school what copies it needs.
  5. The live public banner shows the extended application-submission deadline as 31 October 2026, 5:00 PM. The older PDF timetable still lists 30 September for school applications and 15 October for forwarding. Those are not treated as the new timetable. We did not verify a separate revised school-forwarding deadline, so confirm school processing time early.
  6. A public Track Details link exists. Ask the school for the application reference and use the official tracking route. No tracking record was tested.

The bank-passbook document list allows a student/joint account with a parent or guardian, while the selected-student payment section says an account in the student's own name. Confirm the account requirement with the school before changing an account or submitting records. Aadhaar, bank and family records should go only to authorised school staff, not unknown callers or messages.
