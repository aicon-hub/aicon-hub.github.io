Margadeepam Scholarship 2026-27: Rs 1,500 for Kerala minority students in class 1-8, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 18 October 2026 | Sources checked 18 October 2026

Kerala's Minority Welfare Department has extended the Margadeepam Scholarship 2026-27 deadline to 31 October 2026, 5 PM. The scheme pays Rs 1,500 a year to minority community students studying in Class 1 to 8 in government or aided schools in Kerala, if the family income is up to Rs 2.5 lakh.

Good to knowStudents apply online on the Margadeepam portal, but the application goes through the school: the head of the institution verifies entries and keeps the documents. So tell your school's headmaster or headmistress early - do not wait for the last day. If you got the scholarship in an earlier year, you must apply again for the new academic year.

Who can apply

  • Minority community students (Muslim, Christian - all denominations, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi) who are Kerala residents studying in Kerala
  • Class 1 to Class 8, in government or aided schools
  • Annual family income up to Rs 2.5 lakh
  • 30% of scholarships are reserved for girls; if enough girls do not apply, boys are considered

What you get

  • Rs 1,500 per year, given as a one-time payment

How selection works

  • Selection is by a point system out of 30: family income (up to 10 points), previous year's academic marks (up to 12), and extra points for orphans and children with disabilities (40% or more)
  • Not first-come, first-served

How to apply: step by step

Checked on the live Margadeepam portal on 19 October 2026.

  1. Open margadeepam.kerala.gov.in (Directorate of Minority Welfare, Government of Kerala) and click Register / Login. New users register with their mobile number; a parent or guardian can register for the student.
  2. Fill the online application form with the student's details, class, school and bank account details. Read the 2026-27 notification (Malayalam, linked on the portal) first.
  3. Note the current announcement: the mandatory documents (income, community/minority certificates, bank passbook copy, etc.) are not uploaded with the application - the head of the institution keeps them at the school.
  4. Submit before 31 October 2026, 5:00 PM (the extended deadline shown on the portal).
  5. The school head downloads the form, verifies the details online and keeps the documents at school.
  6. Track the application any time under Track Details on the portal.

The form and tracking steps happen inside your own login, so they are not shown as screenshots.
