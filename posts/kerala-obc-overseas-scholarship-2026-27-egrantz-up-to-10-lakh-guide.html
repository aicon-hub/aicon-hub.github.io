Kerala OBC Overseas Scholarship 2026-27: up to ₹10 lakh for PG or PhD abroad, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 26 October 2026 | Sources checked 26 October 2026

The Kerala Backward Classes Development Department's Overseas Scholarship 2026-27 is open on the e-grantz portal. It funds students from OBC communities on the Kerala state list, other than minority communities who have a separate scheme of their own, for a postgraduate or PhD course at a Times Higher Education top-300 ranked university abroad, with up to ₹10 lakh for the first 20 selected students and up to ₹5 lakh for the next 30. The original last date was 15 October 2026; the department has extended it to 31 October 2026 on the portal's notice board. You apply online through e-grantz, then submit the printout and annexures to the department as required. You need an offer or admission letter from the university before you apply.

At a glance

  • What: Overseas Scholarship 2026-27 of the Kerala Backward Classes Development Department, on the e-grantz 3.0 portal
  • Who: students from non-minority OBC communities on the Kerala state list, going abroad for a PG or PhD course
  • Amount: up to ₹10 lakh each for 20 students, up to ₹5 lakh each for the next 30, towards study expenses and initial living costs
  • Courses: PG or PhD in Medical and allied, Engineering, Pure Science, Agricultural Science, Social Science, Law, Management, or Computer Science including AI; no undergraduate courses
  • Universities: only those in the Times Higher Education world ranking top 300; the list is Annexure 3 of the notification PDF on the portal
  • Last date: 31 October 2026 (extended from 15 October 2026), apply online at egrantz.kerala.gov.in, then submit the printout and annexures to the department as required
  • Contact: 0471-2727379

Who can apply

From the official notification (BCDIR/416/2026-A2, dated 7 September 2026), read on 26 October 2026:

  • You must belong to a non-minority OBC community on the Kerala state list (the community list is Annexure 1 of the notification). Minority community students should not apply to this scheme; as the notification notes, the Minority Welfare Department runs its own overseas scholarship.
  • You must have at least 60% marks in your degree (for PhD applicants, 60% in your PG), and the higher study should be in a subject related to your degree.
  • Your age must be below 40 as on 15 October 2026.
  • Your family's annual income must not exceed ₹6 lakh.
  • You must have an offer letter or admission letter from a THE top-300 university, and it must be uploaded on e-grantz with the application.
  • Only PG and PhD courses qualify. The course must be at most three years long, except for a PhD.
  • If you, or a sibling registered under the same family registration card, have received this scholarship in an earlier year, you should not apply again.
  • Minority community students are not covered by this scheme; the Minority Welfare Department runs its own overseas scholarship.

How the money works

  • Among eligible applicants, the 20 with the lowest family annual income get up to ₹10 lakh each; the next 30 get up to ₹5 lakh each. The amount covers study expenses and initial living expenses.
  • OEC students get reservation within both slabs: 20% of the ₹10 lakh seats (minimum 5 students) and 25% of the ₹5 lakh seats (minimum 7 students).
  • The scholarship is released in installments against actual expense bills, and the final installment comes after you complete the course. An advance of 25% of the course fee, up to ₹2.5 lakh, can be sanctioned ahead.
  • Selected students sign an agreement to follow the scheme's conditions. If you drop the course midway, the full scholarship amount has to be repaid with 18% interest.
  • You cannot change the course or the university after selection, and you must join the university within a year of the selection order, or the scholarship is cancelled.

How to apply

Checked on the e-grantz portal and the official notification on 26 October 2026:

  1. Open egrantz.kerala.gov.in. Check the address bar: the official portal is on the kerala.gov.in domain.
  2. Read the Overseas Scholarship 2026-27 notification linked on the notice board, and confirm your university is in the top-300 World University Ranking 2026 list in Annexure 3 of the notification PDF.
  3. Register on the portal if you do not have an e-grantz login (One Time Registration is on the home page).
  4. Fill the online application and upload your offer letter or admission letter along with the required annexures, on or before 31 October 2026.
  5. Print the submitted application and keep the annexures (documents listed in the notification) ready. The notification requires the printout and annexures to be submitted to the Backward Classes Development Department subsequently. It does not state a separate last date or a postal address for the hard copy; the department's contact for details is 0471-2727379.

Keep these limits in mind

  • The THE top-300 condition narrows this sharply: admission to any foreign university is not enough, it has to be a listed top-300 one.
  • The amounts are "up to" figures paid against actual bills in installments, not a flat grant paid in one go.
  • Minority community students should look at the Minority Welfare Department's own overseas scheme instead.
  • The age limit is counted as on 15 October 2026, the original last date, so the extension does not change the age cut-off as written in the notification.

Frequently asked questions

Is this for undergraduate study abroad? No. The notification restricts the scholarship to PG and PhD courses.

Can I apply before getting an admission offer? No. The notification says the offer letter or admission letter must be uploaded with the application, so apply after you receive it.

Where do I check if my university qualifies? The list of eligible universities, from the World University Ranking 2026 top 300, is Annexure 3 inside the notification PDF on egrantz.kerala.gov.in.

Is the application offline or online? Both. You apply online on the e-grantz portal, then the printed application with annexures must also reach the Backward Classes Development Department. The notification gives no separate date or address for the hard copy.
