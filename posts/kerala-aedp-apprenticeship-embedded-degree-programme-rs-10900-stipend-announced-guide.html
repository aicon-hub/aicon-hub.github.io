Kerala AEDP apprenticeship: reported Rs 10,900 monthly stipend, official rollout notice not yet verified

By AiCoN | Published 22 October 2026 | Sources checked 22 October 2026

News reports on 22 October 2026 said Keralam will implement the Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme (AEDP) of the Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT), under the Union Ministry of Education, from the current academic year. PTI and The Hindu, quoting an official statement, reported that Higher Education Minister Roji M John announced six months of practical industry training alongside studies for final-semester arts, science and commerce undergraduates, with a reported monthly stipend of Rs 10,900 for degree students and Rs 9,600 for diploma students. AiCoN has not independently verified an official Kerala rollout notice, and the application window and route are not yet verified.

What was reported

The following details are as reported on 22 October 2026 by PTI (carried by News18) and The Hindu, both quoting an official statement. At the time we checked (night of 22 October 2026) the full official release text was not yet published on the Kerala Information and Public Relations Department website, so treat these as press-reported details, not portal-confirmed rules.

  • The state government has decided to implement AEDP in higher education institutions in Keralam from the present academic year.
  • The programme is reported to be the Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT) scheme under the Union Ministry of Education.
  • Eligible students are reported to be final-semester undergraduates in arts, science and commerce.
  • Training is reported as six months of practical industrial (on-the-job) training taken alongside studies.
  • Reported monthly stipend during training: Rs 10,900 for degree students and Rs 9,600 for diploma students.
  • The minister reportedly said the initiative aims to improve students' employment prospects at the college level itself. That is a stated aim, not a job guarantee.

What AEDP is (verified from official documents)

AEDP is a central framework that lets a student pursue apprenticeship training as an integrated part of a degree programme. The UGC launched its "Guidelines for Higher Educational Institutions to offer Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme" in 2025, under the National Education Policy 2020, to improve the employability of general-stream students. The framework covers all disciplines recognised under the UGC Act, and training is implemented with higher educational institutions in partnership with the regional Boards of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT) and Boards of Practical Training (BOPT). Keralam falls under BOAT Southern Region, Chennai. Under the guidelines, a degree apprenticeship runs on a tripartite agreement between the institution, the industry or establishment, and the student.

Apprenticeship training in India is governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961. The Department of Higher Education handles apprenticeship programmes for general-stream graduates such as BA, BCom and BSc, as confirmed in Parliament in July 2025.

What is not yet verified

  • No official Kerala rollout notice has been independently located: the application window, portal and route are not yet verified.
  • The list of participating institutions and industries is not published in any source we could verify.
  • Detailed eligibility conditions beyond the reported "final-semester arts, science and commerce students" are not published in any source we could verify.

The University of Kerala has separately said it is preparing six-month paid apprenticeships for its Four-Year Undergraduate Programme students under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) with BOAT Southern Region, but that plan still needs its Academic Council's approval, according to a Careers360 report citing the university's official social media. Its quoted stipend figures differ from the state announcement reports, so wait for each institution's formal notice.

What students can do now

  1. Watch your university and college notice boards and official websites for any AEDP rollout notice for your institution.
  2. Students interested in apprenticeships in general can register on the central NATS 2.0 portal, nats.education.gov.in, which lists apprenticeship opportunities and is the platform used for BOAT-implemented training.
  3. Do not pay anyone who offers to "register" you for this scheme. No fee or paid registration has been announced in the reports, and no application channel has been verified.
