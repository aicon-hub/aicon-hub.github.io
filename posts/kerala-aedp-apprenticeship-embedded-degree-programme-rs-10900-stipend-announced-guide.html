Kerala's Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme: Rs 10,900 a month stipend, announced 22 October

By AiCoN | Published 22 October 2026 | Sources checked 22 October 2026

Keralam will implement the Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme (AEDP) of the Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT), under the Union Ministry of Education, from the current academic year. Higher Education Minister Roji M John announced the decision on Thursday, 22 October 2026. Final-semester undergraduate students in arts, science and commerce will get six months of practical industry training alongside their studies, with a monthly stipend of Rs 10,900 for degree students and Rs 9,600 for diploma students. Applications are not open yet.

What was announced

  • The state government has decided to implement AEDP in higher education institutions in Keralam from the present academic year.
  • The programme is the Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT) scheme under the Union Ministry of Education.
  • Eligible students are final-semester undergraduates in arts, science and commerce.
  • Training is six months of practical industrial (on-the-job) training taken alongside studies.
  • Monthly stipend during training: Rs 10,900 for degree students and Rs 9,600 for diploma students.
  • The minister said the initiative aims to improve students' employment prospects at the college level itself.

The announcement details above are as reported on 22 October 2026 by PTI and The Hindu, quoting an official statement. At the time we checked (night of 22 October 2026) the full official release text was not yet published on the Kerala Information and Public Relations Department website, so treat these as announced details, not yet portal-confirmed rules.

What AEDP is

AEDP is a central framework that lets a student pursue apprenticeship training as an integrated part of a degree programme. The UGC launched its "Guidelines for Higher Educational Institutions to offer Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme" in 2025, under the National Education Policy 2020, to improve the employability of general-stream students. The framework covers all disciplines recognised under the UGC Act, and training is implemented with higher educational institutions in partnership with the regional Boards of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT) and Boards of Practical Training (BOPT). Keralam falls under BOAT Southern Region, Chennai. Under the guidelines, a degree apprenticeship runs on a tripartite agreement between the institution, the industry or establishment, and the student.

Apprenticeship training in India is governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961. The Department of Higher Education handles apprenticeship programmes for general-stream graduates such as BA, BCom and BSc, as confirmed in Parliament in July 2025.

What is not yet known

  • No application window, portal or start date for the Keralam AEDP rollout has been announced. "From the current academic year" is the only timing given.
  • The list of participating institutions and industries is not out yet.
  • Detailed eligibility conditions beyond "final-semester arts, science and commerce students" are not published yet.

The University of Kerala has separately said it is preparing six-month paid apprenticeships for its Four-Year Undergraduate Programme students under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) with BOAT Southern Region, but that plan still needs its Academic Council's approval, according to a Careers360 report citing the university's official social media. Its quoted stipend figures differ from the state announcement, so wait for each institution's formal notice.

What students can do now

  1. Watch your university and college notice boards and official websites for the AEDP rollout notice for your institution.
  2. Students interested in apprenticeships in general can register on the central NATS 2.0 portal, nats.education.gov.in, which lists apprenticeship opportunities and is the platform used for BOAT-implemented training.
  3. Do not pay anyone who offers to "register" you for this scheme. No fee or paid registration has been announced, and no application channel exists yet.
