MNRE mural contest: 9 October deadline, prizes and rules to check

By AiCoN | Published 7 October 2026 | Sources checked 7 October 2026

The current English MyGov page lists Friday, 9 October 2026 at 23:45 IST as the end date for MNRE's mural design contest. The page invites artists, designers and students across India to illustrate Green India 2047. Its linked full rules PDF returned 404 when checked, so some important conditions remain unverified.

Read this before submittingThe visible page gives a current deadline, prizes and artwork specifications. We could not read the full eligibility or intellectual-property conditions because the official-linked rules PDF was broken. Review the complete official conditions before submitting. This guide does not establish that AI-made work is allowed, entry is free, all ages qualify, multiple entries are allowed or copyright stays unchanged.

What is the contest?

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, or MNRE, is inviting mural designs through MyGov. The stated theme is Green India 2047: a future India powered by renewable energy, with innovation, environmental care and inclusive growth.

The page says the winning mural will be showcased at the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit and Expo at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, scheduled for 2 to 5 November 2026. That event description is from the contest page, not a separate check of the event venue or programme.

Which deadline should you use?

The English task page checked on 7 October shows an end date of 9 October 2026, 23:45, with the timezone stated as IST. It also displays Login to Participate.

The earlier AiCoN post listed 30 September. Do not use that older date for this current guide. The live English listing is newer evidence, but the unavailable PDF does not independently confirm the extension or explain its history. Check the portal again before uploading.

What are the prizes?

  • The listed winner prize is ₹5,00,000 and an MNRE recognition certificate.
  • The page says the winner will be reimbursed for round-trip domestic economy airfare to New Delhi.
  • The next five winners are listed to receive ₹10,000 each.

A prize is not guaranteed just for entering. Reimbursement is different from an unlimited upfront travel allowance. Full award, payment and travel conditions were not available in the broken rules file.

Artwork specifications on the visible page

LayoutSuggested 3:2 landscape ratio, scalable to the final wall or backdrop dimensions specified by MNRE.
Submission filesJPEG, PNG or PDF. The winner must provide a print-ready layered source file in AI, PSD or CDR format.
ResolutionAt least 300 DPI at actual print size, or at least 6000 × 4000 pixels when scaled down for online submission.
ColourCMYK for print accuracy. Shortlisted RGB entries may be asked for CMYK conversion.
TextFonts converted to outlines or curves. No editable text in the flattened submission.
AppearanceFull colour, with no watermark, signature overlay or border framing.
Other formatsSuitable for adaptation to standees, backdrop panels and digital displays. Crisp and non-pixelated at 100% magnification.

AI in the file-format list refers to an Illustrator-type source file. It is not permission to use generative AI. The full rules would need to answer that separate question.

A careful submission checklist

  1. Open the official MyGov task page yourself.
  2. Confirm the current date, timezone and participation status.
  3. Try the full Terms and Conditions link. If it remains broken, seek clarification through an official MyGov or MNRE route before committing your work.
  4. Confirm your eligibility, entry limits, accepted creative methods and ownership or licence terms.
  5. Prepare work for the stated theme and dimensions. Keep editable source files and backups.
  6. Check colour mode, outlined fonts, export resolution and absence of overlays.
  7. Use Login to Participate and follow the portal's current form instructions.
  8. Review the final file and details before submitting. Keep any acknowledgement the portal provides.

We did not log in or submit an entry. The logged-in form, upload size cap, acknowledgement process and any account-specific restrictions were not tested.

India and cost boundaries

The main page invites artists, designers and students across India, so Kerala readers are included in that broad invitation. It is not proof of every age, citizenship, team or eligibility condition.

No verified entry-price conclusion is provided here. We also do not repeat the original blanket claim that government contests never charge fees. Design software, printing or preparation can have separate costs.
