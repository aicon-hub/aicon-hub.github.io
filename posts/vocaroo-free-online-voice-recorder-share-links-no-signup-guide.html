Vocaroo: free online voice recorder with instant share links

By AiCoN | Published 22 October 2026 | Sources checked 22 October 2026

Vocaroo is a free, ad-free online voice recorder that has been running since 2007: record, press stop, share a link. No account needed. Here is what its own pages say about recording limits, retention and privacy, and what happened in our scripted test.

Quick facts

  • What it does: records your voice in the browser and gives you a short link (voca.ro/...) to share it, plus QR code, embed, download and delete options. You can also upload an existing audio file.
  • Price: free. The site says it is moving from ads to donations - it shows a Ko-fi donation box and a note titled "No Ads, What Now?", and we saw no ad slots, though its older privacy-policy text still mentions Google Ads.
  • Account: not needed. Its About page says Vocaroo "is fully usable without any account or login". An optional account exists, per its privacy policy.
  • Limits: recordings can run over 6 hours (per the FAQ). Its FAQ says audio is "usually kept for a minimum of 12 months", longer if regularly accessed, but this is not guaranteed - rarely played recordings may expire, and there is no permanent-storage option. A delete button appears in the browser you recorded in.
  • Since: 2007, per the site footer (2007-2026).
  • Best for: sending a quick voice note as a link: feedback, a message for a friend, pronunciation practice, a class recording, without installs or sign-ups.

What it is

Vocaroo calls itself "a quick and easy way to share voice messages over the interwebs". Open the site, press the record button, speak, press stop, then "Save & Share" - you get a short voca.ro link anyone can play in a browser, with QR code, embed code, download and delete options, and share buttons for WhatsApp, email and social sites. The interface is available in many languages; on our connection it appeared in Hindi by default with a language switcher in the corner. There is also an Upload button for existing audio files, and a Schools page for educators.

What we tested

On 22 October 2026 we ran a scripted functional test in a desktop browser with a synthetic test audio device (no real microphone, no real voice). We pressed record, captured 3 seconds of audio, stopped, and pressed "Save & Share". The site uploaded the recording (its uploader reported success) and produced a short voca.ro share link, which redirected to a playable Vocaroo page when we checked it minutes later. We have not kept the test link here: it was a disposable synthetic test, and Vocaroo links should always be treated as public. The sharing screen offered QR code, embed, download and delete. No account was asked for at any point. This was a scripted test of one workflow with synthetic audio, not a review of recording quality with a real microphone.

Is it free?

Yes, and without ads. Every page we read is free to use with no sign-in wall, and the site states it runs on donations via Ko-fi rather than advertising - its own note is titled "No Ads, What Now?". We saw no ad slots during our test. There is no paid plan on the pages we checked; the FAQ mentions guaranteed permanent storage "might be available in the future", implying it does not exist today.

Privacy and data

  • Vocaroo's privacy policy says it collects as little data as necessary: your IP address and browser details may appear in server logs "only when necessary for debugging purposes" and are deleted within two weeks; an email address is collected only if you join the mailing list or create an account, and a mobile number only if you use it as an account login.
  • Data is stored with "well known cloud storage providers located within the European Union", and the policy lists GDPR rights (access, correction, deletion and more).
  • We observed the site loading Rybbit analytics from the operator's own xzist.org domain during our test.
  • Ads are in transition: its privacy policy (last updated June 2021) and Schools page still mention Google Ads on playback pages, while its newer "No Ads, What Now?" note says it is moving to donations. We saw no ad slots on the pages we checked, but treat the ad situation as not fully settled.
  • Important caveat from Vocaroo itself: it is "designed for the easy sharing of audio files" and "should not be used for highly private or confidential audio", because the short shareable links are not strong security. Anyone with the link can play the recording.
  • Its About page also warns: "please don't use this service for anything too important", since messages may occasionally get lost or corrupted.

India

  • No region restriction appears on the pages we read, and the recorder worked in our test. We did not test from India itself.
  • Everything is free, so there is no pricing to convert.

Good to know

  • The FAQ's "minimum of 12 months" is a usual case, not a guarantee, and rarely played recordings may expire sooner - download anything you want to keep forever.
  • The delete button only works in the same browser you recorded in, and disappears if you clear cookies/history.
  • Recording quality depends on the browser; the FAQ itself jokes about browser audio bugs.
  • Vocaroo explicitly allows using its screenshots and logo in publications and offers an official image pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is Vocaroo free?

Yes, completely, with no ads - it runs on donations via Ko-fi, per its own pages.

Do I need an account?

No. Its About page says it is fully usable without any account or login; an optional account exists.

How long are recordings kept?

Usually a minimum of 12 months, longer if regularly accessed, per the FAQ - but rarely played audio may expire, and there is no guaranteed permanent storage. Download anything important.

Is it safe for private recordings?

No - Vocaroo itself says not to use it for highly private or confidential audio, because the short share links are not strong security. Anyone with the link can listen.
