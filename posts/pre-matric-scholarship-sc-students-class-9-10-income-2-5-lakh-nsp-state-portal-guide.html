Pre-Matric Scholarship for SC students: Class 9 and 10, income up to Rs 2.5 lakh

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

The Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Scheduled Castes and others is a central scheme run through state governments. It gives a yearly allowance to school students. Here is what the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment page says, and what it leaves open.

Quick facts

  • Who runs it: the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India. It is Centrally Sponsored and carried out by State and UT governments, so your home state gives the award.
  • Component I: Scheduled Caste students in Class IX and X, studying full time. Parent or guardian income must be up to Rs 2.50 lakh a year.
  • Component II: children of people in these listed occupations: manual scavenging, tanning and flaying, waste picking, and hazardous cleaning. Classes I to X. There is no family income limit. A certificate is needed from the District Social Welfare officer or an authority the state names.
  • Amount, from 2022-23, per year: day scholars Rs 3,500; hostellers Rs 7,000 (Component I) or Rs 8,000 (Component II, Class III to X).
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the ministry scheme page on 9 November 2026. We did not open any application form.

Where do you apply?

The ministry page says applications are invited on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) in April each year. You register there first. Your details then go to your state, which lets you fill the application on its own portal.

What is the last date?

The ministry page gives no date for the current year, and we did not find a Pre-Matric (SC) entry on the NSP list of scholarships. So we cannot confirm a national last date. One state notice we found, from the Manipur OBC and SC department dated 6 November 2026, says registration for SC and OBC pre and post matric scholarships for 2026-27 was extended to 15 November 2026 on NSP. That is one state's notice. Other states can differ, so check your state portal or your school today.

If you are in Kerala

The Kerala government services dashboard lists "Premetric Scholarship for SC Students for Class 9 and 10" under the Scheduled Castes Development Department, with the EBT portal (ebt.kerala.gov.in) as the service website. The entry was last updated on 16 April 2025, and we did not check whether applications are open now. Ask your school or the Scheduled Castes Development office for this year's date.

Before you apply

  • Keep your caste certificate, income certificate (Component I), school record and bank account details ready. These are common requirements; your state's list decides.
  • Components differ on income, so pick the right one.
  • Only use scholarships.gov.in or your state's government portal. Nobody should ask you for money to apply.

Limits of this guide

  • We read the ministry page, not the full guidelines document, so state-level rules and the documents list can differ.
  • The amounts are the ministry's 2022-23 rates. We did not check for newer ones.
