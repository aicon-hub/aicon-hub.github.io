Zotero: free reference manager that builds your citations

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

Zotero is a free tool that saves the papers, books and web pages you read for a project, then writes the citations and bibliography for you. It is made by a nonprofit and is useful for any student or teacher who writes assignments, a thesis or a paper.

Quick facts

  • What it is: a reference manager. It stores, organises and cites books, articles, reports and web pages.
  • Works on: Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android, according to the Zotero home page.
  • Price: the software is free. The Zotero home page says it is open source and made by an independent nonprofit.
  • Free online storage: 300 MB for syncing your files. More space is paid: 2 GB is $20 a year (about Rs 1,770 at Rs 88 to the dollar), 6 GB is $60 a year, unlimited is $120 a year. These are the prices on zotero.org/storage on 9 November 2026.
  • Citation styles: the home page says it supports over 9,000 styles.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the Zotero home page, quick start guide and storage page on 9 November 2026. We did not install or use Zotero ourselves.

What can it do?

  • Collect: it senses research as you browse, so one click can save an article, a preprint or a book record.
  • Organise: sort items into collections, add tags, or save searches that fill up by themselves.
  • Cite: it creates references and bibliographies inside Word, LibreOffice and Google Docs.
  • Sync: optional. Your data can sync across devices, and you can open your library in a web browser.
  • Share: the home page says you can share a library with as many people as you like at no cost, for example to co-write a paper or give course material to students.

How to start

  1. Go to zotero.org and download the app for your computer.
  2. Add the browser connector so you can save pages with one click.
  3. Save a few items, then put them in a collection for your assignment.
  4. In your word processor, use the Zotero plugin to insert citations and build the bibliography. Pick the style your college asks for.

Free and paid

You do not need to pay to use Zotero. The 300 MB of free storage is for files such as PDFs that you sync. Your reference records are small. If you keep many PDFs in Zotero, you may reach 300 MB and need a paid plan, or you can keep the files on your own computer. We did not check how fast 300 MB fills up.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not install or run Zotero, so we cannot say how smooth it feels in your word processor.
  • Check your university or journal for its required citation style. Zotero offers the style, but you decide which one is correct.
  • Always check the finished bibliography for mistakes. Saved records can have wrong details.
  • Sync is optional. If you do not sync, your data stays on your computer.
