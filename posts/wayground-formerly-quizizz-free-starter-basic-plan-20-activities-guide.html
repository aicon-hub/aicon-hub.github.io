Wayground (formerly Quizizz): what the free Starter plan includes

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

Wayground is the new name of Quizizz, the quiz and lesson site for teachers. It has a free Starter plan, also called Basic. This guide lists what the official help page says that plan includes, and its limits.

Quick facts

  • Name: the Wayground logo says "formerly Quizizz".
  • Free plan: the help page "Wayground Starter (Basic) Plan", updated 12 May 2026, describes it as a free plan with the essential features.
  • Content: basic question types such as multiple choice, fill in the blanks and poll. Formats listed include assessments and quizzes, lessons, flashcards, interactive videos and passages.
  • Delivery: students can join live teacher-paced sessions or finish later at their own pace.
  • AI: the page lists Wayground AI to generate content, with a note that some features differ by region.
  • Storage limit: up to 20 resources stored on your account. You can share up to 5 resources per team at one time.
  • Library: you can view and copy questions from the Wayground Library, but copying premium content such as premium question types is restricted.
  • Reports: basic class-level reports, which you can share with colleagues and parents or guardians.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the Starter plan help page on 9 November 2026. We did not make an account or run a quiz.

Who is it for?

Teachers who want ready-made quizzes and lessons, or who want to make a few of their own. The 20-activity limit suits a small set of regular quizzes.

How to start

  1. Go to wayground.com. The old quizizz.com address may lead there; check the address bar before you sign in.
  2. Search the library for a quiz on your topic, or make your own.
  3. Run it live in class or assign it as homework.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not use Wayground. The help page says features and limits can differ by region, so your account may show different limits.
  • Paid plans exist. We did not check their prices or what changed on the free plan recently. Read the plan comparison on the Wayground help site before you rely on a feature.
  • The 20-activity limit means you must delete old activities to add new ones.
  • Check school rules before you add student names or data to any online tool.

Sources: Wayground Starter (Basic) Plan. Independent guide by AiCoN - not affiliated with Wayground. Checked 9 November 2026. Details can change; check the official source before you act.
