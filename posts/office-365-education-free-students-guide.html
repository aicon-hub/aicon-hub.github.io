Office 365 Education free for students: Word, Excel, PowerPoint with an education email

By AiCoN | Published 20 October 2026 | Sources checked 20 October 2026

Students and educators with an eligible education email address can get Office 365 A1 for free - web versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote and Outlook, plus PC-only Publisher and Access. Microsoft lists the A1 plan as "Free - Donated by Microsoft" on its India education page, and there is no deadline: it is an ongoing donated plan, not a limited offer.

Good to knowYou need an eligible education email address (for example one issued by your college or school). Microsoft can re-verify your eligibility at any time, and the plan works while you are enrolled in or working at a qualified educational organisation. A1 gives the web versions of the apps - desktop app installs need the paid A3 plan (₹210 per user/month on the student tab, GST extra). If you are not eligible, you can still use Word, Excel and PowerPoint free through Microsoft 365 for the web.

Who can apply

  • Students and educators with an eligible education email address
  • A Microsoft account (free to create during signup)
  • Works while you are enrolled in or working at a qualified educational organisation - eligibility may be re-verified

What you get (checked 20 October 2026)

  • Office 365 A1: Free, "Donated by Microsoft"
  • Web-based versions of Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote
  • PC-only versions of Microsoft Publisher and Microsoft Access
  • Teams for Education is part of the Office 365 Education experience described on the page
  • Paid upgrades if you ever need them: A3 at ₹210 and A5 at ₹500 per user/month (student licenses tab, GST extra)
  • Related on Microsoft's students page: 50% off Microsoft 365 with a college email, and 10% off select devices - separate paid offers

How to apply: step by step

  1. Keep your college or school email address ready. It must be an eligible education email - personal Gmail or Outlook addresses will not qualify.
  1. Enter your education email on Microsoft's signup page and complete the verification with your Microsoft account. Approval is often instant through automated verification; where the institution itself needs verification, Microsoft says it can take up to a month.
  1. Once approved, sign in at office.com with your education account and start using Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote on the web. Publisher and Access work on PC.
  1. If your plan ever expires, re-verify your student status to extend it, or move to a personal plan. Expired plans drop to view-only mode.
