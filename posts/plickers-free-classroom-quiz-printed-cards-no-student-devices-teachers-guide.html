Plickers: free classroom quizzes where students need no device

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

Plickers is a classroom quiz tool where only the teacher needs a device. Students answer by holding up a printed card, turned a certain way, and the teacher scans the room with the Plickers phone app. This can help in classes where students do not have phones or laptops.

Quick facts

  • How it works: the official help page says students answer by holding up unique cards, so there is no need for student devices or accounts. It says sessions can even be taken offline if there is no internet.
  • Teacher tools: you make classes and question sets on plickers.com, show questions with the Now Playing feature, and scan the cards with the Plickers mobile app.
  • Free account: unlimited classes, with 63 student spaces in each class. Unlimited sets, with up to 5 questions in each set. Unlimited reports. This is from the official Free versus Pro table.
  • Plickers Pro: sets with unlimited questions and sharing packs with other Pro members. The official price page lists $8.99 a month in US dollars on the monthly plan (about Rs 790 at Rs 88 to the dollar). We did not copy the yearly price.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read three Plickers help pages on 9 November 2026. We did not run a class, print cards or use the app.

Who is it for?

Teachers who want quick multiple-choice checks in class, such as a 5-question exit quiz, and whose students may not all have devices. The cards are printed by the teacher.

How to start

  1. Create a free teacher account on plickers.com.
  2. Add a class and give each student a card number.
  3. Print the cards and write a few multiple-choice questions in a set.
  4. In class, play the question, ask students to hold up their cards, and scan them with the phone app.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not use Plickers. We cannot say how well the scanning works in a crowded room or in poor light.
  • The free plan limits a set to 5 questions. Longer quizzes need Pro or several sets.
  • Student results are stored for review. Check your school rules before you add student names to any online tool.
  • Prices on the official page are in US dollars and can change.
