3uTools: check a used iPhone before you pay, free on a computer

By Muhammed Salman | Published 10 November 2026 | Sources checked 10 November 2026

3uTools is a free iPhone manager for Windows, Mac and Linux. Its most useful feature for most people is the verification report: before you buy a used iPhone, it scans the battery, screen, cameras and parts, and flags phones that were refurbished or had their storage swapped. This guide focuses on that check, with Apple's own official steps alongside. The other features get a short note.

Quick facts

  • What it is: a free all-in-one iPhone and iPad manager, per 3u.com. We found no paid tier on the official site.
  • Best use: a one-click verification report for a used iPhone. The official features page says it checks device information, battery health, screen touch, camera imaging and Face ID, and identifies refurbished, storage-upgraded and official replacement devices.
  • Works on: Windows (V9.13, updated 3 November 2026) and macOS (V9.07, 20 September 2026, for Intel and Apple Silicon). The site also lists Linux deb and rpm packages (V9.03, 9 October 2026).
  • Price: free. No paid plan is listed on 3u.com.
  • Privacy: 3uTools is made by a Chinese developer. A 2022 review by tutonaut.de measured frequent connections to the developer's own servers and advised against using it. Do not sign in with your Apple ID inside the app.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read 3u.com on 10 November 2026. We did not install or run 3uTools. We have no iPhone, Windows PC or Mac at hand to test it.

Check a used iPhone before you pay

Ask the seller to let you connect the phone to your computer. If they refuse, walk away.

  1. Download 3uTools only from www.3u.com and install it on your computer.
  2. Connect the iPhone with a USB cable and tap Trust on the phone when asked.
  3. Open the device page and run the verification report. The official site says it produces a one-click report covering battery health, screen, camera and Face ID, and flags refurbished, storage-upgraded and replacement devices.
  4. Read every line before you pay. A storage-swap flag means the seller's "128 GB" phone may have started life with less storage.

Apple's own checks, no extra app needed

  • Activation Lock: Apple says the phone must be erased and removed from the previous owner's account. If you see an "iPhone Locked to Owner" screen, or setup asks for the old owner's Apple Account, do not take the phone. It is ready only when you see the Hello screen.
  • Repair history: on iOS 15.2 or later, open Settings, tap General, then About. Apple's Parts and Service History section shows whether repairs used genuine Apple parts.
  • Warranty: enter the serial number at checkcoverage.apple.com to see coverage and purchase-date status.

What else 3uTools does, in short

  • Backup and restore, and batch import or export of photos, videos, music, ringtones and e-books.
  • A ringtone maker, and contact backup with duplicate merging.
  • Smart Flash: downloads iOS firmware and reinstalls it, with a Retain Data option that keeps your files, and a Deep Clean mode.
  • A toolbox with extras like screen mirroring (3uAirPlayer), photo deduplication and format conversion.

Two sections to skip

  • Jailbreak: 3uTools ships jailbreak tools. AiCoN does not cover jailbreaking. It voids warranty and weakens the phone's security.
  • App Market: it installs apps from outside the App Store. The site says no Apple ID is needed, but leave this section alone, and never type your Apple ID password into the app.

Download only from 3u.com

The official site is www.3u.com. Lookalike domains exist: a security scan flags 3u-tools.com, and 3utools.io is not the developer's site either. On a Mac, the official instructions say to right-click the installer and choose Open. Do that only for a file downloaded from 3u.com.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not install or run 3uTools. The report features above are what the official site describes, not our measurement.
  • The tutonaut.de privacy review is from 2022 and the app has changed since. Treat the privacy caution as still open, not settled.
  • Flashing firmware can go wrong. If you ever use Smart Flash, back up first, and choose Retain Data only after the backup exists.
  • Alternatives: Apple's own Apple Devices app or iTunes covers basic backup on Windows, and the Finder does it on a Mac. iMazing does similar checks but is paid.
