HubSpot Academy: free marketing and sales courses with certifications

By AiCoN | Published 16 October 2026 | Sources checked 16 October 2026

HubSpot says its Academy is a free, self-paced training site open to everyone, with marketing, sales and service courses and certifications. You need to make an account.

What it is

HubSpot Academy is an online training site from the software company HubSpot, with video lessons, courses and certifications on marketing, sales, customer service and HubSpot's own software. HubSpot's help centre describes it as "HubSpot's free, self-paced learning and training resource, publicly available to everyone". It is not an AI tool. We included it as a free learning resource, mainly for people learning business skills such as social media, content, SEO and sales.

Is it free?

Yes. The Academy courses page says "It's Free", and HubSpot's help centre calls the Academy a free resource open to everyone. The Content Marketing certification page is labelled "FREE Certification Course". You do not need to be a HubSpot customer. HubSpot's help centre says that to sign up you fill in a form that asks for a company name and website, but it does not check them, so you can enter any company and web address if you do not have one.

What you can do with it

  • Take courses: the home page lists certification courses such as Social Media Marketing (5 hours), Digital Marketing (3 hours), Content Marketing (8 hours), SEO (4 hours) and Inbound Sales (3 hours).
  • Earn certifications: the courses page says its certifications are offered in 5 languages and that over 250,000 people hold one.
  • Share your result: the help centre says you can view, download or share completed certifications, and update the name on your badge by changing your name in your account.
  • Retake exams: you can retake a certification exam 12 hours after the last attempt, per the help centre.

What to watch out for

  • You need an account. Courses sit behind a HubSpot sign-up.
  • Some certifications include practical exercises done in a HubSpot account, and some are graded by hand, which the help centre says can take 5 to 7 business days.
  • Some courses teach HubSpot's own software. They are most useful if you will use HubSpot. Others cover general skills.
  • HubSpot is a company. The training also introduces its products, and its tools may have paid plans. We did not check those prices.

Privacy and data

  • HubSpot's privacy policy (last modified 16 September 2026) says HubSpot acts as a controller of your personal data when you sign up for its products and services or visit its websites, and uses it to provide services, contact you about relevant content, and improve and promote its products.
  • It says HubSpot also collects some data about people in their professional capacity from public sources and shares it with customers. It gives a form to opt out and delete your data from that dataset.
  • We read only the opening of the policy. Read the sections on what is collected and on your choices before you sign up.

India

  • We saw no country limit on the Academy pages. We did not test sign-up or the exams from India, and did not check Hindi availability.

Frequently asked questions

Is HubSpot Academy free?

Yes. HubSpot calls it a free, self-paced resource open to everyone.

Do I need a company?

No. HubSpot says it does not validate the company name or website on the sign-up form.

Can I retake an exam?

Yes, after 12 hours, per HubSpot.
