Meghalaya CM Scholarship 2026-27: Class XI to Ph.D., apply by 6 November

By AiCoN | Published 12 October 2026 | Sources checked 12 October 2026

The Government of Meghalaya has invited fresh and renewal applications for the Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme (CMSS) for 2026-27. It is for permanent residents of Meghalaya studying regular, full-time courses from Class XI to Ph.D. in recognised institutions, inside or outside the state. The notice proposes student registration from 7 October to 6 November 2026; we did not verify live portal opening or operation.

Good to knowThe official notice (dated 5 October 2026) lists dates and documents but not the scholarship amount or other eligibility rules, and calls the dates "proposed". Check the full scheme details on the portal before you apply.

Proposed dates in the notice

  • 7 October 2026: application portal opens (fresh and renewal)
  • 6 November 2026: portal closes for student registration
  • 4 December 2026: last date for state verification and for students to fix defective applications

Documents to upload

Fresh application (first time)

  • Latest or previous year's marksheet
  • Bonafide certificate
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Bank passbook or cancelled cheque, when giving bank details
  • Identity proof of the applicant or guardian: MHIS card, MGNREGA job card, EPIC/voter ID or ration card

Renewal in the same institute

  • Latest (previous year) marksheet
  • Bonafide certificate from the current institute
  • Recent photograph
  • Bank passbook or cancelled cheque, only to change the earlier bank details

Renewal in a new institute

  • Latest (previous year) marksheet
  • Bonafide certificate from the new institute
  • Recent photograph
  • Bank passbook or cancelled cheque, only to change the earlier bank details

How to apply

The notice says to apply online only, through the Meghalaya One link it gives: meghalayaone.gov.in/meghalaya-one. For queries it gives the email scholarshipregn@gmail.com. Only use links from the official notice, and do not give your documents, bank details or money to an unofficial agent. The notice does not state an application fee; we did not verify the portal process.

We did not open the application portal. We read the announcement page and the notice PDF on 12 October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply?

Permanent residents of Meghalaya, in regular full-time courses from Class XI to Ph.D., per the notice.

How much is the scholarship?

The notice does not say. Check the scheme page on the portal.

What changed since this post?

Official announcement and both pages of the scanned notice checked 12 October 2026. Notice reference CE/SCHOL/CM-SS/3/2025-26/51 is dated 5 October 2026. Although its reference and PDF filename contain 2025-26, the body explicitly invites applications for Academic Year 2026-27. Dates are proposed, not live portal confirmation. No award amount, income threshold or merit rule is stated in this notice; those remain unverified.
