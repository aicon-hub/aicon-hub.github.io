Design for Bharat 2027: Open Challenge for youth 18-29, submit by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 13 October 2026 | Sources checked 13 October 2026

Design for Bharat 2027 is a national product design challenge from the Department of Youth Affairs, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, under the Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue (VBYLD) 2027. The Open Challenge takes entries on the MY Bharat portal until 31 October 2026, and participation is free of cost, as the official FAQ states.

Good to knowThe official page does not list a cash prize amount for the Open Challenge. It describes state-level recognition, mentoring and a national showcase. Do not expect or promise prize money. A few details on the page differ from each other (see the last section), so confirm them on the portal when you register.

At a glance

  • Who: Indian citizens aged 18 to 29, enrolled in a college, university or recognised academic institution when applying
  • Team: individual, or up to 3 members
  • Task: design a tangible product, system or solution in one of 7 categories
  • Last date to submit: 31 October 2026
  • Fee: none

The 7 categories

  • Smart Agriculture and Agri-Tech (Krishi)
  • Accessible Healthcare and Assistive Tech (Swasthya)
  • Sustainable Clean Energy and Mobility (Paryavaran)
  • Rural Housing and Climate-Resilient Infrastructure (Vikas)
  • Waste-to-Wealth and Circular Economy (Swachh Bharat)
  • Next-Gen Handicrafts and Artisanal Tooling (Vocal for Local)
  • Next-Gen Civic Utilities and Disaster Resilience (Jan Raksha)

The detailed FAQ says Challenge 1 must centre on a physical, tangible product. Standalone software, websites and purely UI/UX solutions are not eligible. Digital components can support a physical product.

What to submit

  • A concept note of up to 500 words
  • Up to 7 posters: Poster 1 concept visuals, Poster 2 main visual or render, Posters 3 to 5 process and detail, Posters 6 and 7 supporting information such as sustainability or technology
  • A declaration confirming the work is original and allowing display in exhibitions and publications linked to the competition
  • Posters in JPG at 300 DPI

How to apply: step by step

Checked on the live MY Bharat portal on 19 October 2026.

  1. Confirm you are eligible (aged 18-29) and pick your challenge - the Open Challenge or the Graduation Project Challenge - and one of the 7 categories.
  2. Register on the MY Bharat portal if you do not have an account: open mybharat.gov.in, click Register Now, then Register on the registration page. Enter your 10-digit mobile number or email, tap Get OTP, enter the OTP, then fill the sign-up form (name, password, date of birth, gender, state, urban or rural area, local body, PIN code), tick the Terms & Conditions box and Submit. You land on your MY Bharat profile, where you can also download a joining certificate.
  3. Sign in and open the Design for Bharat page: mybharat.gov.in/pages/design-for-bharat. Click REGISTER NOW in the "Registration Open" box.
  4. Choose your challenge and category, fill the application form and upload your design entry as the form directs. The page's FAQ section (Q2-Q4) explains registration, eligibility and what the application needs.
  5. Submit before 31 October 2026.

Steps 4-5 happen inside your own MY Bharat login, so they are not shown as screenshots.

What happens next

  • State or UT expert panels evaluate entries. State results are listed for 11 to 18 November 2026, with state award events between 15 November and 7 December 2026.
  • The top entries from each state or UT go to the national stage. 36 national finalists are chosen.
  • Finalists get online mentoring from 8 December 2026 to 7 January 2027, then in-person mentoring in New Delhi and a national showcase at Bharat Mandapam during VBYLD 2027 (10 to 12 January 2027).

Check these on the portal

  • The DfB page lists online mentoring from 8 December, while the general VBYLD timeline lists 10 December. The detailed FAQ says final venue and dates will be communicated through official channels. Treat post-submission dates as listed plans, not final travel instructions.
  • The poster file size limit is given as 20 MB per poster in the submission rules and 7 MB in the FAQ.
  • The FAQ mentions a theme called Duality (Dwividhta) and submitting via a State/UT nodal institution. The process section says to submit through the portal. Confirm what applies to your entry.
  • Detailed eligibility and stages depend on the competition docket or kit, which the page says will be provided separately. Registration is offered for further details; AiCoN did not verify when or how the full docket is delivered.
  • Graduation project students have a separate challenge (DfB Graduation Project Challenge) with the same 31 October date.

We read the Design for Bharat page and the VBYLD 2027 page on mybharat.gov.in on 13 October 2026. We did not register or open the docket.

What changed since this post?

Official MY Bharat pages rechecked on 13 October 2026. The Open Challenge deadline remains 31 October 2026. The conflicting poster limits (20 MB and 7 MB), Duality theme and State/UT nodal-institution FAQ route are preserved rather than silently choosing one. No prize amount is promised.
