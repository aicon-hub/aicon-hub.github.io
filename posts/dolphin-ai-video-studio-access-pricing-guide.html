Dolphin AI: invite access, paid credits and video limits

By AiCoN | Published 7 October 2026 | Sources checked 7 October 2026

Dolphin now publishes subscription plans, but entry still requires an invite code or waitlist admission. Its own site gives different 60- and 90-second claims, so check the actual template before paying.

What does Dolphin AI offer?

Dolphin presents an AI studio for social videos, ads, micro-dramas and other longer productions. Its site describes templates, character and script inputs, multi-shot output, lip sync, recasting, motion control and an editor. It also lists several image and video model providers under one subscription.

The idea is to keep a production coherent across shots rather than manually joining unrelated short clips. These are vendor descriptions, not a hands-on assessment of character consistency, lip sync or export quality.

Can you use it now?

The original dolphinai.pro page now presents product features rather than only a Coming Soon notice. The current linked product surface is trydolphin.ai. Its early-access page explicitly says access is via exclusive invite codes and offers a waitlist.

A pricing page or a sign-up link does not guarantee immediate admission. This check did not submit a waitlist form, create an account, buy credits or generate a video. Confirm you can actually use the intended tools before committing to a paid plan.

Monthly prices and approximate INR

The current public pricing page, checked in the browser, lists Starter at US$7 monthly with 105 credits, approximately ₹675. Pro is shown at US$28, reduced from US$39, with 590 credits, approximately ₹2,700 at the displayed price and ₹3,761 at the crossed-out reference. Max is US$68, reduced from US$100, with 1,500 credits, approximately ₹6,557 and ₹9,643 respectively.

These estimates use a sourced reference of ₹96.43 per dollar and exclude taxes, conversion and a final account quote. The displayed discounts have no verified expiry in the pages checked. Do not treat them as permanent prices.

The page lists access to all models, commercial use and Dolphin MCP across the plans, with additional support or generation-speed features for higher tiers. A commercial-use label does not clear third-party faces, music, logos or copyrighted inputs.

Credits expire and renewals are automatic

The pricing FAQ says generation cost varies by model, template and duration. It gives example output counts, but these are not an unlimited allowance or a guarantee for your settings.

Unused subscription credits do not roll over and expire at the end of the monthly billing period. The subscription renews automatically. Upgrades take effect immediately under the stated FAQ. Check cancellation, refund and unused-credit treatment in the current account terms before buying; those details were not tested here.

Is the limit 60 or 90 seconds?

The original official site has a heading promising up to 60-second videos, while other sections describe 90-second videos and a complete 90-second production from one prompt. That is an unresolved conflict on the vendor's own page.

Neither number is being presented here as a tested universal limit. Ask which model, template, resolution and workflow supports the length you need, and whether extra credits or editing are required. A maximum-length claim does not prove every output maintains quality for that duration.

How to evaluate it safely

  1. Start from the official site and verify invite or waitlist access.
  2. Read the current plan, renewal, cancellation and refund terms.
  3. Choose a short test with material you own or have permission to use.
  4. Check the selected model, duration and credits before generation.
  5. Inspect character details, speech, subtitles, products and shot continuity.
  6. Confirm output rights and remove misleading or unapproved likenesses before sharing.
  7. Review remaining credits and renewal settings before the next billing period.

For creators in Kerala

Test Malayalam speech, pronunciation and captions separately. General multilingual lip-sync marketing does not establish Malayalam quality. For a local product ad, check that the output shows the real packaging, price and offer accurately. MCP access is also a permission decision: review what connected software can generate and spend.

What changed since the original post?

The archive post said Coming Soon and no usable public tool. Current pages now provide paid plans and invite-code access. This guide adds expiring credits, auto-renewal and the vendor's unresolved 60/90-second descriptions rather than repeating the old waitlist-only pricing claim.
