GOVERNMENT SCIENCE SCHOLARSHIP

INSPIRE-SHE 2025: applications open until 7 December 2026

By AiCoN | Official sources checked 18 November 2026

The Department of Science and Technology's official INSPIRE portal confirms that applications for INSPIRE-SHE (2025) are open from 7 November to 7 December 2026, 23:59 IST. SHE supports eligible students pursuing basic and natural sciences, with an annual scholarship component and a separate summer-research mentorship grant.

Important bank condition: DST's official FAQ requires a regular normal State Bank of India savings account in the scholar's own name, Aadhaar-linked, for DBT. Joint accounts and other banks are not accepted under that FAQ. This is a required savings-account condition, not a choice of any bank. Check it before deciding whether to apply.

How much support does SHE provide?

  • Rs60,000 per year in scholarship: the FAQ expresses this as Rs5,000 per month. Do not assume monthly bank credits; release depends on required documents and approval.
  • Rs20,000 per year for summer mentorship: linked to research under an active researcher at a recognised centre, with a project report and mentor's certificate. It is not an unrestricted extra monthly allowance.
  • Up to five years, or course completion, whichever is earlier: continuation is conditional on academic performance and institutional recommendation.

DST describes 12,000 SHE scholarships annually. This is a Government of India science scholarship, not a bank-sponsored scholarship or a loan. Its separate SBI account requirement still applies.

Who should check eligibility?

The official call is labelled SHE-2025, even though the application window is in 2026. Do not confuse the application year with the batch. DST describes SHE's target age group as 17 to 22. Its FAQ says Class XII completion and enrolment in the eligible science degree must be in the same year.

Eligible degree formats in the newer DST FAQ include BSc, BS, four-year BSc with Research, Integrated MSc and Integrated MS in supported basic and natural sciences. Merely taking any BSc course is not enough.

The main route is aggregate Class XII marks within the top 1% of the relevant state or central board, together with enrolment in an eligible science course. The FAQ also describes specified national rank/talent routes. Read the current call for the category that fits you; a JEE or medical entrance rank does not make an engineering or medical degree an eligible SHE course.

Subjects: check the exact list, not just the degree name

The DST FAQ lists undergraduate science subjects including Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Statistics, Geology, Astrophysics, Astronomy, Electronics, Botany, Zoology, Biochemistry, Anthropology, Microbiology, Geophysics, Geochemistry, Atmospheric Sciences and Oceanic Sciences.

It excludes courses such as Engineering, Medicine, Computer Science, Computer Applications, IT, Biotechnology and professional or distance-education courses under the stated rules. The expanded subject list for MSc continuation is a different section from undergraduate entry eligibility. Do not assume every later-stage subject is eligible for a fresh BSc application.

Current-call limitation: the homepage's SHE-2025 call-document link returned to the homepage during our check instead of opening the document. We verified the dates on the live official homepage, and amounts, degree formats and general conditions against DST's official FAQ. We could not verify batch-specific board quotas, a new 28-subject undergraduate list or a date-of-birth cutoff from that call. Those newspaper details are not presented here as confirmed facts. Read the functioning current call before submitting.

Documents to prepare

DST's FAQ names these documents:

  • Class XII marksheet.
  • Class X marksheet/certificate for date-of-birth proof.
  • An endorsement certificate in the prescribed format, signed by the college principal, institute director or university registrar. Do not replace it with an informal letter or assume a department-level signature is enough.
  • The appropriate rank or talent-award certificate if applying through that eligibility category.
  • A board eligibility/advisory note, if supplied; the FAQ lists this as optional.

Check the current portal's photo and file requirements before uploading. Incomplete applications can be rejected. Keep identity documents and account details private.

How to start safely

  1. Open online-inspire.gov.in and check the SHE-2025 announcement, deadline, current rules and formats.
  2. Use the Scholarship section, not Fellowship or Faculty. These are different programmes.
  3. New applicants can choose New User? Register here. The public registration form asks for name, mobile number, date of birth, email, password, scheme, identification details and captcha. Use your own correct information.
  4. Complete your own registration and follow the current portal's instructions. Obtain the prescribed institutional endorsement before applying.
  5. Review the eligibility category, degree, subject, uploaded documents and final preview before submitting. Save the acknowledgement and monitor your portal account for decisions and document requests.

Our test stopped at the blank public registration form. We did not create an applicant account, enter personal information, upload documents or submit a scholarship application. The authenticated form, final submission and a successful award were not checked by us.

Renewal is not automatic

The FAQ requires at least 60% marks or GPA 7.0 on a 10-point scale, in the first attempt, for continuation. Failing a subject or falling below the required level can forfeit that year's scholarship. Annual marksheets, the performance report and institutional recommendation matter.

The first release follows approval of the required first-year academic documents; this is not money paid immediately after registration. Applicants must also follow the programme's rules about other scholarships and continuing in a supported course.

What Kerala readers should note

Kerala students in an eligible board/category and science degree can check this national scheme. We have not verified the clipping's figures of 331 for Kerala, 1,397 for CBSE or 82 for CISCE against the inaccessible current call, and do not treat these numbers as a personal chance of selection. The board top-1% rule is not the same as a universal 99% marks requirement.
