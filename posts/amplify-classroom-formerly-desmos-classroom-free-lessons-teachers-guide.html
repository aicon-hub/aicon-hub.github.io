Amplify Classroom (formerly Desmos Classroom): free lessons for teachers

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

If you used Desmos Classroom, it is now called Amplify Classroom. Amplify says it is a free K-12 teaching platform with interactive lessons in maths, literacy and science, and real-time views of what students are doing.

Quick facts

  • Name change: the Amplify page says "Amplify Classroom (formerly Desmos Classroom)". Amplify wrote that in 2025 Desmos Classroom became Amplify Classroom.
  • Free lessons: the page says more than 1,000 free interactive lessons across K-12 maths, literacy and science. It also lists Desmos classics for grades 6 to 8 and Polypad manipulatives.
  • Free for teachers: Amplify says teachers can create a free account and use the teaching tools and many lessons without a district licence or subscription.
  • Paid programs: Amplify Desmos Math and Amplify CKLA also run on the platform, and you access them if your school or district subscribes.
  • Will free stay free: Amplify says everything that is free will remain free. That is a promise on its page, not something we can check.
  • Teacher tools listed: customise ready lessons or build your own with a drag-and-drop builder, take snapshots of class work, watch student ideas in real time and pace the class.
  • Login: the old Desmos Classroom email and password work through "Login with Amplify" at classroom.amplify.com. Google sign-in continues to work, per Amplify.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the Amplify Classroom page and the migration FAQ on 9 November 2026. We did not create an account or run a lesson. We did not check the free Desmos graphing calculator.

Who is it for?

Maths teachers first, since the lessons started as Desmos maths. The page now also lists literacy and science. Check that the lessons match your syllabus and language.

How to start

  1. Open amplify.com/classroom and choose a grade band to browse free lessons.
  2. Log in at classroom.amplify.com if you have an old Desmos Classroom account.
  3. Pick a lesson, preview it, then run it with your class.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not use Amplify Classroom. The lessons are made for US grade levels, so they may not match Indian syllabuses.
  • Student accounts and data rules differ by school. Check yours.
  • Amplify says old account content is kept if the accounts were verified. Check your own content after you log in.

Sources: Amplify Classroom · Desmos Classroom to Amplify Classroom FAQ. Independent guide by AiCoN - not affiliated with Amplify or Desmos. Checked 9 November 2026. Details can change; check the official source before you act.
