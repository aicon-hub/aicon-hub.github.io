Project Nevermore: 24-hour hackathon at JAIN Kochi, ₹30,000 prize pool, 29-30 October

By AiCoN | Published 20 October 2026 | Sources checked 20 October 2026

μLearn JUKC and Yugantra 2026, led by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at JAIN (Deemed-to-be) University, Kochi, are running Project Nevermore, a Halloween-themed 24-hour hackathon. It starts at 2:00 PM on 29 October and ends at 2:00 PM on 30 October 2026 at the university's Infopark campus in Kakkanad, Kochi. Teams of 4 to 6 members pay ₹249 each and compete for a ₹30,000 prize pool. Food is provided during the event.

Official publisher artwork hosted by its publisher. Official source. Independent guide, not affiliated or sponsored. Artwork and trademarks belong to their owners.

Good to knowThis is a paid event: ₹249 per member, and every member of the team needs a ticket, except one eligible μLearn Level 5 Karma participant who can get free registration. Tickets are sold on Snaptiqz, a third-party ticketing platform, not the university's own site, and payment gateway charges apply on top (a 4-member team totalled ₹1,037.55 when we checked). The "Side Quest" prize, a one-month Claude or ChatGPT subscription, is announced by the organisers; its detailed terms are not published.

Who can enter

  • Teams of 4 to 6 members
  • The registration form asks for the team name, the team leader's college or school ID number, the leader's GitHub profile and an emergency contact, so this is aimed at college and school students
  • One participant with μLearn Level 5 Karma can get 100% free registration, per the organisers

Prizes

  • Winner: ₹15,000
  • Runner-up: ₹9,000
  • Second runner-up: ₹6,000
  • Every participant gets a certificate and stickers
  • Side Quest: a separate challenge with a chance to win a one-month Claude or ChatGPT subscription

How to register: step by step

Checked on the live ticketing page on 20 October 2026.

  1. Open the official event page: discover.snaptiqz.com/event/24-hour-hackathon (linked from the 24-Hour Hackathon card on yugantra.in). Check the date, time, venue and fee shown, then tap Get Ticket.
  2. Choose Log in or sign up or Continue as guest. Guest tickets are not saved to an account, so signing in is safer if you want to find your ticket again later.
  3. On "Select tickets", tap Add on the General ticket (₹249 per member, team of 4-6) and then Proceed.
  4. On "Details & payment" a 15-minute timer starts. Enter the team name, the team leader's college or school ID number, the leader's GitHub profile and an emergency contact name and number. Tick the consent to event rules and to photos and videos, pick the team captain (the captain pays for the team and gets the confirmation) and tap Next. There is a discount code box on this screen; the organisers have not said publicly how the Level 5 Karma free registration is applied, so eligible participants should confirm with the organiser (+91 81298 69269) before paying.
  5. Pay online to finish. Payment gateway charges are added to the ticket total. Every member of the team needs their own ticket.
  6. On 29 October, report at the venue by 1:30 PM for check-in. Bring a laptop and charger, an extension cord or power strip, a phone charger or power bank, and personal essentials for the overnight stay.

Rules to read

  • All project work must begin during the event. Pre-built projects are not allowed
  • Final submissions close at 2:00 PM on 30 October
  • Overnight stay rules will be announced by the organisers
  • Misconduct or plagiarism leads to disqualification
  • Food and refreshments are provided during the event
