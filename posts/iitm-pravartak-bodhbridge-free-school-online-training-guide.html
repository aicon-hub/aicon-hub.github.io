IITM Pravartak + BodhBridge: free live online classes for Classes 7-12

By Muhammed Salman | Published 19 November 2026 | Sources checked 19 November 2026

Official publisher-hosted logo. Independent guide, not affiliated or sponsored. Official source

School students across India, including Kerala, can register for live online training in AI fundamentals, entrepreneurship, electric vehicles and other practical subjects through IIT Madras Pravartak and BodhBridge. The training registration is free. The optional certification assessment is a separate route, not a promise of a free certificate.

What we checked: the official course directory, its enrolment link to Fundaspring, the AI course details and the public registration form. We did not register a student, submit personal information, pay for an exam, attend a class or evaluate teaching quality. This is an enrolment-access guide.

Who can join?

The school-student programme is for Classes 7 to 12 across India. The form includes State Board, CBSE, ICSE and other board options, and a Kerala state option labelled "Keralam". It asks for the student's class and school. Parents should help younger students review the form and its contact consent before applying.

This is an IITM Pravartak training initiative in collaboration with BodhBridge. It is not admission to an IIT degree programme. The directory also lists teacher programmes and offline courses; those have different audiences and arrangements. This guide focuses on the free online school-student route.

All times below are Indian Standard Time. These are class dates, not stated application deadlines. Check the current page and joining instructions before making plans.

AI schedule detail: the Fundaspring AI course page lists two live sessions, on 23 and 27 November, both 6-8 PM IST. The registration form summary lists only 23 November. Follow the organiser's final joining instructions for the complete schedule. The product page's total-duration wording also includes learning resources; do not read it as eight hours of live classes.

Several other subjects in the form have dates marked TBA. Space Science illustrates why a current check matters: the directory still shows 14 November, while the form says TBA. We are not presenting that older date as an upcoming class. The current directory and form describe cohorts running from November 2026 to February 2027.

How to find the free training form

  1. Start at the official IITM Pravartak + BodhBridge directory.
  2. Under For School Students, choose an Online - LIVE course. Do not confuse these with the offline bootcamps in Chennai.
  3. Select Enroll. The official directory takes you to the course page on Fundaspring, the BodhBridge platform. For example, open the AI Fundamentals course page we checked.
  4. Choose Register for Free Training. This leads to the school-student online-course registration form. The separate training-and-certification-exam button is a different route.
  5. Review the fields, select the courses you want and read the contact consent. The form allows multiple course selections. Only submit if the details are correct and you accept the terms. We did not test submission or receipt of joining details.

What information does the form ask for?

The public form asks for name, gender, email, student phone, parent's phone, class, school board, school name, course choices, city/town, state and PIN code. It requests WhatsApp-enabled numbers where possible for updates. It also asks whether you may take the optional certification assessment later, with Yes, No and Maybe choices.

Privacy point: the form includes a required consent checkbox allowing Fundaspring and its partners to contact you by email, SMS, WhatsApp and similar channels. Read it before sharing student and parent contact details. If you do not agree, ask the organiser about alternatives rather than submitting anyway. We have not audited retention, partner sharing or marketing practices.

Free class does not mean free certification

The official directory describes free registration for online live sessions and an optional proctored certification exam. The course page separates free training from the exam route. The form says exam registration can be done later, separately.

We have not verified the final exam fee, taxes, refund terms or any later checkout total. Do not treat the training's zero-price listing as the certificate price. You do not need to use the certification checkout just to follow the free-training link. The AI course page says certification requires passing the examination; attendance alone is not a guaranteed certificate.

What does the AI course cover?

The organiser lists AI basics, how AI learns from data, the AI project life cycle, generative AI and prompting, AI applications, and responsible AI. These are provider descriptions, not learning outcomes we tested. This may suit a student who wants a scheduled introduction with a trainer, rather than only self-directed chatbot questions. We have not verified teaching language, recordings, seat availability, accessibility support or how quickly joining links arrive.

Before registering

  • Use the official directory first so you reach the intended course and form.
  • Read current dates and the final joining instructions. Ask the organiser if the two AI schedule descriptions differ.
  • Do not enter passwords, Aadhaar or payment-card details in the free-training form. Those were not part of the fields we inspected.
  • Use the support details on the current official course page for access or schedule questions. The directory lists +91 8248916174 and +91 9003106272.

Sources and test limits

  • IITM Pravartak + BodhBridge official directory: audience, online/offline distinction, free registration and course dates.
  • Fundaspring AI Fundamentals course page: free-training and certification routes, syllabus and two-session schedule.
  • Public school-student registration form: current course choices, information requested and consent.
