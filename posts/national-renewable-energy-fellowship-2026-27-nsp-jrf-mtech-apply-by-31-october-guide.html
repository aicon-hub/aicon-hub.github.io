National Renewable Energy Fellowship 2026-27: up to ₹67,000 a month on NSP, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 25 October 2026 | Sources checked 25 October 2026

The National Renewable Energy Fellowship (NREF) is the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy's merit-based fellowship for higher studies and research in renewable energy: M.Tech, PhD (JRF to SRF) and post-doctoral research. Monthly support ranges from ₹12,400 for M.Tech to ₹37,000 for JRF and ₹58,000 to ₹67,000 for post-doctoral fellows. The National Scholarship Portal lists a live 2026-27 application window closing 31 October 2026, but awards go only to students selected by NREF-allotted institutes through GATE, CSIR-JRF, NET or AICTE score routes.

At a glance

  • What: central fellowship for M.Tech, PhD and post-doc research in renewable energy subjects
  • Who: students joining renewable-energy courses at NREF-allotted institutes, selected through GATE / CSIR / CSIR-JRF / AICTE / NET scores
  • Amount: M.Tech ₹12,400/month; JRF ₹37,000/month + HRA + ₹20,000/year contingency; PDF/RA ₹58,000-67,000/month
  • Scale: 33 new fellowships each year (15 M.Tech, 16 JRF, 2 RA/PDF), 165 over the five-year scheme period
  • Deadline: 31 October 2026 (student applications on NSP)
  • Where: scholarships.gov.in, under Ministry of New and Renewable Energy; no application fee

Fellowship amounts (official norms)

  • JRF (PhD track): ₹37,000 per month plus HRA as per central government fellowship norms and ₹20,000 per year contingency; 2 years as JRF, then up to 3 years as SRF (5 years maximum).
  • PDF / Research Associate: ₹58,000 (level I), ₹61,000 (level II) or ₹67,000 (level III) per month, for 3 years.
  • M.Tech: ₹12,400 per month for 20-24 months as per institute norms.

How selection actually works

Checked against MNRE's administrative approval for the scheme (12 August 2026) and the live NSP listing on 25 October 2026. Two things are true at once, so read both:

  1. The NSP listing shows a live fresh application window for 2026-27: scheme open from 1 June 2026, student applications open till 31 October 2026. Note that this NSP card links no scheme guidelines or FAQ document (both links are empty as of 25 October 2026), and MNRE's own rules document does not describe a student application on NSP at all.
  2. The ministry's rules describe institute-led selection: NREF slots are allotted to selected institutes, which pick students for M.Tech and PhD courses through GATE, CSIR, CSIR-JRF, AICTE or NET scores and forward names to the ministry. Students without these qualifications are not eligible, and fellowships apply to renewable-energy subjects only.

So treat the NSP window as the portal route, not the selection: the real gate is selection by an NREF-allotted institute, which requires the right score for your category - GATE for M.Tech, CSIR-JRF or UGC-NET (JRF) for the PhD track, and a completed PhD for RA/PDF - in a renewable-energy subject. An NSP application by itself does not make anyone a fellow.

How to apply on NSP

  1. Open scholarships.gov.in and complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if you have not already.
  2. Log in and find Financial Support / National Renewable Energy Fellowship Scheme under Ministry of New and Renewable Energy in the scheme list.
  3. Fill the application with your qualifying score details (GATE / CSIR / NET as applicable) and upload the documents the form asks for.
  4. Submit before 31 October 2026. Institute verification runs till 15 November and district/state verification till 30 November, so your institute must also act on time.

Stay safe

  • Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a fellowship slot or selection.
  • Apply only on scholarships.gov.in, the official National Scholarship Portal. Lookalike portals are common.
  • The ministry disburses by DBT to the fellow's bank account through the host institute; nobody can "arrange" a slot for a fee.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply without GATE or NET? The ministry's rules say students are selected through GATE score (M.Tech), CSIR-JRF / UGC-NET JRF qualification (PhD track) or equivalent AICTE/CSIR/DST/UGC norms, and that students without these qualifications are not eligible. The NSP form is not the only gate.

Is it for any course? No. The fellowship is exclusively for renewable-energy-related M.Tech, PhD and post-doctoral research at institutes allotted NREF slots.

Is the deadline really 31 October? That is what the live NSP listing showed on 25 October 2026 for student applications. Verification stages continue into November.

How many get it? 33 new fellowships each year - 15 M.Tech, 16 JRF and 2 RA/PDF - per the ministry's approval document, 165 across the five-year scheme period.
