RRB Station Master 2026: 1,265 posts for graduates, apply till 25 November

By AiCoN | Published 4 November 2026 | Sources checked 4 November 2026

Indian Railways has opened Station Master recruitment under CEN No. 08/2026, with 1,265 vacancies across the Railway Recruitment Boards. Any graduate can apply. The Press Information Bureau says online applications close on 25 November 2026.

What we could and could not checkThe facts below come from the PIB release of 27 October 2026. The RRB application portal (rrbapply.gov.in) showed "Server Under Maintenance" when we opened it on 4 November, and the notice PDF on rrb.indianrailways.gov.in did not load for us. So we have not read the notice itself. Age limit, fee, exam pattern and the post count for each RRB (including any Kerala board) are not confirmed here. Read them in the official notice before you apply. Nothing was submitted or paid.

Key facts (PIB, 27 October 2026)

  • Notice: Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 08/2026, Station Master.
  • Vacancies: 1,265 across the RRBs. The largest are RRB Mumbai (177), Jammu-Srinagar (122) and Chennai (119).
  • Who can apply: any graduate. PIB says detailed eligibility is in the notification.
  • Applications opened 27 October 2026 and close on 25 November 2026.
  • From 2026 the Railway Board notifies Station Master recruitment separately from other posts.

Where to read and apply

PIB links the detailed notification (CEN 08/2026) and the RRB site rrb.indianrailways.gov.in, and the online application portal rrbapply.gov.in. Use only these official addresses. If the portal is under maintenance, try again later and ignore anyone who asks for money to "reserve" a form.

Not confirmed by AiCoN

  • Age limit, fee and refund rules.
  • Pay level and selection stages (we have not read the notice).
  • How many posts each RRB has, including the one for Kerala.
  • The last date for fee payment. Other websites quote different dates, so check the notice.

Sources: PIB: Indian Railways to Recruit 1,265 Station Masters Nationwide · RRB website · Online application portal. Checked 4 November 2026. Explainer by AiCoN - not a Railway Recruitment Board publication. Details can change; check the official notice before you apply.
