JustWatch: find where to stream a movie or show in India, free

By AiCoN | Published 22 October 2026 | Sources checked 22 October 2026

Type a movie or show, pick your country, and see every service that carries it with subscription, rent and buy prices. Free, no account needed. Here is what the official pages confirm and what our hands-on test on the India catalog found.

Quick facts

  • What it is: a free streaming search engine. Type a movie or TV show and it shows which services carry it in your country, with subscription, rent and buy prices plus quality tags. The India catalog showed 67,526 titles on 22 October 2026, and title pages state the site checked 92 streaming services for India that day.
  • Cost: free, no account needed. An optional account only syncs your watchlist across devices. The site earns from promoted provider offers and advertising.
  • Made by: JustWatch GmbH, headquartered in Berlin, Germany (per its privacy policy).
  • Works on: web browsers (we tested on desktop). The site's menu also links to official apps, which we did not test.
  • Extras: Streaming Charts per title, Top 5 lists for your country, a New section for recent arrivals per provider, and a Sports section.
  • Link: justwatch.com/in

Why it is useful

India has a crowd of streaming services, and remembering which one has a particular film is a small daily annoyance. JustWatch answers "where can I watch this?" in one search, instead of opening five apps one by one.

Our hands-on test (22 October 2026)

We used the India edition, justwatch.com/in, in a desktop browser with no account:

  • The homepage showed the India catalog: 67,526 titles sorted by popularity, with filters for release year, genre, price, rating and age rating, and "Free" tags on some titles.
  • We searched "3 Idiots". The correct 2009 film topped the suggestions. Its page showed ratings (92% JustWatch, 8.4 on IMDb), synopsis, full cast, and honestly reported that it is not available for streaming right now, with a "notify me" option for when it returns.
  • We searched "Interstellar". The page reported "currently available on 5 streaming services" and listed India offers in rupees: a subscription option at ₹149.00/month, rent at ₹119.00 and ₹129.00, and buy at ₹499.00, each with quality tags (HD or 4K, closed captions) and a Prime Video trial offer (30 days free, then ₹299.00/month). Providers shown included JioHotstar, Amazon Prime Video and Apple TV.
  • The page stated it checked 92 streaming services for India on 22 October 2026, with a timestamp.
  • The site's footer listed India's current top providers: Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV, JioHotstar and Apple TV Store.

All of this worked without signing in.

What you can use it for

  • Checking where a specific movie or show streams before movie night.
  • Filtering by "Free" to see what is watchable at no cost in India right now.
  • Comparing rent and buy prices across stores for the same title.
  • Tracking new arrivals on each provider and what is trending on the Streaming Charts.
  • Setting a notify alert for titles that are not streaming at the moment.

Limits to know

  • Availability changes constantly. Each title page carries its own "we checked" timestamp, so treat prices and availability as of that check; the provider's own page is the final word.
  • Not every title is streaming somewhere, as our 3 Idiots search showed.
  • The site carries advertising: promoted provider offers (clearly labeled "PROMOTED") plus third-party advertising scripts - we observed Amazon and Google advertising scripts on the page, and the privacy policy describes personalized advertising (see below).
  • We tested the desktop browser edition only, not the apps or the mobile layout.

Privacy

Searching needs no account. JustWatch's privacy policy names the responsible party as JustWatch GmbH, Saarbrücker Straße 38, 10405 Berlin, Germany, with privacy@justwatch.com as the contact, and says processing follows the GDPR. Per the policy, its servers log technical data (IP address, browser, access times) for connection and security purposes, and personalized advertising uses tracking cookies that you can manage or disable through your browser settings or the site's cookie management tool. A cookie consent notice appeared on our first visit. That is the provider's own policy statement; we did not audit their servers or network.

Access from India

We browsed the India edition directly during our test: the India catalog, rupee pricing and Indian providers (JioHotstar, Prime Video India) all worked. We did not test from an Indian internet connection, but we encountered no sign-up wall or gate during the session.

The verdict

JustWatch ends the "which app has it?" guessing game for Indian streaming. One search, every service, real rupee prices, free and no account: justwatch.com/in
