HIX AI workspace and slides: exports, credits and checks

By AiCoN | Published 7 October 2026 | Sources checked 7 October 2026

HIX AI describes an agent workspace for research, writing, images, video, data analysis and slides. Its presentation page says users can generate, improve or convert slides and export to PowerPoint. Those are vendor feature claims, not proof that every free account can generate and export any deck without limits.

One workspace, different features

The current pricing comparison lists agent workflows, research, writing, slides, images, video and data analysis. Feature access differs between plans.

This guide combines the archive's two HIX posts, including advanced presentation generation. Separate HIX products, such as a browser extension, can have different plans. Do not use an extension's free word allowance as proof of slide access.

What the slides page claims

The official page describes starting from a topic, document, URL or images. It offers generating a deck, improving an uploaded presentation and converting existing files to structured slides.

It says text and visuals stay editable and can be exported to PowerPoint with layout preserved. We did not generate or download a deck. Exact free-plan export access, file formats and fidelity remain untested.

Research and image claims

HIX says its slide workflow researches topics, builds a storyline and keeps uploaded brand fonts and colours consistent. The page names GPT Image 2 and Nano Banana Pro.

A model name does not establish that it is used on every plan or every step. AI research still needs citation checks. AI charts and pictures can communicate a false result convincingly.

Free credits and incomplete prices

The live public pricing page displayed HIX AI Free at $0/month with 20 credits. Paid Max and Pro rows were labelled billed yearly, but their price fields displayed dashes and their credits displayed zero.

Those incomplete fields are not proof the paid plans cost nothing. We could not establish a reliable paid price, credit refresh period or credits per presentation. Read the actual plan offer before paying.

No INR checkout total, paid subscription, free download allowance or refund approval was verified. A page titled Free AI Presentation Maker is not a promise of unlimited free production.

A safe first presentation

  1. Use a short topic and public source material.
  2. Specify audience, purpose and a small slide count.
  3. Ask for a plain outline before detailed slides.
  4. Check the facts and cited sources.
  5. Verify any numbers behind a generated chart.
  6. Inspect text size, spacing, images and missing content.
  7. Check credit use and export access before expanding the deck.
  8. Open the exported file in your intended presentation software.
  9. Review every slide before sharing.

Editable does not guarantee perfect export

Check whether text remains text, charts remain editable and fonts render correctly on another computer. Some generated visuals can still be flattened images.

A marketing statement about preserved layouts is not a measured PowerPoint compatibility test. Keep a copy of your original source file and do not overwrite it with an unreviewed conversion.

India and Malayalam

HIX's About page describes writing and translation in more than 100 languages. This guide did not test Malayalam slides, fonts, layout or an Indian account.

For Kerala work, test a short sample with names and mixed-language text. Check the downloaded deck, not only the browser preview.

Privacy before uploading

A pricing row headed Security & Privacy does not provide a retention schedule or training guarantee. We did not establish the exact policy for uploaded documents, brands, charts or slides in this workflow.

Use public or approved material. Avoid customer records, passwords and confidential drafts until the actual policy and account controls are clear. No files or personal information were uploaded here.

Other work in the workspace

Research, writing and data analysis can support a deck. Do not treat their output as independently verified simply because different agents helped produce it.

Check spreadsheet formulas, citations and final text yourself. Generating SEO text does not guarantee a search ranking or that a claim is correct.

What changed since the original posts?

We consolidate the workspace and advanced-PPT posts into one topic, distinguish vendor export claims from tested output and record incomplete paid price fields instead of inventing a tariff.

Frequently asked questions

No unlimited free deck or export allowance was verified.

Check the exact privacy and account terms first.
