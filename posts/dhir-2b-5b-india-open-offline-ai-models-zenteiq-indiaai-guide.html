Dhīr 2B and 5B: open AI models from India that run offline

By AiCoN | Published 7 November 2026 | Sources checked 7 November 2026

Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B are small language models from the Indian company ZenteiQ. The IndiaAI Mission released them on 3 November 2026. They are built to run on your own laptop or device, without an internet connection.

What was released?

PIB says IndiaAI released two sets of models that day: EKA-1 by Soket, and Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B by ZenteiQ AiTech Innovations. PIB describes the Dhīr models as open source, offline-capable assistants for English and 10 Indian languages.

ZenteiQ lists the languages as Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and Odia, plus English.

The two models

  • Dhīr 2B: 2.03 billion parameters. The 4-bit file is 1.21 GB.
  • Dhīr 5B MoE: 5.09 billion parameters in total, 1.79 billion active per token. The 4-bit file is 2.95 GB.

Both handle 16,384 tokens of context and are released as instruction-tuned checkpoints. ZenteiQ says the licence is Apache 2.0, and its page links to a download on AIKosh, the government's AI repository.

Can I run it on my phone or laptop?

ZenteiQ says Dhīr 2B at 4-bit fits comfortably on a device with 4 GB or 8 GB of memory for regular chats, and Dhīr 5B needs about 8 GB. It reports speeds measured with llama.cpp, for example about 18 tokens per second for Dhīr 2B on laptop integrated graphics. These are the company's own figures.

These are model files, not a finished chat app. Running them means using a tool such as llama.cpp, so this suits developers and curious users more than someone who wants a one-tap app.

The limits

  • ZenteiQ says recall of facts without a source is the weakest area. For law, finance, agriculture, medicine or history, use retrieval from trusted sources and human review.
  • There is no reasoning mode, and the models are text only.
  • The benchmark results on ZenteiQ's page are the company's own. AiCoN has not verified them.

What about EKA-1?

PIB calls EKA-1 an open model with 26.3 billion parameters across all 22 scheduled Indian languages. We could not confirm a public download for it, so this guide does not cover it.

What AiCoN did and did not do

We read the PIB release and ZenteiQ's own pages. We did not download or run the models.
