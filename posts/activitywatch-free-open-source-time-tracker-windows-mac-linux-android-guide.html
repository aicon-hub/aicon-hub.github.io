ActivityWatch: a free time tracker that keeps your data on your device

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

ActivityWatch is an app that tracks how you spend time on your computer or phone. It records which apps and windows you use, then shows you charts. Students can use it to see their study hours. The project says your data stays on your device and is not uploaded to the cloud.

Quick facts

  • What it is: a free and open source automatic time tracker, according to activitywatch.net.
  • Works on: Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.
  • Price: the site calls it free. It is built by volunteers and asks for donations.
  • Privacy: the site says data is stored locally and does not leave your device. It says it is a privacy-first project.
  • What it tracks: the active application and window title out of the box, with more through add-ons the project calls watchers. Browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox can track the active tab.
  • Sync between devices: the site lists this as "we are working on it". Do not plan on it.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the ActivityWatch home page on 9 November 2026. We did not install or run it.

What is it useful for?

  • See how many hours you really spend on study apps and on games or videos.
  • Group your time into categories, such as study, writing and entertainment.
  • Check work hours for a project. The site also lists screen-time habits and work-life balance as uses.

How to start

  1. Go to activitywatch.net and open the download page for your system.
  2. Install it and start it. It appears in your system tray.
  3. Open localhost:5600 in your browser to see the tracking screen.
  4. Install the browser extension if you want to see which sites you use.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not install ActivityWatch. We cannot say how much battery or memory it uses.
  • It records window titles. Those can contain private words, such as document names or page titles. Your data stays on your device, so protect the device itself.
  • On a shared computer, ask before you install any tracker, and do not track other people without their knowledge.
  • Android support is listed on the site. We did not check how it behaves on your phone.
