Cyber Hygiene quiz (NCSAM 2026): official terms, prizes and entry-status caveat

By AiCoN | Published 9 October 2026 | Sources checked 9 October 2026 | Availability rechecked 24 October 2026

The official MyGov page describes an Online Quiz on Cyber Hygiene Practices under ISEA with CERT-In, MeitY and MyGov for NCSAM 2026. Its displayed terms list 15 questions in 300 seconds, ten cash prizes from ₹10,000 to ₹3,000, and an e-certificate for participants. Indian citizens are eligible subject to the organiser-employee and immediate-family exclusions; eligibility does not confirm that entries are currently open. The displayed end date is 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

Availability rechecked 24 October 2026: the browser showed login controls and that end date, while the fetched page said Closed. Actual entry availability is unverified. We did not log in or play. Read the current official page before attempting entry.

How to play: step by step

Checked on the live MyGov quiz pages on 19 October 2026.

  1. Open the official quiz page: quiz.mygov.in/quiz/online-quiz-on-cyber-hygiene-practices-2. The page shows 15 questions, 300 seconds and the end date (31 October 2026, 11:45 PM).
  2. Tap Login with MyGov AUTH to Play Quiz or Login with OTP to Play Quiz.
  3. New to MyGov: register with MyGov Auth or with DigiLocker (MeriPehchaan single sign-on). Already registered: enter your email or mobile number and tap Request OTP.
  4. Enter the OTP sent to your email/mobile to log in.
  5. Fill your profile details if the quiz asks for them, then click Play Quiz - the 300-second timer starts immediately and entries cannot be withdrawn.
  6. If entry is available, answer the 15 questions and submit within the timer. The official terms promise an e-certificate of participation to all participants and list ten cash prizes; winning is subject to the stated selection rules. Certificate delivery was not tested.

Steps 5-6 happen inside your own login, so they are not shown as screenshots.

What does the quiz cover?

The official page lists strong passwords and passphrases, multi-factor authentication (MFA), phishing awareness, software updates, device security, data protection, backups and incident reporting. More information is on staysafeonline.in, per the page. Questions can go to isea@cdac.in.

Rules from the official page

WhoAll Indian citizens. Employees connected with organising the quiz, and their immediate family, are not eligible.
FormatUp to 15 questions within 300 seconds, according to the page header and both English and Hindi terms checked on 24 October 2026. The quiz starts when you click Play Quiz after login, if entry is available.
EntriesOne per participant. Submitted entries cannot be withdrawn.
DetailsYou give your name, email and phone number, which MyGov and C-DAC may use to confirm your details.
Dates8 October 2026, 3:00 PM to 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

What are the prizes?

  • 1st prize: ₹10,000
  • 2nd prize: ₹8,000
  • 3rd prize: ₹5,000
  • Next 7 participants: ₹3,000 each
  • E-certificate of participation for all participants

Winners are selected by an automated random draw from a pool of entries that answer everything correctly within the time limit. The page does not say how the draw assigns the prize tiers. Winners are announced at mygov.in. Entering does not guarantee a prize.

A careful checklist

  1. Open quiz.mygov.in and find the quiz yourself.
  2. Read the full Terms and Conditions on the page.
  3. Revise the basics above: unique passwords, MFA, spotting phishing and where to report incidents.
  4. First confirm that entry is available in your own account. The displayed official end date is 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM; if entry is available, allow 5 uninterrupted minutes before the stated deadline.

We did not log in or play. We read the prizes and all the rules on the quiz page.
