Cyber Hygiene quiz (NCSAM 2026): prizes up to ₹10,000, ends 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 9 October 2026 | Sources checked 9 October 2026

As part of National Cybersecurity Awareness Month, October 2026, the ISEA programme with CERT-In under MeitY and MyGov is running an Online Quiz on Cyber Hygiene Practices. It has 15 questions in 300 seconds and is open to all Indian citizens. Ten winners get cash prizes from ₹10,000 down to ₹3,000, and everyone gets an e-certificate. It runs until 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

Check before you playThe MyGov quiz page showed a "Closed" label when we opened it without logging in, although its dates run to 31 October 2026 and the quiz list shows it as running. Open the page yourself and confirm it lets you start. The timer starts when you click Play Quiz and entries cannot be withdrawn.

What does the quiz cover?

The official page lists strong passwords and passphrases, multi-factor authentication (MFA), phishing awareness, software updates, device security, data protection, backups and incident reporting. More information is on staysafeonline.in, per the page. Questions can go to isea@cdac.in.

Rules from the official page

WhoAll Indian citizens. Employees connected with organising the quiz, and their immediate family, are not eligible.
FormatUp to 15 questions within 300 seconds, according to the page header and English terms. The Hindi terms instead say 10 questions. Check the format shown before starting. The quiz starts when you click Play Quiz.
EntriesOne per participant. Submitted entries cannot be withdrawn.
DetailsYou give your name, email and phone number, which MyGov and C-DAC may use to confirm your details.
Dates8 October 2026, 3:00 PM to 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

What are the prizes?

  • 1st prize: ₹10,000
  • 2nd prize: ₹8,000
  • 3rd prize: ₹5,000
  • Next 7 participants: ₹3,000 each
  • E-certificate of participation for all participants

Winners are selected by an automated random draw from a pool of entries that answer everything correctly within the time limit. The page does not say how the draw assigns the prize tiers. Winners are announced at mygov.in. Entering does not guarantee a prize.

A careful checklist

  1. Open quiz.mygov.in and find the quiz yourself.
  2. Read the full Terms and Conditions on the page.
  3. Revise the basics above: unique passwords, MFA, spotting phishing and where to report incidents.
  4. Start when you have 5 uninterrupted minutes and finish before 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

We did not log in or play. We read the prizes and all the rules on the quiz page.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an entry fee?

The page does not mention one.

Does a better score get a bigger prize?

Not necessarily. Winners come from a random draw among entries with all correct answers. The page does not explain how tiers are assigned.
