Kerala Employment Exchange: check your seniority list online

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

Registered with a Kerala Employment Exchange? The official public portal lets you look up seniority-list categories by district, exchange, list type and year. Checking the list early can help you spot a problem before your own exchange's objection deadline.

Quick facts

  • Where: the Kerala Employment Directorate public seniority-list page, eemployment.kerala.gov.in/pub/publicSeniorityList/seniority_list.
  • You choose a district, the exchange where you are registered, Provisional or Final, and the year range. The registration-number field is marked optional, and the page asks for a CAPTCHA.
  • A seniority list is not a vacancy notice, a job offer or a promise of appointment.
  • Deadlines are local. A Thalavady Grama Panchayat notice dated 23 October 2026 says Kuttanad Town Employment Exchange published provisional lists for 2027-2029 and asks for objections or complaints by 18 November 2026. That is one exchange, not a Kerala-wide deadline.
  • Checked by AiCoN: on 9 November 2026 we ran the public category search for Kuttanad, 2027-2029, Provisional, with the registration number blank. It returned 40 draft categories, with different cutoff dates per category. We opened no list contents.

How do you find your list?

  1. Open the official seniority-list page and check that the address is eemployment.kerala.gov.in.
  2. Select your district and the Employment Exchange where you are registered.
  3. Choose Provisional or Final, then the available year range.
  4. If you use the optional registration-number field, enter only your own number. Do not post your number or a screenshot of your own list in public.
  5. Enter the CAPTCHA and choose Get Details.
  6. Match the category, age group, list type and cutoff to your own case. Each category has a View control. Use it only for the information that concerns you.

The results table labels a column SeniorityUpto. That is a seniority cutoff, not an objection deadline. For the deadline, use an official notice or ask your exchange.

Provisional or final list: where do complaints go?

The portal's own disclaimer says that for a provisional list you should use the appeal option in the seniority-list view. For a final list it tells you to report at the Employment Exchange with your original certificates and ID card. This is the portal's wording. We did not check what an online appeal asks for, or whether a particular complaint would be accepted.

Do not send original documents or personal details to an unofficial social media account.

What if the search shows nothing useful?

Recheck the district, exchange, list type, year and CAPTCHA. A category table does not confirm that you are on the list. A missing or unexpected result is a reason to ask your exchange, not proof that your registration was cancelled. This guide is not about special renewal and gives no renewal date.

Limits of this guide

  • We did not check that any person is included in a list, and we did not open list contents, log in or use the appeal option.
  • We checked one exchange (Kuttanad). Other exchanges can look different or have different dates.
  • We did not check complaint costs, if any, or any appeal submission.
  • We did not verify a Kerala-wide deadline. Confirm yours with your exchange.

Sources: Kerala Employment Directorate: public seniority list search · Thalavady Grama Panchayat notice, 23 October 2026. Explainer by AiCoN - not a government publication. Checked 9 November 2026. Details can change; check the official source before you act.
