INDIA HEALTH SERVICE

Check reported blood stock. Confirm before travelling.

By AiCoN | Published 30 October 2026 | Checked 30 October 2026

When a hospital asks a family to help locate blood, random forwards can waste time. The government's eRaktKosh website lets you look up reported stock by state, district, blood group and component. We completed a Kerala search and narrowed its results by town. Use the results to make informed calls, not as a promise that a unit is available or reserved.

Quick facts

  • Useful for: Indian families and hospital teams locating blood-centre contact information and reported stock.
  • Tested: Kerala, Malappuram, O+Ve, Packed Red Blood Cells, then the result table's Manjeri text filter.
  • The completed result showed 18 entries; the text filter showed 2. These are test observations from 30 October 2026, not a live stock bulletin.
  • No patient details, login, Aadhaar, OTP, card or payment were requested in this public lookup.
  • Not tested: a phone call, unit reservation, request, collection, payment, donor registration, mobile app or GPS directions.

Start with the hospital's requirement

Ask the treating team for the required blood group and component before searching. This guide demonstrates a website, not a recommendation to use O-positive blood or packed red cells for any patient. Do not choose a different group or component because a website says another option is available. Compatibility and clinical need are for the treating team and blood centre to decide.

If care is urgent, keep working with the treating hospital. Do not delay treatment while repeatedly refreshing a website or travelling to an unconfirmed listing.

How to run the public search

  1. Open the official Blood Stock Availability page.
  2. Select your state and district. We used Kerala and Malappuram.
  3. Select the group and component specified by the treating team. Our demonstration used O+Ve and Packed Red Blood Cells.
  4. Select Search and wait for Loading to disappear and the result rows to populate.
  5. Read each row's centre name, contact details, Availability, Last Updated and Type together.
  6. If helpful, type a town or centre name in the result table's Search box. This filters the existing table; it is not a new clinical match or a nearest-centre calculation.
  7. Call the blood centre and coordinate with the treating hospital before travelling or making any arrangement.

The last step is recommended follow-through, not a call we made. We did not submit a request or arrange any blood product.

What the test actually showed

Our selected filters returned 18 entries. The first page included both reported availability and Not Available messages, plus different Last Updated values. The initial Loading screen also temporarily showed zero entries. We waited for it to finish rather than treating the temporary zero as proof that no stock existed.

Typing Manjeri into the table's Search box narrowed the display to two entries and showed the status line filtered from 18 total entries. One row displayed an available quantity, while another displayed Not Available for the selected component. We are not reproducing those quantities as current advice or endorsing either centre.

Last Updated matters more than the page footer

The official FAQ directs users to the Last Updated date or LIVE status when checking stock. In our result, each row had its own timestamp. A date in the site's footer is not proof that every centre's inventory was refreshed then.

The new portal homepage calls this real-time blood availability. Our check establishes that the public search returned reported records. We did not independently verify stock, update latency or completeness. Call even when a row looks recent, since demand and reservations can change availability.

Read the Type column too. Our result included both Blood Bank and BSU labels. Similar centre names appeared as separate records with different types and contacts. Do not combine those rows or assume one status applies to all records under a hospital name.

What to ask when you call

  • Confirm the centre identity and the exact group and component requested by the treating team.
  • Ask whether the required quantity can actually be supplied now and what the hospital must arrange.
  • Ask what documentation or request from the hospital is needed, where to report and when.
  • Confirm any applicable processing charges or conditions directly. We did not verify a tariff or entitlement to free supply.

No call was made in this test, so the guide does not promise that a listed phone number will be answered. If a record is incomplete, work through the treating hospital rather than assuming a blank result means there is no possible supply anywhere.

Why use this instead of an AI-generated list?

The useful feature is a structured official-source search with component filters, contact fields and row-level update timestamps. A general chatbot answer or an old social-media appeal cannot establish current stock. eRaktKosh gives a better starting point for verification, while the final confirmation still comes from the blood centre and treating hospital.

The National Government Services Portal lists eRaktKosh as a blood-stock information service. C-DAC's March 2025 update describes its repository's use in blood-bank discovery through the Unified Health Interface. An independent March 2025 Hindustan Times report describes Maharashtra's blood-bank integration. Neither establishes that every Kerala centre is covered or that today's quantities are independently checked.

Privacy, costs and limits

Our public desktop lookup used no patient information or account, and no Google sign-in. It asked for no payment. This is not a claim that blood products, processing or related hospital services are free.

The provider's privacy policy says it collects basic technical usage information, including IP address, browser and pages visited. No login required is not the same as no data collection. Do not put patient identifiers, medical records or credentials in a general table search.

We tested the public stock page linked here, not the separate beta interface, mobile app, GPS search, donations or reservations. The filtered page's URL did not encode our selections, so opening the link does not promise the same filtered result. Choose your own filters again.
