Wikivoyage: a free worldwide travel guide

By AiCoN | Published 17 October 2026 | Sources checked 17 October 2026

Wikivoyage is a free worldwide travel guide that anyone can edit, hosted by the Wikimedia Foundation. It is volunteer-written, so double-check key details.

What it is

Wikivoyage is a travel guide website run by volunteers. Its main page calls it "The free worldwide travel guide that you can edit" and "the official, non-commercial sister site of Wikipedia for sightseeing, activities, cuisine and accommodation around the world". It is hosted by the Wikimedia Foundation, a non-profit. It is not an AI tool. We included it as a free way to research a trip before you spend money on guidebooks.

Is it free?

Yes, per the site. The main page and the About page both describe it as a free worldwide travel guide. The About page says the content is open and released under a Creative Commons licence. We saw no price, plan or sign-in needed to read.

What you can do with it

  • Read destination guides. The main page says the English edition had 34,760 articles when we read it. The page is live and the count changes.
  • Get current info. The About page says guides are edited continuously, and that vandalism is usually reverted within minutes.
  • Use it in many languages. The About page calls it multilingual.
  • Edit. Anyone can edit an article, including anonymously, per the About page.

What to watch out for

  • Volunteer-written. The About page says authors write without pay. Quality can vary between places.
  • No source requirement. The About page says Wikivoyage writers are not bound to provide sources the way Wikipedia is. Check opening hours, prices and entry rules with the place or an official site before you go.
  • Not tested. We did not read a full guide or check a specific destination.

Privacy and data

  • The Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy says you can read, edit or use any Wikimedia site without registering an account.
  • It says you can register an account without giving an email address or real name.
  • It says the Foundation collects some information when you use the sites, make public contributions, register, or contact them.
  • Edits to articles are public.

India

  • It is free, so no rupee price is involved. The pages mention no country limits. We did not check how complete the guides for Indian destinations are.
