Infocepts Innovate for Impact Scholarship 2026-27: up to ₹50,000 a year, apply by 30 October

By AiCoN | Published 25 October 2026 | Sources checked 25 October 2026

The Infocepts Innovate for Impact Scholarship 2026-27, a CSR initiative of Infocepts Foundation, pays up to ₹50,000 per academic year for a maximum of 4 years to undergraduate students studying computer engineering, IT, AI, machine learning or data science and analytics. It is open to students in any year of the course who are studying in, or domiciled in, Nagpur, Pune, Chennai or Bengaluru, with at least 60% in Class 10 and Class 12 and an annual family income of ₹6 lakh or less. Applications are through Buddy4Study and close on 30 October 2026. Infocepts confirmed the current call, including the deadline and the award, on its own website on 12 October 2026.

At a glance

  • Amount: up to ₹50,000 per academic year, renewable for a maximum of 4 years
  • Who: undergraduate students in any year of computer engineering, IT, AI, machine learning, or data science and analytics
  • Marks: at least 60% in Class 10 and in Class 12
  • Income: annual family income of ₹6 lakh or less
  • Location: studying in, or domiciled in, Nagpur, Pune, Chennai or Bengaluru
  • Deadline: 30 October 2026
  • Where: the scholarship listing on buddy4study.com, the programme's application partner; no application fee

Eligibility

  • Enrolled in any year of an undergraduate engineering course in Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or Data Science and Analytics
  • At least 60% in Class 10 and at least 60% in Class 12
  • Studying in, or domiciled in, Nagpur, Pune, Chennai or Bengaluru
  • Annual family income of ₹6 lakh or less
  • Children of employees of Infocepts Foundation and Buddy4Study are not eligible

The scholarship can be renewed for up to 4 years in total; to keep it, you need to maintain at least 60% marks. Infocepts Foundation says it can change the programme terms, so re-check the listing before you apply.

How to apply

Checked on the live pages on 25 October 2026: Infocepts' official blog confirms the call, and the Buddy4Study listing shows Apply Now with the deadline 30-Oct-2026. We did not register on Buddy4Study, so the steps below describe the route the official pages present, not a completed test. Buddy4Study registration asks for a mobile number, which we did not provide.

  1. Open the Infocepts Innovate for Impact Scholarship listing on Buddy4Study: Infocepts Innovate for Impact Scholarship 2026-27 on Buddy4Study. Infocepts' own step-by-step guide at infocepts.ai describes the same route.
  2. Choose Apply Now. You will be asked to register or log in on Buddy4Study with your mobile number and email.
  3. Fill the application form with your personal, academic and family income details, exactly as your documents show them.
  4. Upload the documents the form asks for (full list below) and review every detail before final submission.
  5. Submit before 30 October 2026. Save a copy or screenshot of the submitted form for your records.

Documents the application asks for

  • Passport-size photograph
  • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets
  • College admission proof (admission letter or bonafide certificate)
  • Identity proof (Aadhaar card, driving licence or PAN card)
  • Address proof
  • Education expense receipts (fee receipts)
  • Family income proof (ITR, salary slips or an income certificate)

We read the public listing and Infocepts' official guide on 25 October 2026, including the eligibility, award and documents sections. We did not create an account, so any declaration steps inside the form itself were not seen.

Selection

Infocepts Foundation shortlists applications, verifies documents and holds a telephonic interview before awarding the scholarship. Meeting the eligibility criteria does not guarantee selection.

Stay safe

  • There is no fee to apply. Buddy4Study itself runs a scholarship-fraud alert on the listing page: never pay anyone who promises selection.
  • Apply only through the Buddy4Study listing, linked from Infocepts' official pages. Lookalike pages and agents are common around popular scholarships.
  • The scholarship is selection-based; treat "guaranteed scholarship" claims as fraud.

Frequently asked questions

Can a first-year student apply? Yes. The scholarship is open to students in any year of the listed undergraduate courses.

I study in Kochi but my home is in Pune. Can I apply? Yes. The rule is studying in, or domiciled in, Nagpur, Pune, Chennai or Bengaluru. Domicile in one of the four cities is enough.

Is 60% needed in both Class 10 and Class 12? Yes. The listing requires at least 60% in each of Class 10 and Class 12.

Is the ₹50,000 guaranteed? No. Up to ₹50,000 per academic year is the maximum award, for a maximum of 4 years. The actual award and renewal depend on selection and on maintaining 60% marks.

Is this a government scholarship? No. It is a corporate CSR scholarship from Infocepts Foundation, with Buddy4Study as the application partner.
