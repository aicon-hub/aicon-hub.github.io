KERALA WELFARE PENSION

Kerala welfare pensions: ₹2,000 a month, who can apply and how

By AiCoN | Published 28 October 2026 | Sources checked 28 October 2026

Kerala's social security (welfare) pensions now pay ₹2,000 a month. The Sevana Pension portal of the Local Self Government Department lists seven pension schemes at this rate, and its own amount-history table shows the rise from ₹1,600 to ₹2,000 took effect on 1 November 2025. The named schemes cover the elderly, widows, persons with disabilities, agricultural labourers and unmarried women above 50. Applications go through your grama panchayat, municipality or corporation - this is a local-body service, not an online-only one.

Quick facts

  • Monthly amount: the Sevana Pension portal's criteria table lists all seven pension rows at ₹2,000 a month. Its amount-history table for the Agricultural Labour Pension shows the rate moved from ₹1,600 to ₹2,000 with effect from 1 November 2025.
  • The schemes: the portal's application forms page names five pensions - Agricultural Labour Pension, Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension, Indira Gandhi National Disabled Pension for the mentally/physically challenged, Pension for Unmarried Women above 50 years, and Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension. The criteria table shows seven rows at ₹2,000; two row names render blank on the current page, so confirm the full list with your local body.
  • Who qualifies (example - Agricultural Labour Pension): per the portal's published criteria - annual family income not above ₹1 lakh; age 60 or above; at least 10 years' continuous residence in Kerala; not an income-tax payer; not a service or family pensioner (ex-gratia or NPS pension up to ₹4,000 is excluded); worked as an agricultural labourer for at least 10 years; family land not above two acres (Scheduled Tribe applicants exempted); house not above 2,000 sq ft. Other pensions have their own criteria on the same page.
  • One person, limited pensions: the criteria say recipients of another social welfare pension cannot apply, except disability pension - a maximum of two pensions including EPF.
  • Special-amount caveat: the page also lists "special amounts" of ₹1,500 (old age pension, 75 years above) and ₹1,100 (disability pension, 80% and above) that look like older figures next to the ₹2,000 base rate. We could not confirm from the page how these apply now - ask your local body office before counting on a specific amount.
  • Site note: the portal's homepage currently shows a maintenance notice, but its inner pages - criteria, forms, pension search - open directly (links below).

How to apply

  1. Get the application form: download it from the portal's Download - Application Forms page (links below) or collect it from your grama panchayat, municipality or corporation office.
  2. Fill the form for the pension that fits - old age, widow, disability, agricultural labour, or unmarried women above 50 - and attach the documents the form asks for.
  3. Submit it to the local body where you permanently reside. The criteria say you cannot be an applicant or beneficiary in two different local bodies.
  4. After applying, track status on the same portal: Pension Search, Pension Dashboard and DBT (direct benefit transfer) pages are public.

If you or a family member may qualify - especially an elderly parent drawing the older ₹1,600 rate - the local body office can confirm the current amount and any arrears position.
