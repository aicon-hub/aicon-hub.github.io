IndiaSkills 2026-27: registration reopened till 19 October, 63 skills from age 16

By AiCoN | Published 17 October 2026 | Sources checked 17 October 2026

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE) has reopened registration for the IndiaSkills Competition 2026-27 till 19 October 2026, after requests from institutions and aspirants. IndiaSkills is India's national skill championship, run by MSDE with NSDC as the implementation partner, across 63 skills. Registration is on the Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH).

Good to knowRegistration closes 19 October 2026, so this is a 2-day window. The PIB announcement lists no cash prize; the draw is medals at each level and the chance to represent India at WorldSkills. Each skill has its own age cut-off, so check your skill before you register. The SIDH page loads slowly and needs sign-in to see the form.

What is IndiaSkills?

It is the country's biggest skill competition, covering 63 industry-relevant trades: manufacturing, engineering, construction, digital technologies, creative industries and services. You compete through district or zonal rounds, then state, regional and national rounds. National-level winners can progress towards representing India at international skill competitions. About 4 lakh people have already registered for 2026-27, per the ministry.

Who can register?

  • Open to Indian nationals; minimum age 16 years
  • For most skills: born on or after 1 January 2006
  • For 13 skills - including Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, Mechatronics, Industry 4.0, Additive Manufacturing, Digital Construction and Aircraft Maintenance - born on or after 1 January 2003
  • No formal qualification is needed; both individual and team skills exist
  • Registration on SIDH uses Aadhaar-based e-KYC

Why the buzz now?

Team India finished 10th at WorldSkills Shanghai 2026 with six silver medals and 20 medallions for excellence, its best result yet. The Prime Minister met the team on 9 October 2026, and the ministry says the reopened window follows renewed demand after that.

How to register: step by step

These steps follow the official IndiaSkills 2026-27 participant user manual and the live SIDH pages we opened on 19 October 2026.

  1. Open skillindiadigital.gov.in/india-skills-competition (or skillindiadigital.gov.in/home). The link takes you straight to the Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH) registration screen.
  2. Select Learner/Participant, type your 10-digit mobile number, tick the Terms & Conditions box, and tap CONTINUE.
  3. Enter the OTP sent to that mobile number.
  4. First-time users: set a 4-digit account password (numbers only), confirm it, and tap CREATE. You will use this password for later logins.
  5. Complete Aadhaar e-KYC: enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and verify it with the OTP method. The manual recommends OTP e-KYC for this competition, and e-KYC is mandatory for first-time users.
  6. After e-KYC, your SIDH account is ready. Open the IndiaSkills banner on the home page, or tap the profile icon and choose My Dashboard, then select IndiaSkills Competition 2026-27.
  7. Choose Individual Participation and tap Next. (Team skills have a separate team flow.)
  8. Check your skill's age rule before you fill the form: most of the 63 skills need you born on or after 1 January 2006; 13 skills (including Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, Mechatronics, Industry 4.0, Additive Manufacturing, Digital Construction, ICT Network Infrastructure, Industrial Design Technology, Optoelectronic Technology, Robot Systems Integration, Water Technology, Dental Prosthetics and Aircraft Maintenance) allow born on or after 1 January 2003.
  9. Fill the form's three sections in order - Personal Details, then Address Details, then Education Qualification/Employment - checking the entries before tapping Next on each.
  10. Tick the declaration confirming the state you are participating from (your current state of residence), tap CONFIRM, and submit before 19 October 2026.
