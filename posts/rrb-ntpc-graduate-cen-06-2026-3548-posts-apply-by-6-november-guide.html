RRB NTPC graduate 2026: 3,548 posts, apply by 6 November

By AiCoN | Published 5 November 2026 | Sources checked 5 November 2026

Indian Railways has invited applications for 3,548 graduate-level NTPC posts under CEN 06/2026. The Press Information Bureau says online applications close on 6 November 2026 at 11:59 PM. That is the last day or close to it, so check today.

What we checked, and what we did notThe facts below come from the PIB release of 11 October 2026. The RRB application portal (rrbapply.gov.in) showed "Server Under Maintenance" when we opened it on 4 and 5 November, and we could not open the notification PDF. So we have not read the notice itself. Nothing was submitted or paid.

Key facts (PIB, 11 October 2026)

  • Notice: Centralised Employment Notification (CEN) No. 06/2026, Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC), graduate level.
  • Vacancies: 3,548 in four categories. Goods Train Manager 2,750, Senior Clerk cum Typist 371, Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist 300, Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor 127.
  • Initial basic pay under the Seventh Central Pay Commission: ₹35,400 a month (Pay Level 6) for Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor, and ₹29,200 a month (Pay Level 5) for the other three. Allowances are extra.
  • Who can apply: a degree from a recognised university or equivalent, plus the post-specific conditions. Age 18 to 33 on 1 January 2027, with relaxations for eligible categories.
  • Dates: applications opened 8 October 2026 and close on 6 November 2026 at 11:59 PM. The last date to pay the exam fee is 8 November 2026. The application modification window runs from 9 to 18 November 2026.
  • Selection: first-stage CBT, second-stage CBT, document verification and medical examination, as applicable. The typing skill test is not held for Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist and Senior Clerk cum Typist.

Where to read and apply

PIB points to the RRB website rrb.indianrailways.gov.in for the detailed notification and vacancy list, and to rrbapply.gov.in for the online application. Use only these official addresses. Nobody can reserve a form for you for money.

Not confirmed by AiCoN

  • The exam fee amount and the refund rules. PIB mentions refund provisions but does not give the amounts.
  • The number of posts at each RRB, including any Kerala board.
  • Post-specific eligibility. Read it in the notification.
