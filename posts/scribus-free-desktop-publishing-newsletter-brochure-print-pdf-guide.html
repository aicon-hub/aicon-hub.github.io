Scribus: free desktop publishing for newsletters, brochures and print PDFs

By AiCoN | Published 18 October 2026 | Sources checked 18 October 2026

Scribus is a page layout program for newsletters, brochures and print-ready PDFs. Its home page says it is Free, with a capital F, and we saw no price.

What it is

Scribus is a page layout program, also called desktop publishing. Its website calls it "the Go-To Libre Desktop Publishing Application". You place text, pictures and shapes on pages to make things like newsletters, brochures, posters, magazines and print-ready PDFs. The About page says it runs on Linux, Windows, macOS and several other systems. It is not an AI tool. It is a more serious tool than a simple design app, and its site does not describe it as a quick one.

Is it free?

Yes, as far as the site says. The home page says: "Scribus is reliable and Free. Free really means Free with an uppercase F. Free is more than just gratis (which is just a side-effect)." We saw no price on the pages we read. The downloads page links to installers on SourceForge and says nothing about paying. We did not find a licence name on the pages we read, so we do not state one.

What it says it can do

  • Open files. The site says Scribus files use an XML-based format that can be read in a simple text editor, so damaged files can be repaired more easily.
  • Colour. The site says it ships with almost 200 colour palettes, supports RGB, CMYK and spot colours, and can emulate colour blindness so you can see how your choices look to others.
  • PDF for printing. The site says it was the first desktop publishing program to support PDF/X-3, and from version 1.6.0 exports PDF/X-1a:2001 and PDF/X-4. It also makes PDFs for screens and forms.
  • Scripts and languages. It says it can be automated with Python, and supports many writing systems under Unicode.

How to get it

  1. Open the project's downloads page.
  2. The page says the current stable version is 1.6.6 and the development version is 1.7.3. It recommends stable for almost all production work.
  3. Pick the Windows or macOS installer. Links go to SourceForge, which the project's own page points to. The page lists macOS 12 or higher for Intel and macOS 13 or higher for Apple Silicon, and Windows 7 to 11.

A Scribus 1.6.x quick tour was added to the site on 11 September 2026, per the site. We did not read it.

What to watch out for

  • Not a quick drag-and-drop designer. It is built for print layout. If you only want a social post, a simpler tool may suit you better. We did not compare them.
  • Third-party download host. The installers are on SourceForge. Use the links on the Scribus downloads page only.
  • Templates. We did not look for ready-made templates on the site, so we do not say whether they exist.

Privacy and data

  • The site's privacy policy covers the website, not the program. It says user accounts and comments are not allowed on www.scribus.net, and the only cookies in use should be functional ones.
  • We found no statement about what the Scribus program itself sends over the internet, so we cannot say.

India

  • We saw no region limit and no price. We did not test downloads from India.

Yes, per its home page. We saw no price.

Does it work on Windows and Mac?

Yes, per the downloads page, with the versions listed above.
