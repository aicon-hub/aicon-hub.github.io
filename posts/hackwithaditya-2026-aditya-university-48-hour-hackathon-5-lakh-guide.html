HackWithAditya 2026: Rs 5 lakh prize pool, free Level 1 entry for student teams

By AiCoN | Published 22 October 2026 | Sources checked 22 October 2026

HackWithAditya 2026 is a national 48-hour residential hackathon by Aditya University, Andhra Pradesh, in association with Oracle Academy and in collaboration with the OpenAI Student Collective. Student teams of 4 to 6 members submit an existing working prototype for a free online screening by 25 November 2026; shortlisted teams build on campus from 18 to 20 December 2026 for a total cash prize pool of Rs 5 lakh.

Banner: official HackWithAditya website (hackathon.adityauniversity.in), not an AiCoN image.

How it works: two levels

  • Level 1 - prototype screening (free): register your team and submit an existing working prototype online. No card, no fee. Reviewers score problem relevance, prototype credibility, feasibility, clarity and originality, and may call teams for a short ownership viva with commit-history and similarity checks.
  • Level 2 - the 48-hour enhancement challenge: shortlisted teams are invited to Aditya University for an on-site 48-hour build with two mandatory enhancement tasks plus one optional stretch goal, judged live by a technical jury. Level 2 costs Rs 800 per member.

Who can take part

The university FAQ specifies currently enrolled college or university students in India, including mixed-college teams. Teams have 4 to 6 members; the team lead registers everyone at once. Participants must be above the legal age of majority in their country of residence. Companies and professional organisations cannot participate, per the Devpost rules.

Themes

Every team registers under one of eight themes: AI for Bharat; AgriTech and Aquaculture; HealthTech and Bio-Innovation; Energy, EV and Sustainability; Robotics, IoT and Smart Manufacturing; Smart Infrastructure and Water; Cybersecurity, FinTech and Digital Trust; or Open Innovation for ideas that fit none of the seven.

Prizes

  • Total cash prize pool: Rs 5,00,000 across six judged categories.
  • Categories: Overall Winners, Strongest Engineering, Best User Impact, Hardware Innovation, Best Women-Led Team, and Community Choice voted by fellow participants.
  • Separate fun awards (Best Team Name, Night Owl, Best Bug Story, Team Spirit, Best Duck). Final judging runs 18-20 December with a top-8 finalist showcase and four-minute demos.

Key dates (all times IST)

  • Level 1 registration and prototype submission: open now, closes 25 November 2026 (the official site says 11:59 pm; the Devpost listing shows 11:45 pm - submit well before the evening to be safe).
  • Screening and ownership viva: 26 to 30 November 2026.
  • Shortlist announced: 1 December 2026.
  • Level 2 payment and documents: by 9 December 2026, 11:59 pm.
  • Optional on-site preparation sessions: 12 to 16 December 2026.
  • Prototype baseline freeze: 17 December 2026.
  • The 48-hour event: 18 to 20 December 2026 at Aditya University - check-in starts at 9 am on 18 December; opening ceremony is 3:30 pm and the build launches at 5 pm. Coding ends at 5 pm on 20 December, followed by the top-8 showcase at 6:15 pm, dinner at 7:40 pm and farewell at 8:40 pm.

What the Rs 800 Level 2 fee covers

Three days of hostel stay and food on campus, a HackWithAditya t-shirt and snacks, a participation certificate for every Level 2 builder, mentors, startup meetings and a possible 30-day pilot. Shuttle between Samalkota and the campus is free.

How to register

Start from the official site hackathon.adityauniversity.in and use "Register free". On 24 October this opens the university's own Level 1 registration form, not Devpost. The public form has team/theme, team lead, members, project and review steps. The team lead registers the whole team before the 25 November deadline. We viewed the first public form screen but did not enter team data or test submission. The Devpost listing remains a secondary event listing, with its earlier 11:45 pm cutoff still differing from the university's 11:59 pm.

The official rules ask for a working prototype, setup steps, features and limitations, and a demo video under five minutes. AI coding assistants and APIs are allowed if disclosed, but the team must explain the code. All members must be on site; outside people or paid services building during the event lead to disqualification.
