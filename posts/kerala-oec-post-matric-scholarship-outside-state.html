Kerala OEC outside-state scholarship: 30 November notice, current entry unverified

By AiCoN | 6 October 2026 | 6 min read

Scoped recheck 24 October 2026: separated OEC no-income-limit wording from the annexure's ₹6 lakh ceiling for 30 OBC(H) communities. Current fetched announcement retains 30 November, but actual homepage/Settings Notice Boards were empty and login/registration entry was not exposed. Full current notification and institution list not recovered. Old application screenshots removed so they cannot imply current access. No account or application tested.

The fetched official e-Grantz announcement names 30 November 2026 for OEC/OBC(H) post-matric students in institutions of national importance outside Kerala. However, the actual public homepage and Settings page showed an empty Notice Board on 24 October, with no working registration/login entry exposed there. We could not verify an open application route or recover the full cycle-specific notification. Confirm the route and your institution with BCDD before uploading records; do not treat the displayed deadline as proof that applications can currently be submitted.

What is it?

The OEC Post-Matric Educational Assistance scheme gives educational scholarship to OEC (Other Eligible Communities) students from Kerala. The Backward Classes Development Department (BCDD) page says it covers OEC post-matric students in the state and those who got admission to courses outside the state, including allotment from central agencies. The scheme covers lump sum grant, monthly stipend and legitimate fees for students from +2 to PhD.

The e-Grantz notice (in Malayalam) invites applications from OEC and OBC(H) post-matric students studying in institutions of national importance outside the state. The last date is 30.11.2026.

Who can apply?

  • The student must be in the Kerala State OEC list and have got admission through merit or reservation (BCDD page).
  • The BCDD general scheme page says no income limit for OEC students. Do not apply that statement to every OBC(H) applicant: Annexure 1 in the linked e-Grantz document lists 30 OBC(H) communities with an annual family-income ceiling of ₹6 lakh under G.O.(Ms)10/2014/BCDD dated 23 May 2014. Confirm the current cycle-specific conditions with BCDD.
  • The student studies from +2 to PhD level, in a course covered by the scheme.
  • For the outside-state notice, the institution must be one of national importance, not simply any college outside Kerala. The full current institution list was not recovered in this recheck.

The BCDD page also says to apply within two months after admission. The fetched 2026-27 announcement says 30 November 2026; the full current notification and entry remain unverified. Confirm the route promptly rather than waiting for the last day.

Application route: current limitation

On 24 October, the actual public homepage and Settings page loaded, but their Notice Board was empty and the LOGIN navigation pointed to a missing page section. The earlier 19 October screenshots are not evidence that registration or submission is open now. We removed them from this current route guide.

  1. Start at the official e-Grantz portal and ask your institution or BCDD to confirm the current outside-state scheme route, institution eligibility, documents and processing timetable. Do not use an unofficial substitute link if a button is missing.
  2. The portal's general description says students first register their name, address, date of birth, gender, mobile number, bank details and Aadhaar, then use that registration for schemes. It also says institutions can enter applications if students cannot apply themselves. These are general instructions, not a tested current outside-state form.
  3. The visible "How to get your OTR No" link opens an NSP video. Do not assume this is the local e-Grantz One Time Registration form or a substitute for the scheme's confirmed route.
  4. Once the correct official route is confirmed, check the current document list before uploading identity, community, income, bank or admission records. Keep an application reference after any successful submission. No registration, consent, login, tracking lookup, upload or submission was tested in this recheck.
  5. The general portal says assistance reaches the student's bank account through DBT. Confirm the scheme's bank/Aadhaar requirements with the institution before changing records. No account or uploaded-record deletion route was verified.

Stay safe

  • Apply only on egrantz.kerala.gov.in. No agent can speed it up.
  • Do not pay anyone to fill the form.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date?

The fetched announcement says 30 November 2026, but the actual current application entry was not verified. Confirm with BCDD.

Is there an income limit?

For OEC, the general BCDD page says no limit. The annexure lists a ₹6 lakh annual family-income ceiling for its 30 OBC(H) communities. Confirm current cycle conditions; do not merge the two categories.

Who is OEC?

Students in the State OEC list. Check your community against the list.

How is the money paid?

Directly to your bank account.

Is Kedavilakku the same scheme?

No. Kedavilakku is a different scheme, and its last date was 30 September 2026, which has passed.
