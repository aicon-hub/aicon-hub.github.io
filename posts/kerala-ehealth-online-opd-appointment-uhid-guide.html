Book Kerala government hospital OPD appointments online: eHealth Kerala UHID guide

By AiCoN | Published 30 October 2026 | Sources checked 30 October 2026

Booking a government hospital outpatient visit online starts with a Unique Health ID. eHealth Kerala - the State Digital Health Mission, a Government of India-funded project run with Kerala's Health and Family Welfare Department - lets you register once with Aadhaar to get a permanent Unique Health ID (UHID), then book outpatient appointments at e-Health enabled government hospitals online. We checked the official site and opened its login page on 30 October 2026; we did not log in, register or book.

Quick facts

  • One ID for life: per the official site, registration is Aadhaar-based and creates a permanent Unique Health ID - one citizen, one health record - usable lifelong at government hospitals.
  • The citizen portal: ehealth.kerala.gov.in/portal handles login and registration. You can sign in with your mobile number (OTP) or your UHID; first-time users tap "Generate / Reset Password" to get a default password, then change it after the first login.
  • Online appointments: the mission states citizens can book online appointments at e-Health enabled government hospitals through the citizen portal, and a hospital search page lists facilities district by district.
  • Telemedicine: review consultations with doctors are available virtually through the M-eHealth mobile app, starting as a pilot.
  • Also on the site: dashboards, the vaccination portal link, ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) section and an Aadhaar grievance page.
  • Outside Kerala's network: the national Online Registration System at ors.gov.in (NIC, Digital India) offers OPD appointments, lab reports and blood availability at central and other states' hospitals, integrates with ABHA.

How to book

  1. Open ehealth.kerala.gov.in and go to the citizen portal. Per the official site, new users register using Aadhaar to get a permanent UHID; users who already have a UHID sign in to take an appointment.
  2. On the citizen portal login page, which is the page we opened, you can use a mobile number (Send OTP) or a UHID. First-time users are told to use "Generate / Reset Password" first.
  3. Per the official site, after login you can book an online appointment at e-Health enabled government hospitals through the citizen portal (not tested by us; we only opened the login page).

If your hospital is not on e-Health yet, try the national portal ors.gov.in - or call the hospital directly.
