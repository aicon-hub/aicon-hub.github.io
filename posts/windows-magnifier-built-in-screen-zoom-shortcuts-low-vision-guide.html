Windows Magnifier: built-in screen zoom, shortcuts and views

By AiCoN | Published 19 October 2026 | Sources checked 19 October 2026

Magnifier zooms part or all of your Windows screen. Microsoft lists shortcuts for full screen, lens and docked views, plus inverted colours.

What it is

Magnifier is a screen zoom tool in Windows. Microsoft's support page says it "makes part or all of your screen bigger so you can see words and images better." It helps people with low vision, and anyone who wants to read small text or look closely at a part of the screen. It is not an AI tool.

Is it free?

No Microsoft page we read says "free" or shows a price for Magnifier. The support page describes it as part of Windows 10 and 11 and tells you to turn it on from Settings, with no separate download. That means you need a copy of Windows, and we did not look at what Windows itself costs.

How to use it, per Microsoft

  1. Turn on: press the Windows logo key and the plus sign (+). Turn it off with the Windows logo key and Esc. Or go to Start, Settings, Accessibility, Magnifier, and switch it on.
  2. Zoom level: in Magnifier settings, choose how much it enlarges the screen and the zoom increments. Microsoft lists the Windows key plus Minus to zoom out, and Ctrl+Alt with the mouse wheel to zoom with the wheel.
  3. Pick a view: Ctrl+Alt+F for full screen, Ctrl+Alt+D for docked, Ctrl+Alt+L for lens. Ctrl+Alt+M cycles between them.
  4. Invert colours: Ctrl+Alt+I flips black and white, which Microsoft says "may reduce eye strain or make text more readable for some people". Press it again to go back.
  5. Look around: Ctrl+Alt+Spacebar briefly shows the whole screen when you are zoomed in. Ctrl+Alt+arrow keys move the view.

Other settings Microsoft lists

  • Start Magnifier before or after you sign in to Windows, which can help if a computer is often used by someone with low vision.
  • Choose what Magnifier follows: the mouse pointer, keyboard focus, text cursor or Narrator cursor.
  • Smooth the edges of images and text, which Microsoft suggests you try both ways.
  • Touchscreen: tap the plus and minus signs at the screen corners to zoom, and drag along the borders to move around.

What to watch out for

  • Shortcuts use a US keyboard layout. Microsoft says keys on other layouts may not match.
  • Full screen zoom can make it hard to see where you are. Our advice: learn Ctrl+Alt+Spacebar first.
  • Instructions differ a little between Windows 10 and 11. The page covers both. We did not test it.

Privacy and data

  • The Magnifier support page does not say anything about data collection. Windows privacy settings are separate and we did not read them.

India

  • The support page was in English (United States). We saw no region limit. We did not test it, and we quote no price.

Frequently asked questions

Is Magnifier free?

No page we read says so. It is a built-in Windows feature, with no separate download listed.

How do I turn it on fast?

Windows logo key and plus (+). Windows logo key and Esc turns it off.

Microsoft's page covers both.
