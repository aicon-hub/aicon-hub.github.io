LemonSlice CWM-1: interactive avatars, real pricing and free limits

By AiCoN | Published 7 October 2026 | Sources checked 7 October 2026

LemonSlice's Character World Model-1 animates face, body and hands live during a conversation from a single photo. The free browser tier only covers chatting with featured avatars; custom avatars and the API are paid, CWM-1 itself is reserved for Ultra and Enterprise plans, and idle time is billed like talking time.

What is CWM-1?

Character World Model-1 is LemonSlice's interactive avatar model. Instead of playing back recorded clips, it generates every frame of a character live while you talk, from one input image, with no per-character fine-tuning.

A built-in emotion engine decides what the character appears to feel and do, not only what it says. LemonSlice calls it its most controllable avatar model; that is a vendor claim, not an independent ranking.

What the free tier actually includes

The free tier lets you chat with LemonSlice's featured avatars in the browser. It does not include uploading your own photo to build a custom avatar, and it does not include API access.

Treat the free chat as a demo of latency and quality, useful for judging whether the product fits your need before paying.

Current plan prices

The checked pricing page lists monthly tiers at 8, 40, 100, 240 and 960 US dollars. Paying yearly changes the effective monthly rate to 7, 33, 83, 200 and 800 dollars respectively.

CWM-1 is available only on the Ultra and Enterprise plans. Lower tiers run earlier models. If CWM-1 quality is the reason you are paying, confirm the model name on your plan before checkout, not after.

Two billing traps to know

First, idle time is billed the same as talking time. A session left open while the avatar silently listens still consumes credits, so close sessions deliberately rather than leaving a tab running.

Second, unused credits are lost when you cancel. Time any cancellation near the end of a billing period after using what you paid for, and keep a record of the cancellation confirmation.

Languages, privacy and enterprise terms

LemonSlice advertises support for more than 70 languages, which makes the product relevant for Indian language use cases. Quality per language is a vendor claim to test with your own phrases.

Zero data retention and data-residency options are restricted to Enterprise plans. On lower plans, assume your photo and session content are processed under the standard policy and read it before uploading anyone's image.

Consent and impersonation risk

Only animate photos you own or have clear permission to use. A realistic talking avatar of a real person without their consent can deceive viewers and may violate platform rules and law.

The same realism cuts the other way: treat unexpected video calls from familiar faces with suspicion and verify through a second channel before acting on urgent requests.

India price and access checks

Public prices are in US dollars. Your Indian card total will include conversion and possibly tax, and the displayed plan does not establish local support or uptime.

There is no Indian rupee pricing on the checked page. Confirm renewal, cancellation and credit rules in your actual account before committing to a yearly tier.

A careful first test

  1. Open the official site and try a featured-avatar chat free.
  2. Read the pricing page and confirm which plans include CWM-1.
  3. Check the API documentation only if you need integration.
  4. Start monthly rather than yearly while you measure real usage.
  5. Use a photo you have rights to, with a neutral background.
  6. Watch credit consumption during both talking and idle periods.
  7. Test cancellation terms before upgrading.

This guide did not create an account, upload a photo or purchase a plan.

What changed since the original post?

The original post said API access was for paid Enterprise and Ultra customers. The current pricing page confirms that and adds the fuller picture: five paid tiers, yearly discounts, CWM-1 restricted to Ultra and Enterprise, free tier limited to featured-avatar chat, idle billed as talking, and credits forfeited on cancellation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I try CWM-1 free?

You can chat with featured avatars free. Custom avatars and the API require a paid plan, and CWM-1 sits on Ultra and Enterprise.

Do credits roll over if I cancel?

No. The checked terms say unused credits are lost on cancellation.

Is my data kept private on cheap plans?

Zero data retention and residency options are Enterprise-only on the checked page.
