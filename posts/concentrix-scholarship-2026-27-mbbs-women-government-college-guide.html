Concentrix Scholarship 2026-27: Rs 65,000 a year for women MBBS students

By AiCoN | Published 22 October 2026 | Sources checked 22 October 2026

Female students in the first year of MBBS at a government medical college, with annual family income up to Rs 5 lakh, can apply for the Concentrix Scholarship Program 2026-27. Selected scholars receive up to Rs 65,000 per year for the full duration of the MBBS course, paid by direct bank transfer. Applications close on Friday, 30 October 2026.

Who can apply

  • Female students from anywhere in India.
  • Enrolled in the first year of the MBBS program at a government medical college.
  • Completed Class 12.
  • Annual parental income from all sources not more than Rs 5,00,000.
  • Children of employees of Buddy4Study India Foundation or Concentrix Pvt. Ltd. are not eligible.

Preference is given to children from single-parent families or orphans, children from Aspirational Districts, and students with disabilities.

Amount and what it covers

  • Up to Rs 65,000 per year, for the entire duration of the MBBS course.
  • Covers institution fees, examination fees, books, stationery, equipment and other academic costs.
  • Paid by Direct Bank Transfer to the scholar's account.

The scholarship page does not list separate renewal conditions for later years; it states support runs throughout the duration of the course.

Key dates

  • Applications opened: 28 September 2026.
  • Last date to apply: Friday, 30 October 2026.

Documents to keep ready

  • Recent digital colour photograph.
  • Government ID: masked Aadhaar card, Driving Licence or Voter ID.
  • Proof of current enrolment: admission letter, institution ID card or current-year fee receipt.
  • A breakdown of the year's educational expenses: tuition, hostel, mess, books and other expenses.
  • Class 12 marksheet.
  • Income proof: salary slips, Income Tax Returns, or an income certificate from a competent authority such as a Tehsildar or Magistrate.
  • Bank passbook or a cancelled cheque showing account holder name, account number and IFSC.
  • Category proof if applicable: disability certificate, or documents confirming orphan or single-parent status.

How to apply

Apply online through the official scholarship page on Buddy4Study. Log in or create an account with your email, mobile number or Google account, open the Concentrix Scholarship Program 2026-27 application form, fill in your details, upload the documents, preview and submit.

Help and contact

The scholarship page routes queries to the Buddy4Study support team through its contact link on the application page.
