PM SVANidhi: loans for street vendors, step by step

By AiCoN | 6 October 2026 | 6 min read

PM SVANidhi is a government micro-credit scheme for street vendors. You can borrow in stages as you repay on time, with an interest subsidy. These are loans, not grants.

What is PM SVANidhi?

PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendors AtmaNirbhar Nidhi) is run by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. In August 2025 the Union Cabinet extended the lending period until 31 March 2030 and increased loan amounts. PIB says vendors can get collateral-free working capital loans of Rs 15,000, Rs 25,000 and Rs 50,000 in progressive tranches.

Benefits

  • Loan stages: Rs 15,000 first, then Rs 25,000, then Rs 50,000. Timely repayment makes you eligible for the next stage.
  • Interest subsidy of 7 percent a year on timely and early repayment, per PIB.
  • Credit guarantee support, and a UPI-linked RuPay Credit Card up to Rs 30,000 for eligible vendors who have repaid the second-stage loan, per PIB.
  • The portal says vendors from census towns are now eligible too.

Who can apply?

The portal says the scheme works only in States and UTs that have notified rules under the Street Vendors Act, 2014. Vendors usually need a Certificate of Vending or Letter of Recommendation from their urban local body or municipality, or be covered in its survey. The portal lets you check your survey status. Final eligibility is decided under scheme rules.

How to apply, step by step

  1. Check that your Aadhaar-linked mobile number is active.
  2. Open pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in and use the Check Eligibility option, with or without Aadhaar.
  3. Check your survey status with your municipality if you are unsure whether you were covered.
  4. Fill in the loan application online or ask your municipality or a bank for help.
  5. Wait for the digital approval and for the lender to disburse the loan.
  6. Repay on time, digitally if you can, to unlock the next stage and the subsidy.

What changed since the short post?

The August 2025 restructuring increased the first two loan stages to ₹15,000 and ₹25,000; the third stays ₹50,000. The current portal also includes census towns and a credit-card facility for vendors who repaid the second stage.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free money?

No. It is a loan you must repay. Timely repayment earns the subsidy and the next stage.

Do I need a certificate of vending?

It is the usual proof. Ask your municipality about a Letter of Recommendation if you do not have one.

Can I apply without Aadhaar?

The portal has a Without Aadhaar option. Check the details on the portal.
