MyGov video contest on new criminal laws: ₹20,000 prize, 31 October deadline

By AiCoN | Published 7 October 2026 | Sources checked 7 October 2026

The Bureau of Police Research and Development (BPR&D), Ministry of Home Affairs, is running a video contest on MyGov about the new criminal laws. Make a 2-5 minute video introducing new offences under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, and you could win ₹20,000. Entries close 31 October 2026 at 23:45 IST.

Read this before submittingThe contest page is titled "Video Contest on Use of MedLEaPR to speed up the investigation", but the description on the same page invites videos introducing new offences under the new criminal laws for public awareness. MedLEaPR is BPR&D's medico-legal examination and post-mortem reporting system; the page itself does not explain how it connects to the video brief. Read the full Terms and Conditions on the contest page and confirm the current brief before you start filming. This guide does not establish an age limit, team entries or how many entries one person may submit, because the terms do not say.

What is the contest?

BPR&D, under the Ministry of Home Affairs, is hosting the contest on MyGov. The page invites citizens, especially the youth, to make videos that spread awareness of the new criminal laws: the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam. The stated focus is introducing new offences under these laws.

Participation is through the MyGov portal. The Terms and Conditions confirm the contest is open to Indian citizens and there is no entry fee.

Video specifications on the official page

FormatMP4 video file.
Length2 to 5 minutes.
LanguageHindi, English or a regional language.
How to submitUpload the video to YouTube or Google Drive (public link) and share that link on the MyGov contest page.
Content ruleNo provocative, objectionable or inappropriate content, per the Terms and Conditions.

What are the prizes?

  • First prize: ₹20,000
  • Second prize: ₹10,000
  • Third prize: ₹5,000

BPR&D, MHA will disburse the prize amounts. Winners will be announced on the MyGov blog (blog.mygov.in). Participants whose entries are not selected will not be notified. A prize is not guaranteed just for entering.

Which deadline should you use?

The contest page checked on 7 October 2026 shows an end date of Saturday, 31 October 2026 at 23:45 IST, and displays Login to Participate, meaning entries were open when checked. Confirm the live page before uploading, because contest pages and dates can change.

A careful submission checklist

  1. Open the official MyGov contest page yourself and read the full Terms and Conditions PDF linked there.
  2. Confirm the current deadline, timezone and participation status on the live page.
  3. Check your MyGov profile is accurate and updated, since it is used for further communication.
  4. Make your video in MP4, 2-5 minutes, in Hindi, English or a regional language, focused on new offences under the new criminal laws.
  5. Keep the content free of anything provocative, objectionable or inappropriate. Plagiarised, false or erroneous information can lead to disqualification.
  6. Upload to YouTube or a public Google Drive link, then submit that link through Login to Participate on MyGov.
  7. Keep a copy of your submission and any acknowledgement. Proof of submission is not proof of receipt, per the terms.
  8. Watch blog.mygov.in for the winner announcement; non-winners are not notified.

We did not log in or submit an entry. The logged-in form, upload checks and any account-specific restrictions were not tested.

India and cost boundaries

The Terms and Conditions state the contest is open to Indian citizens, so Kerala readers are included in that invitation. The page highlights youth participation but states no age limit in the terms we read.

Entry is free. Your own production costs - filming, editing, data and time - are separate and yours.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an entry fee?

No. The Terms and Conditions state there is no entry fee.

Is there an age limit?

The terms we read say the contest is open to Indian citizens and the page invites especially the youth, but no specific age limit is stated. Confirm on the official page if this matters to you.

How will I know if I win?

Winners are announced on blog.mygov.in. Entries that are not selected receive no notification.

Can the contest change?

Yes. BPR&D, MHA reserves the right to cancel or amend the contest, its terms, technical parameters and evaluation criteria. Disputes are subject to Delhi jurisdiction.
