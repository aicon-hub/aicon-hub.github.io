Pinterest GenAI settings: opt out of Canvas training and see fewer AI Pins

By AiCoN | Published 13 October 2026 | Sources checked 13 October 2026

Pinterest lets you opt out of Canvas training on your saved content and choose to see fewer AI Pins. Here are the steps, what the settings do not cover, and what to check.

What it is

Pinterest has settings that let you steer how AI shows up for you. Pinterest's help pages describe three things: a setting to stop your saved and uploaded content being used to improve its image model Pinterest Canvas, a "see fewer AI Pins" choice in your feed, and labels on Pins that look AI-made or AI-edited.

Is it free?

Yes. Pinterest's India site says "Join Pinterest for free", and these settings are part of the normal account settings. We found no Pinterest page that puts any of them behind a paid plan.

What you can control

  • Training setting. Pinterest says that when you save or upload content, it may use it to improve the accuracy, safety and performance of Pinterest Canvas, and that you can opt out at any time. Pinterest says Canvas is used to make ad backgrounds on product images. Under-18 accounts are opted out automatically and cannot opt back in, per Pinterest.
  • See fewer AI Pins. Pinterest says you can choose to see fewer AI Pins in certain categories in your home feed. Pinterest's newsroom listed categories such as art, beauty, fashion, health, home decor, sport and, later, food and drink (post dated 16 October 2025; food and drink added in its 12 December 2025 update).
  • AI labels. Pinterest says it adds an "AI modified" label on the close-up of a Pin when metadata or its detection systems suggest AI was used, or when the creator says so. For promoted Pins, the label is in the ellipsis menu next to the Pin, not its close-up. Pinterest says its detection can find AI content even without obvious markers. It does not say it is always right, and creators can appeal a label.

How to turn them on

  • Training opt-out, on the web: More options, Settings, Privacy and data, then untick "Use your data to train Pinterest Canvas" and click Save. In the app: your profile picture, your profile photo, Privacy and data, then the toggle next to the same line.
  • Fewer AI Pins: Refine your recommendations, then the "AI content" tab on the web. The app tab is called "GenAI interests".
  • Pinterest notes that GenAI interests is on desktop and Android only, with iOS "coming soon" on the page we read. The dated newsroom post said iOS would roll out in the following weeks, but current help still says coming soon. We did not verify iOS availability. Menu names can change.

Who can use it

Pinterest's terms say you must be at least 13, or older where local law sets a higher age of consent. Its Terms also require parent or legal guardian permission for users aged 13 to 18. Pinterest has an India site, in.pinterest.com. We did not find an official page that lists these AI settings by country, so check that they appear in your app.

Privacy

  • Pinterest says the opt-out is about training Canvas. Its help page does not say it stops all other uses of your data, so do not read it that way.
  • Turning on fewer AI Pins changes what you see. Pinterest says recommendation controls do not change the ads in your home feed. It does not remove AI Pins from the site, and the page does not say it hides AI Pins everywhere.
  • AiCoN advice: check the setting after app updates and avoid saving or uploading photos you would not want used for training.

Frequently asked questions

Does this stop Pinterest using AI?

No. Pinterest says it uses machine learning for recommendations and Gen AI for ads and support tools. The settings cover the choices above only.

Can I remove the AI label from my own Pin?

Pinterest says you can appeal a label from the Gen AI labels help page.

What changed since this post?

Pinterest help, Terms, India site and dated newsroom post checked on 13 October 2026. Canvas-only opt-out remains supported; under-18s cannot opt in. Added guardian permission for ages 13 to 18, the separate ad-label location and the limit that recommendation controls do not change ads. Current help still says iOS coming soon, despite the older newsroom rollout promise. Food and drink were added in the 12 December 2025 newsroom update. Terms also announce an update effective 12 November 2026; that is not yet the current rule. No account, setting, feed, detection, appeal or India-access test performed.
