Ask Maps: ask Google Maps real questions in India

By AiCoN | 8 October 2026 | 5 min read

Ask Maps adds Gemini to Google Maps so you can type or speak a question and get place suggestions, route comparisons and next steps. In its 12 March 2026 post, Google said it was starting to roll out in India in English on Android and iOS, with Hindi coming soon. We did not verify a later Hindi launch.

What it is

Ask Maps is a Gemini-powered feature in Google Maps. Google's help page calls it an AI tool that helps you find places to go and gives specific details about a destination. You ask in your own words, by text or voice, and can keep asking follow-up questions.

India status

Google's India blog post of 12 March 2026 says Ask Maps was starting to roll out in India in English on Android and iOS, with Hindi launching soon. A rollout is gradual, so you may not see the button yet. We did not verify whether Hindi has launched since that post.

How to use it on Android

  1. Open the Google Maps app.
  2. Under the search bar, tap Ask Maps.
  3. Type a question or tap the microphone, then tap Send.
  4. Ask follow-up questions in the same chat.

Google's own examples include weekend things to do within a 15-minute drive, the fastest way to the airport with when to leave, and where to find a phone charger nearby. Google's India post gives an example of comparing driving with the metro to Connaught Place.

What it can do, per Google

Answers about placesGoogle says it draws on information from over 300 million places, including community reviews.
Route comparisonGoogle's India example compares driving and public transport using traffic conditions and travel time.
PersonalisedGoogle says responses use things like places you searched for or saved in Maps.
Next stepsGoogle's blog says you can save places to a list, get directions, and book restaurant reservations. We did not test booking.

Your chat history

  • Tap History in Ask Maps to see past conversations.
  • You can delete a whole conversation, but not part of one.
  • Tap thumbs down under a reply to report a problem.

Limits to know

  • It is AI, so double-check timings, opening hours and prices before you travel.
  • It uses your saved and searched places to personalise answers. Decide if you are comfortable with that.
  • Google's pages do not state a price, and we did not find any. We quote none.
  • Availability may vary by phone, account and rollout stage.
AiCoN checkChecked Google Maps Help (Ask questions in Google Maps, Android) and Google's India blog post on 8 October 2026. We did not use Ask Maps ourselves. Hindi availability now, reservation partners and accuracy were not verified.

Frequently asked questions

Is it in India?

Google said it started rolling out in India in English in March 2026. Check for the Ask Maps button in your app.

Does it work in Hindi?

Google said Hindi was launching soon. We did not verify that it has launched.

Is it free?

Google's pages do not mention a charge, but we did not verify pricing.

Can I delete my chats?

Yes, whole conversations, from History inside Ask Maps.
