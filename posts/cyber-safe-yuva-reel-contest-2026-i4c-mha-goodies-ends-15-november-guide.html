Cyber Safe Yuva Reel Contest 2026: official rules and possible rewards

By AiCoN | Published 17 October 2026 | Sources checked 17 October 2026 | Scoped recheck 24 October 2026

The official MyGov task page invites reels for I4C’s Cyber Safe Yuva Reel Contest 2026. Its terms describe eligibility for Indian citizens aged 18 or above, subject to organiser-linked award restrictions. Selected top-three entries may receive I4C/Cyber Dost goodies, a certificate and a memento; these are not guaranteed rewards. The page displays 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM IST as the end date. Rechecked 24 October 2026: a Login to Participate control was visible, but account-level submission was not tested. No entry was submitted.

Good to knowThe displayed gratification lists no cash prize - possible rewards are goodies, a certificate, a memento and a possible feature on I4C/Cyber Dost channels, and I4C decides the number of winners by entry quality. Once submitted, an entry cannot be withdrawn. By entering, you grant I4C, Cyber Dost, MHA and MyGov non-exclusive, royalty-free rights to use, edit, adapt, translate and distribute your reel for the stated awareness, educational, promotional, archival and non-commercial government purposes, with acknowledgement wherever appropriate.

What to make

  • A vertical (9:16) reel of up to 90 seconds; MP4 is preferred by the official technical parameters
  • Hindi, English or any Indian regional language; subtitles allowed
  • Topics: digital arrest scams, investment scams, KYC fraud, phishing, malicious apps/APK files, fake job scams, QR/UPI fraud, social media fraud, identity theft, deepfakes or online harassment
  • Keep the facts accurate and end with a clear safety takeaway: Stop. Think. Then Take Action. For financial cyber fraud, call 1930 and report on cybercrime.gov.in
  • No watermarks or unauthorised third-party material. The terms allow third-party music or footage only when owned by you or used with the right permission/licence. AI-assisted content is allowed only where organisers permit it, with disclosure where required.

Rewards

  • Selected top 3 may receive (at the organisers’ discretion): I4C/Cyber Dost goodies, a certificate of recognition and a memento
  • Selected reels may be featured on official I4C, Cyber Dost and government digital platforms

Rules to read

  • Eligibility: Indian citizens aged 18 or above on submission; this is not a tested submission guarantee
  • People linked to organising the contest, and their immediate family, may be restricted from awards
  • No political or partisan messaging, and do not present claims as official government advice
  • Get consent from anyone who appears in your reel
  • Keep your MyGov profile accurate - it is used for all communication

How to enter: step by step

Checked on the live MyGov pages on 19 October 2026.

  1. Make your reel first, following the specs in this article (theme, length, language and format from the official terms).
  2. Open the official task page: mygov.in/task/cyber-safe-yuva-reel-contest-2026. The page shows the end date (15 November 2026, 11:45 PM IST) and a "Login to Participate" button.
  3. Tap Login to Participate. New to MyGov: register with MyGov Auth or with DigiLocker (MeriPehchaan single sign-on). Already registered: enter your email or mobile number and tap Request OTP.
  4. Enter the OTP sent to your email/mobile to log in.
  5. Back on the task page, upload your reel and submit your entry as the page directs.
  6. Submit before 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM IST.
