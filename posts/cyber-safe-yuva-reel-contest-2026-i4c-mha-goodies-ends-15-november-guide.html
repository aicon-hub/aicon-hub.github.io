Cyber Safe Yuva Reel Contest 2026 by I4C: goodies and certificate, ends 15 November

By AiCoN | Published 17 October 2026 | Sources checked 17 October 2026

The Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C), under the Ministry of Home Affairs, is running the Cyber Safe Yuva Reel Contest 2026 on MyGov. Make a short reel that helps people spot online scams and stay safe. The top three get I4C/Cyber Dost goodies, a certificate and a memento, and selected reels may be featured on official platforms. Entry is free for Indian citizens aged 18 and above. Entries close 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM.

Good to knowThere is no cash prize - the gratification is goodies, a certificate, a memento and a possible feature on I4C/Cyber Dost channels, and I4C decides the number of winners by entry quality. Once submitted, an entry cannot be withdrawn. By entering, you give I4C and MyGov a royalty-free right to reuse your reel for awareness, with acknowledgement.

What to make

  • A vertical (9:16) MP4 reel of up to 90 seconds
  • Hindi, English or any Indian regional language; subtitles allowed
  • Topics: digital arrest scams, investment scams, KYC fraud, phishing, malicious apps/APK files, fake job scams, QR/UPI fraud, social media fraud, identity theft, deepfakes or online harassment
  • Keep the facts accurate and end with a clear safety takeaway: Stop. Think. Then Take Action. For financial cyber fraud, call 1930 and report on cybercrime.gov.in
  • No watermarks or third-party copyrighted music or footage; AI-assisted content only where permitted and disclosed

Rewards

  • Top 3 (decided by I4C): I4C/Cyber Dost goodies, a certificate of recognition and a memento
  • Selected reels may be featured on official I4C, Cyber Dost and government digital platforms

Rules to read

  • Open to Indian citizens aged 18 or above on the date of submission
  • People linked to organising the contest, and their immediate family, may be restricted from awards
  • No political or partisan messaging, and do not present claims as official government advice
  • Get consent from anyone who appears in your reel
  • Keep your MyGov profile accurate - it is used for all communication

How to enter

  1. Make your reel following the specs above
  2. Open the contest page on mygov.in and log in
  3. Submit before 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM IST

We read the contest page and all the terms and conditions on 17 October 2026. We did not submit an entry.
