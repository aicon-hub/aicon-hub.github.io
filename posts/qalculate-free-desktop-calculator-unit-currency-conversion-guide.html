Qalculate!: free desktop calculator with unit and currency conversion

By AiCoN | Published 19 October 2026 | Sources checked 19 October 2026

Qalculate! is a desktop calculator that understands what you type, with units and currency conversion. Its site says it is free software under the GPL v2 or later.

Official Qalculate! media

What it is

Qalculate! is a calculator for your computer that understands what you type. Its site calls it "a multi-purpose cross-platform desktop calculator" and says it is "simple to use but provides power and versatility normally reserved for complicated math packages". The manual says you enter an expression "as you would write it on paper" and press Enter. It is not an AI tool.

Is it free?

Yes. The home page says: "Qalculate! is free software, licensed under the GNU Public License v2 (or later)." We saw no price, account or sign-up on the pages we read.

What it does, per the site

  • Everyday tools: the home page lists "currency conversion and percent calculation", plus unit calculations and conversion, physical constants, plotting and a number base conversion dialog.
  • Maths tools: customizable functions, symbolic calculations including integrals and equations, arbitrary precision, uncertainty propagation and interval arithmetic.
  • Fault-tolerant input: the manual says that if an expression is not fully recognized or is ambiguous, it shows "an informative, but unobtrusive, error or warning", and if it cannot fully solve something, it simplifies as far as it can.
  • Interfaces: Gtk, Qt and a command line version, per the home page.

How to get it

  1. Go to the downloads page. It showed version 5.13 on the home page.
  2. Windows: a 64-bit installer and a 64-bit portable zip. The page says it requires Windows 10 (1809) or later. It says the portable file can be unpacked and run without installing, and that no application data are written outside the unpacked folder. It can also be installed with winget or Chocolatey.
  3. Linux: Flatpak (recommended on the page), Snap packages. Mac: the page lists MacPorts and Homebrew, not a plain installer.

What to watch out for

  • The author asks for testing. The downloads page says: "Qalculate! definitely needs more testing and no guarantees whatsoever are given", while the author finds it "highly stable and reliable in everyday use". Do not rely on it for anything where a wrong answer is costly without a second check. That last part is our advice.
  • Currency conversion. We did not verify where its exchange rates come from, how often they update, or whether it needs internet for them. Check the rate against your bank before you rely on it.
  • Mac is not a simple installer. The page lists package managers only. We did not check further.
  • Not tested. We did not install or use it.

Privacy and data

  • We did not find a privacy statement on the pages we read, and we did not test what it sends. The downloads page lists a networking library (libcurl) as strongly recommended for building it, but we did not verify what it is used for.

India

  • We saw no region limit. We did not test downloads from India. We quote no INR price.
