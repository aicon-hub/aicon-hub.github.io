Worker-child scholarship 2026: NSP deadline and updated rules

By AiCoN | Published 6 October 2026 | Sources checked 7 October 2026

Children of eligible beedi, cine and mine workers can seek education support through NSP. Read the updated Ministry rules: older FAQ income limits do not match the current guideline.

Which scheme is this?

The Ministry of Labour and Employment offers financial assistance for education to the children of beedi, cine, iron ore, manganese ore, chrome ore, limestone, dolomite and mica workers. Contract and Gharkhata, or home-based, workers are included under the current guideline.

The scheme has Pre-Matric and Post-Matric entries on the National Scholarship Portal, or NSP. It is a welfare-based scholarship. It is not a general scholarship for every low-income student or every person working in mining or cinema; the worker and student must meet the scheme's conditions.

Current deadline: 31 October 2026

The live NSP scheme list checked on 7 October 2026 shows student applications open till Saturday, 31 October 2026 for both the Labour Pre-Matric and Post-Matric entries. It lists defective-application and institute verification till 15 November, with DNO/SNO/MNO verification till 30 November. The later verification dates are not extra time for a student to submit a first application.

A June 2026 PIB notice had given 31 August for Pre-Matric and 31 October for Post-Matric. The current portal has the later Pre-Matric closing date. Always check your exact live scheme row before submitting; dates can change again.

Updated income and study rules

  • Either parent must be a listed worker with at least six months of service.
  • The current Ministry guideline sets a family-income ceiling of ₹21,000 per month or ₹2,52,000 per year for the listed worker groups.
  • The student must have passed the last qualifying examination in the first attempt. Students promoted to the next class are also eligible.
  • The guideline requires regular admission in a recognised Government or Government-aided institute in India.
  • Correspondence study is not eligible.

The guideline also excludes some repeat or second qualifications: another subject at the same completed level, a different professional path after completing one, and a repeat year in the same class. Read those conditions against your own course rather than assuming that every higher-education admission qualifies.

Why the old FAQ can mislead you

The NSP Labour FAQ still gives older ₹10,000 and ₹8,000 income figures in one answer. It also says no other scholarship in one section, then allows permitted combinations from 2026-27 in the next. Those statements should not be mixed into the newer rules.

The Ministry guideline linked below uses ₹21,000 as the family-income ceiling and classifies the scheme as welfare-based. It allows the Ministry scholarship alongside permitted merit-based and welfare-based scholarships from Central or State Government agencies. The current NSP announcement also allows one merit-based and one or more welfare-based schemes, subject to eligibility. Confirm your particular combination with the institute or scheme officer.

How much assistance is listed?

The 2026 PIB notice lists yearly amounts by study level: Classes I-IV ₹1,000; V-VIII ₹1,500; IX-X ₹2,000; XI-XII ₹3,000; ITI, polytechnic and degree courses including B.Sc. Agriculture ₹6,000; and listed professional courses such as BE, MBBS and MBA ₹25,000. Course and eligibility verification still apply.

These are assistance amounts, not a promise to pay all tuition or living costs. An application does not guarantee selection or payment on a particular date.

How to apply

  1. Open the official NSP and read the current Labour scheme guideline.
  2. Complete One Time Registration, or OTR. NSP describes it as a 14-digit student identifier based on Aadhaar or an Aadhaar Enrolment ID.
  3. Use your OTR to log into the scholarship application service.
  4. Select the correct Pre-Matric or Post-Matric Labour scheme for your study level.
  5. Enter accurate worker, income, course and institute details and supply the documents requested in the live form.
  6. Check everything before final submission and keep the acknowledgement.
  7. Follow up on institute verification and any defective-application notice before its deadline.
  8. Track status through NSP instead of trusting an agent's payment promise.

DBT and documents

The current guideline requires a valid mobile and identity route, and an Aadhaar-seeded, DBT-enabled bank account with active NPCI mapping. It gives an EID or parent/legal-guardian identity route for students without Aadhaar, subject to the current NSP and DBT framework. Follow the exact official form for your case.

Prepare evidence of the parent's work, required income details and the student's study record. Check the live document list with your institute. Merely having an Aadhaar number does not prove that bank mapping is active; check with the bank if payment readiness is uncertain.

Free application and safety

Use the official portal. A middleman cannot guarantee approval or make verification disappear. Do not give OTPs, passwords or full identity documents to an unsolicited caller. A local service centre may charge for separate assistance; that is not a scholarship approval fee.

For a Kerala student, worker status and the recognised institute still matter. Do not assume residence in Kerala alone creates eligibility. Ask the institute's scholarship contact or the official NSP help route if the current guideline and form do not fit your circumstances.

What changed since the original AiCoN post?

This guide adds the revised family-income ceiling, current eligibility and DBT rules, the live Pre-Matric date and the stale-FAQ warning. The original Post-Matric 31 October date remains supported by the current NSP list.

Frequently asked questions

Is the family-income limit still ₹10,000?

The current Ministry guideline uses ₹21,000 per month across the listed groups.

Does 15 November mean I can apply late?

No. It is a verification deadline, not the listed student closing date.

Is payment guaranteed after submitting?

No. Eligibility, verification and processing still apply.
