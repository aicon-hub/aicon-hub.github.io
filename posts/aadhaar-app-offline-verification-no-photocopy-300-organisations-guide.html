Aadhaar App offline verification: 300 organisations now accept it

By AiCoN | Published 7 November 2026 | Sources checked 7 November 2026

UIDAI says 300 organisations now verify identity through the Aadhaar App instead of collecting photocopies of the card. It is consent-based, and the release lists hotels, healthcare, education, events and gig platforms.

What did UIDAI announce?

In a PIB release on 5 November 2026, UIDAI said it has onboarded 300 Offline Verification Seeking Entities (OVSEs) since the service started in January. The sectors named are hospitality, healthcare, education, events, the gig economy and consumer-facing businesses.

UIDAI calls the method consent-based and paperless, and says it keeps user control and data minimisation in line with the DPDP Act. The Economic Times reports that you can choose what details to share, and that the organisation does not receive your 12-digit Aadhaar number. That detail is from the Economic Times, not from the PIB text, so check it on UIDAI's own pages.

Why it matters

A photocopy of Aadhaar left at a hotel desk or a clinic can be copied, kept or misused. Verification through the app means you show what is needed and the organisation does not need a paper copy.

The numbers UIDAI gave

  • Nearly 7.5 crore Aadhaar App downloads
  • Over 86 lakh mobile number updates through the app
  • 22.16 lakh address updates and 40.25 lakh email updates
  • 4.6 crore biometric lock and unlock transactions
  • 3.61 lakh Aadhaar lock and unlock transactions

What you can do

  1. Get the Aadhaar App only through the links on uidai.gov.in.
  2. If a business asks for a photocopy, ask whether it accepts Aadhaar App offline verification.
  3. Share only what the check needs, and do not send screenshots of your Aadhaar over chat.
  4. Keep biometric lock on when you are not using it. The app has this feature.

What AiCoN could not confirm

  • Which 300 organisations are covered. The release does not name them.
  • Whether a given hotel or hospital will accept it today. Acceptance depends on the organisation.
  • Any rule that forces businesses to stop taking photocopies. The release does not state one.
