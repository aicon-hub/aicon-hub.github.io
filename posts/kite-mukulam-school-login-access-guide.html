KERALA STUDENT ACCESS GUIDE

KITE Mukulam: free entrance preparation through your school

By AiCoN | Access route checked 18 November 2026

Studying in Class 11 or 12 at a Kerala public school? Mukulam brings school-subject resources and entrance-oriented practice into one KITE portal. The important first step is not a new personal signup: ask your school for your student login.

This is an access guide, not a learning-quality review. We checked the official public portal and followed its Learning Room route to the student-login page. We did not sign in, attempt a lesson or mock test, or assess the AI study plan.

Who is it for?

The Kerala Government describes Mukulam as free preparation for Classes 11 and 12 in Kerala public schools, covering Higher Secondary and Vocational Higher Secondary students. Its announcement includes Science, Commerce and Humanities, rather than only science entrance coaching.

The portal identifies HSE/VHSE students, teachers and school administrators as its users. The student guide says schools supply access. We have not verified open registration for private-school students, students outside Kerala, repeat candidates or the general public. If your eligibility is unclear, check with your principal or nodal teacher instead of assuming the public homepage gives everyone a student account.

How to get access

  1. Open mukulam.kite.kerala.gov.in, the official site linked by the Kerala Government.
  2. Ask your school for your student username and initial password. The official guide says the username is the student's hsCAP Registration Number; ask the school if you do not have it.
  3. Choose Login or Get Started. The official site takes you to Key to Entrance. This different site name is part of the route we checked.
  4. Choose Student Login, then enter your school-issued details and the displayed captcha. Do not borrow another student's account.
  5. According to the student guide, first login requires changing the initial password and completing the student profile. Keep your new password private. If it is lost, the guide says the school can reset it.
  6. After registration, use the learning sections with your teacher's guidance. Read the official student guide (PDF) for screen-by-screen instructions.

What does KITE say is inside?

The official homepage lists learning resources, assignments, practice exams and a personalised study plan. It names NEET, JEE, KEAM and CUET as entrance-preparation areas. This is not a promise that every exam or subject has a complete current course available in every account.

  • Entrance Exam Suite: the student guide describes teacher-prepared mock exams and performance information.
  • Samagra Classroom: the guide describes chapter-wise topics, interactive activities, videos, topic reviews, assignments and online exams.
  • Learning Room: KITE describes AI-assisted personalised practice and learning support.

These are official feature descriptions, not features we tested inside an account. The practical difference from a general chatbot is the intended connection to Kerala school resources, teacher-led work and student progress. We have not measured its accuracy, syllabus coverage, teaching quality or effect on exam results.

Is it free?

The Kerala Government announcement calls this free AI-assisted entrance preparation, and KITE's student guide describes free entrance coaching. We did not create an account or reach an authenticated payment screen. There is no need to pay a third party just to obtain the school-issued login described in the guide.

What our check confirmed

On 18 November 2026, the Mukulam public homepage loaded and its Learning Room link opened the Key to Entrance site. The Student Login form asked for username, password and captcha. We read the official student guide to confirm the school-issued login and first-login steps.

We stopped before authentication. We did not enter a student's registration number, password or profile information, and did not test lessons, downloads, exams, scoring or personalised recommendations. Availability inside an account may depend on school setup and resources made available at the time.

Before you share details

Use only the official KITE domains above. The guide describes completing a student profile and viewing learning progress; this is not an anonymous tool. Do not send student passwords, registration numbers or profile screenshots to unofficial help accounts. Ask your school when a profile field or access problem is unclear. We have not audited the portal's data retention or AI processing.

Official sources

  • Kerala Government: Mukulam announcement (updated 17 September 2026) - free scope, public-school audience, Classes 11/12 and streams.
  • Mukulam official homepage - current entry point and feature descriptions.
  • Key to Entrance student-login route - destination reached from Mukulam.
  • KITE student user guide (PDF) - credentials, first-login registration and learning-section instructions.
