Kerala employment registration cancelled? Special Renewal on the e-Employment portal, 31 October notice

By AiCoN | Published 31 October 2026 | Notice checked 31 October 2026

The Kerala e-Employment Exchange home page carries a notice titled "Special Renewal Opportunity". It says employment registrations that were cancelled can be renewed through a Special Renewal option on the home page up to 31/10/2026. We read the notice and the Special Renewal page on 31 October 2026. We did not log in or renew anything.

Who the notice covers

The notice is in Malayalam. In our reading, it covers registrations that had a renewal recorded on the employment registration card from 10/2024 to 06/2026 and were later cancelled for various reasons. The "Special Renewal" tile on the same page says it is for job seekers who are eligible for special renewal under a government order.

How it works

  1. Open www.eemployment.kerala.gov.in.
  2. Use the "Special Renewal" option in the top menu or the Special Renewal tile.
  3. The Jobseeker Special Renewal page asks for district, exchange, local body, ward, registration number, gender, date of birth, mobile number and a captcha, then "Get Details".

Deadline

The notice says 31/10/2026. It does not give a time of day, so if you need this, do it today and check the portal. We cannot say what happens after that date.

We did not test the renewal, so we do not know what the next screens ask for. For help, the home page lists district helpline numbers (working days, 10:00 to 17:00) and the Employment Directorate email deker.emp.lbr@kerala.gov.in.

Sources checked 31 October 2026:

  • e-Employment Exchange home page and notice (verified)
  • Jobseeker Special Renewal page (verified)
