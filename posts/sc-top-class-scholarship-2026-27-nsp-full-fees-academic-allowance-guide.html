SC Top Class Scholarship 2026-27: full fees plus Rs 86,000 allowance, apply on NSP by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 18 October 2026 | Sources checked 18 October 2026

The Central Sector Scholarship of Top Class Education pays the full tuition fee and non-refundable charges for SC students admitted to notified top institutions - IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS, NLUs and others - plus an academic allowance of Rs 86,000 in the first year and Rs 41,000 every year after. Fresh applications for 2026-27 are open on the National Scholarship Portal; student applications close on 31 October 2026.

Good to knowSlots are limited and shared among notified institutions; the institution selects students on entrance merit, and at least 30% of slots at each institution are reserved for girls. For private institutions the fee cover is capped at Rs 2 lakh a year per student. You cannot hold another scholarship at the same time. Keep your income certificate, caste certificate, bank details, admission rank and fee details ready before you apply.

Who can apply

  • Scheduled Caste students admitted to a full-time prescribed course at a notified institution (IIMs, IITs, IIITs, AIIMSs, NITs, NIFTs, NIDs, IHMs, NLUs, other Central Government institutions, NAAC A++/A+ accredited institutions, top-100 NIRF institutions, and Institutions of National Importance)
  • Total annual family income from all sources up to Rs 8 lakh
  • Both fresh admissions (first year) and renewals apply on NSP

What you get

  • Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges (ceiling of Rs 2 lakh per year per student for private sector institutions)
  • Academic allowance: Rs 86,000 in the first year, Rs 41,000 in every subsequent year - for living expenses, books and stationery, and a computer with accessories
  • Paid by Direct Benefit Transfer; students must deposit the fee with the institute within a month of receiving it

How to apply

  1. Register your One Time Registration (OTR) on the National Scholarship Portal
  2. Apply under "Central Sector Scholarship of Top Class Education for SC Students"
  3. Upload income certificate, caste certificate, and fill bank account, admission rank and fee details
  4. The institute verifies and forwards your application; keep watching the portal for verification status

Deadlines on the NSP scheme list (checked 18 October 2026): student applications close 31 October 2026; institute verification 15 November 2026; state/nodal verification 30 November 2026. Benefits and rules above are from the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment scheme guidelines published on NSP.
