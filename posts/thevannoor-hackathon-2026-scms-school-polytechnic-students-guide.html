Thevannoor Hackathon 2026: Rs 3.5 lakh prizes for school and polytechnic students

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

The 12th Dr. Pradeep P. Thevannoor Hackathon at SCMS School of Engineering and Technology, Cochin, is open to students in Grades 8 to 12 and polytechnic colleges. Registration and idea submission run 5 to 30 October 2026, the total prize pool is Rs 3.5 lakh, and the grand finale is on 16 and 17 November 2026.

Who can take part

Students in Grades 8 to 12 and students of polytechnic colleges, per the official page. Prizes are awarded separately for the School and Polytechnic categories.

Problem statements

  • Smart Mobility: AI-based road safety and accident prevention, vehicles of 2050, green mobility, smart public transportation.
  • Disaster and Climate: AI-based landslide prediction and early warning, smart flood monitoring and emergency response, climate-resilient communities, portable foldable bridge for landslide rescue.
  • Intelligent Technology for an Inclusive Society: AI solutions for personalised learning, assistive technologies for senior citizens and people with visual, hearing, speech or mobility impairments, smart robots for sustainable farming, AI for preserving culture, heritage and traditional knowledge.

Prizes and benefits

  • Total prize money: Rs 3.5 lakh, awarded separately for School and Polytechnic categories.
  • 1st prize Rs 1 lakh, 2nd prize Rs 50,000, 3rd prize Rs 25,000.
  • SCMS tuition-fee scholarships for winners: 30% (1st), 25% (2nd), 20% (3rd).
  • Winners get space at incubation centres of SCMS institutions, plus opportunities for funding and assistance.

Key dates

  • Registration and idea submission: 5 to 30 October 2026.
  • Shortlisted teams announced: first week of November 2026.
  • Grand finale: 16 and 17 November 2026 at SCMS School of Engineering and Technology, Cochin (the page also describes the final event as the third week of November).

How to register

The official page links two Google Forms, one for school students and one for polytechnic students, each labelled "Register Here". Start from the official hackathon page below and pick the form for your category. The page lists no registration fee.

Contact

+91 94465 03448, +91 97461 77760, sset@scmsgroup.org. Venue: SCMS School of Engineering and Technology, Vidya Nagar, Palissery, Karukutty, Ernakulam, Kerala 683576.
