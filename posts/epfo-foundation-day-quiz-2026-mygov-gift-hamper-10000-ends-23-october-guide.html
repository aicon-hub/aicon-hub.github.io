EPFO Foundation Day Quiz 2026: gift hampers up to ₹10,000, ends 23 October

By AiCoN | Published 16 October 2026 | Sources checked 16 October 2026

EPFO is running a Foundation Day Quiz on MyGov, open to all Indian citizens. It has 10 questions in 5 minutes. Five winners get gift hampers worth ₹10,000 down to ₹6,000, and everyone gets an e-certificate. The quiz runs until 23 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

Good to knowWinners are picked by a computer-generated random draw from everyone who answers all questions correctly in time. A fast or high score does not rank you. The MyGov page showed "Closed" when we opened it without logging in, so confirm that it opens for you. The rules say EPFO can amend or cancel the quiz.

What you can win

  • First: gift hamper worth ₹10,000 (about US$120)
  • Second: gift hamper worth ₹9,000
  • Third: gift hamper worth ₹8,000
  • Fourth: gift hamper worth ₹7,000
  • Fifth: gift hamper worth ₹6,000
  • Every participant: an e-certificate of participation

The prizes are described as gift hampers, not cash. The prize ceremony is at the EPFO Foundation Day function. For winners, EPFO will reimburse AC-III rail fare from home to New Delhi, and arrange stay or reimburse boarding and lodging as per its Group B officer entitlements.

Quiz details

  • Dates: 13 October 2026, 5:00 PM to 23 October 2026, 11:45 PM
  • 10 questions, 300 seconds. The timer starts when you click Play Quiz
  • Topics: EPFO schemes, including the Code on Social Security 2020, the EPF Act 1952, EPF, EDLI and Pension Schemes 2026, the Employee Enrolment Campaign, CiTES, PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana, Amnesty Scheme 2026 and Vishwas 2026

Rules to read

  • Open to all Indian citizens. People connected with organising the quiz and their immediate family are not eligible
  • One entry per person. Entries cannot be withdrawn once submitted
  • You give your name, email, phone number and other details. By entering you agree that MyGov and EPFO may use them to run the quiz
  • Winners are drawn at random from the pool of all-correct entries submitted in time
  • Impersonation or double entries make the entry void. The organisers' decision is final

How to play: step by step

Checked on the live MyGov quiz pages on 19 October 2026.

  1. Open the official quiz page: quiz.mygov.in/quiz/epfo-foundation-day-quiz-2026. The page shows the quiz banner, end date (23 October 2026, 11:45 PM), 10 questions and 300 seconds.
  2. Tap Login with OTP to Play Quiz (or Login with MyGov AUTH). If the page shows "Closed", log in first - it usually opens after login.
  3. New to MyGov: register with MyGov Auth or with DigiLocker (MeriPehchaan single sign-on). Already registered: enter your email or mobile number and tap Request OTP.
  4. Enter the OTP sent to your email/mobile to log in.
  5. Come back to the quiz page, read the topic list and the Terms and Conditions, then click Play Quiz - the 5-minute timer starts when you click.
  6. Answer all 10 questions and submit. One entry per person; entries cannot be withdrawn after submission.
  7. After you finish, download your e-certificate of participation. Winners are picked later by a computerised random draw from all-correct entries, so a fast score does not help.

Steps 4 to 7 happen inside your own login, so they are not shown as screenshots.
