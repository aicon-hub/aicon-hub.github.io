D.E.S.I.G.N. for BioE3 Challenge: ₹1 lakh for top 10 ideas, final cycle closes 20 October

By AiCoN | Published 15 October 2026 | Sources checked 15 October 2026

The D.E.S.I.G.N. for BioE3 Challenge, run by the Department of Biotechnology with BIRAC and BRIC, is in its twelfth and final monthly cycle. The window closes on 20 October 2026. In the open category for all Indian citizens, the top 10 ideas each month get ₹1,00,000 (about US$1,200), shared equally within a team. School students (Classes 6 to 12) enter with a video and can win certificates.

Good to knowThe MyGov page says the portal accepts entries until 5:30 PM on the 20th. The BRIC portal page we could read showed an older cycle status, so confirm that the form is open for you before you start. Do not wait for the last day.

Two categories

  • Classes 6 to 12 (school students): a 60 to 120 second video in English or Hindi, uploaded to YouTube by a parent or guardian, submitted on MyGov at innovateindia.mygov.in/bioe3. Teams of up to 5 from the same school. The top 10 get digital Certificates of Merit, and 20 to 30 more get certificates of appreciation
  • Open to all Indian citizens: a written concept proposal on the BRIC portal. The cash prize is in this category. Individuals or teams of up to 5. An Aadhaar number is needed, and you do not need an institution in round 1

What the open category offers

  • Top 10 winning ideas each month: ₹1,00,000 each (the November 2025 cycle result page confirms the ₹1 lakh for its top 10)
  • Prize money is divided equally among team members
  • Winners may then apply to BIRAC within 3 months for a follow-on grant of up to ₹25 lakh (about US$30,000) to turn the idea into a proof of concept. Funding is decided by DBT and BIRAC alone

The proposal

The RFP asks for a concept summary, design logic, potential use and BioE3 link, feasibility and safety, sustainability and impact, and novelty. Each part has a word limit, plus a complete proposal PDF (max 10 MB) and an originality undertaking. Ideas are screened for feasibility, technical soundness and fit with the BioE3 policy.

Rules to read before you submit

  • No entry fee
  • One proposal per applicant in a month. Unselected entries can be revised and resubmitted in a later window
  • The form locks after submission, including the team list
  • Copied work is disqualified. For school videos, AI-generated visuals or narration are not allowed
  • Under-18 entrants need a signed parent or guardian consent form
  • Winners are announced within one month of closing, and the date can change

How to apply: step by step

Checked on both official portals on 19 October 2026. The route depends on who you are. The current monthly cycle closes 20 October 2026; the portal notes the window runs 20 days each month until 20 October 2026.

School students

  1. Open the MyGov Innovate India page: innovateindia.mygov.in/bioe3. It shows "SUBMISSION Open" and a "Login To Participate Now" button.
  2. Log in or register on MyGov (mobile/email + OTP, or DigiLocker).
  3. Upload your video link and the signed consent form as the page directs, then submit before 20 October 2026.

Everyone else (college students, researchers, startups, others)

  1. Open the BRIC challenge portal: bric.nic.in/bioe3challenge.
  2. Click Register Now and register with your Aadhaar number.
  3. Log in, prepare your concept proposal (the portal has a "Download your Proposal" option), fill the application and upload the proposal.
  4. Save a draft, re-read the terms on the Guidelines page, then submit before 20 October 2026.

The form and upload steps happen inside your own login, so they are not shown as screenshots.
