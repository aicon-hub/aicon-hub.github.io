Habitica: free habit tracker and to-do app that works like a game

By AiCoN | Published 19 October 2026 | Sources checked 19 October 2026

Habitica turns your habits and to-do list into a game. Its site says there is a free version; paid Gems and a subscription are optional.

Official Habitica media

What it is

Habitica is a habit and to-do app that turns your tasks into a game. Its website calls it "a free habit-building and productivity app that treats your real life like a game". You add Habits, Dailies and To Dos, tick them off, and your avatar earns Gold and experience, and loses health when you skip Dailies. It is not an AI tool.

Is it free?

Yes, there is a free version. The Habitica homepage and its Google Play listing both say "free". Habitica's terms say you may "choose our free Service or paid Service (Premium)". The paid side is optional: the FAQ says Gems are an in-app paid currency for items like equipment and avatar looks, and subscribers get extras such as a monthly gear set. Group Plans are a paid feature, while Challenges are free to everyone. We could not see current prices, so we quote no price or INR amount.

What you get, per Habitica

  • Three task types: Habits (positive, negative or both), Dailies on a schedule, and one-off To Dos.
  • Rewards and penalties: tasks change colour from yellow towards blue (better) or red (worse). Missing a Daily costs HP. A "Pause Damage" button in Settings stops that, for example on holiday.
  • Party and quests: fight monsters with friends. Mobile apps for Android and iOS, and a web version.
  • Data export: task data can be exported from the Site Data tab in Settings on the website.

How to start

  1. Open habitica.com, or find "Habitica: Gamify Your Tasks" by HabitRPG, Inc. on Google Play.
  2. Make an account (the privacy policy mentions email sign-up and log in with Google or Apple).
  3. Add a few tasks. Our advice: start with two or three, not twenty.

What to watch out for

  • Age: Habitica's privacy policy says the service is for users 18 or older. The Google Play listing shows a content rating of "Everyone", so the two do not match. We go by Habitica's own policy.
  • Ads: the Google Play listing says "Contains ads" and "In-app purchases". The homepage does not mention ads. We did not see which screens show them.
  • Real-life penalties are game penalties only. The avatar loses HP; nothing else happens. Some people find that motivating, some find it stressful. That is our view.
  • We did not install or test it.

Privacy and data

  • The privacy policy says it may collect contact details, account information, and content you add such as task lists. Payments go through Apple, Google, PayPal or Stripe.
  • It says it uses analytics and advertising cookies on the website, and that some personal information may be "sold" or shared for targeted advertising, with an opt-out section. Read the policy before you sign up.
  • The privacy policy says you can delete or reset your account from Settings. The Google Play data safety section says data may be shared with third parties and you can request deletion. Tasks can hold personal details, so our advice: keep task names general.

India

  • The Google Play page opened with the India store setting (en_IN) and showed the app, "50L+ downloads" and 4.7 stars from 68.9K reviews. We did not install it from India and saw no INR price for paid items.
