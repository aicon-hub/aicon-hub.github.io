PG Scholarship 2026-27: ₹1.5 lakh a year for 2 years, apply on NSP by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 10 October 2026 | Sources checked 10 October 2026

UGC's National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies gives ₹15,000 a month for 10 months a year (₹1.5 lakh a year) for two years. There are 10,000 slots a year and the NSP FAQ says there is no income limit. Applications for 2026-27 are open on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) until 31 October 2026.

Check before you applySelection is by merit, so applying does not guarantee a scholarship. You must be in the first year of a regular, full-time PG degree and below 30 at admission. Your institute must verify the application, and it cannot be edited after the closing date.

What does it give?

The UGC page lists ₹1,50,000 per annum for 2 years, and the NSP FAQ explains this as ₹15,000 a month for 10 months. It is paid by Direct Benefit Transfer to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account. Renewal for the second year needs a renewal application on NSP.

Who can apply?

CourseFirst year of a regular, full-time PG degree at an eligible institute with an AISHE code. For integrated courses, the scholarship covers the PG part only.
AgeBelow 30 on the date of admission to the first semester or year.
IncomeThe NSP FAQ says there is no income criterion.
Not eligibleAnyone who already holds a PG degree, distance, open or part-time students, and anyone already on another scholarship.

Key dates on NSP

Scheme open16 September 2026
Student applicationUntil 31 October 2026
Institute verificationUntil 15 November 2026
District, state or ministry verificationUntil 30 November 2026

How are awardees picked?

The NSP FAQ says selection is purely on merit, with slots allocated under Government of India reservation policy. 30% of slots are reserved for women, filled first by single, twin or fraternal girl children without considering their UG marks. Half of the slots are for arts, humanities, social sciences, law and management, and half for science, engineering, medical, technical, agriculture and forestry. The guideline says ties are broken using UG marks, then Class XII marks.

How to apply on NSP: step by step

Checked on the live National Scholarship Portal on 19 October 2026. NSP now uses One Time Registration (OTR) - do this first if you have never registered.

  1. Open scholarships.gov.in (Academic Year 2026-27 is live), click Students → Apply now, then Register. Registration is free - no fee is required.
  2. Read the OTR guidelines, tick the consent boxes and click Next. Register your active mobile number and verify it with the OTP; a reference number is sent to your mobile.
  3. Complete eKYC: install the NSP OTR app and the Aadhaar Face RD app on an Android phone and do face authentication with the reference number. Your OTR ID is then generated. Aadhaar is required; a minor without Aadhaar can register with a parent's or legal guardian's Aadhaar.
  4. Log in on NSP with your OTR ID, open Fresh Application for Academic Year 2026-27 and choose the National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies from the scheme list.
  5. Fill the application form and upload the documents it lists.
  6. Submit before 31 October 2026. Track the application in the same dashboard; institute and district/state verification happen after you submit.

Steps 4-6 happen inside your own NSP login, so they are not shown as screenshots. Generating an OTR alone is not an application - you must still log in and submit the scholarship application.

  2. Apply under National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies and submit before 31 October 2026.
  3. Ask your institute to verify the application before 15 November 2026.
  4. Make sure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get this and another scholarship?

No. The FAQ says a student can avail only one scholarship at a time.

What is a single girl child?

The only girl child in the family with no siblings. A girl who is one of twin or fraternal daughters is also eligible. One daughter with a brother does not count.
