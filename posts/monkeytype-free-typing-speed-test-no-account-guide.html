Monkeytype: free typing speed test, no account needed

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

Monkeytype is a free typing speed test that runs in your browser with no account. We took a real test on it today. Here is what its pages say and what we found when we used it.

Quick facts

  • What it does: measures your typing speed and accuracy in timed tests, word sets, quotes and more, right in the browser.
  • Price: free. Open source under GPL 3.0. An optional account (for saving history) is also free. The site shows ads and sells merch to support itself.
  • Platforms: any modern web browser; best on a physical keyboard.
  • Best for: students and professionals who want to type faster, or just check their speed once.

What it is

Monkeytype describes itself as "the most customizable typing website with a minimalistic design". You open the page, words appear, and you type them against the clock. Tests run from 15 seconds to two minutes, or by word count, quotes, or a calm "zen" mode. The project is open source on GitHub with over 20,000 stars.

We tried it

This was a functional workflow test: we drove the site with scripted keystrokes to check that every step works, not to judge how it feels for a human typist.

  • Opened monkeytype.com in a desktop browser on 21 October 2026 and declined non-essential cookies. The test still worked fully.
  • Completed the default 30-second test with no account, start to finish. Words appeared instantly and every keystroke registered live.
  • The result screen showed speed (wpm), accuracy, raw speed, character counts and consistency, then invited us to "Sign in to save your result". Testing without an account costs you nothing but saved history.
  • An ad slot appeared on the results page; the typing area itself stayed clean during the test.

Is it free?

Yes. The code is under the GNU GPL 3.0 licence, the site is free to use, and the optional account is free. It supports itself with ads and a merch store, per the pages we read and what we saw in our test.

Privacy and data

  • Without an account, your custom texts stay in your browser's local storage and are not collected, per the privacy policy.
  • If you create an account, Monkeytype collects your email, username, test results, settings and typing statistics, per the policy.
  • The site uses cookies; our test worked after rejecting the non-essential ones.

Our advice

  • Start with the 30-second test a few times a week and watch accuracy first; speed follows. This is our advice, not the project's.
  • You do not need an account unless you want long-term history and leaderboards.

India

  • It is a website, so it works anywhere with a browser and no region limits appear on its pages. Our test connection was not an Indian one, so local ad behaviour in India may differ.

Frequently asked questions

Is Monkeytype free?

Yes. It is free and open source under GPL 3.0. It shows ads and sells merch; the optional account is free too.

Do I need to sign up?

No. We completed a full test without an account. Sign-up only adds saved history and leaderboards.

What is a good typing speed?

Around 40 words per minute is average for everyday typists; 65 or more is fast for most office work. Treat these as rough guides, not official figures.
