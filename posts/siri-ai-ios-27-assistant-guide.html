Siri AI on iPhone: what Apple says the new assistant can do

By AiCoN | 9 October 2026 | 5 min read

Apple says Siri AI, a new version of Siri built on the next generation of Apple Intelligence, began rolling out in beta in English with iOS 27. It can use personal context, onscreen awareness and app actions.

What it is

Apple's newsroom post says Siri AI is a reimagined Siri with detailed, conversational answers, personal context understanding, broad world knowledge, onscreen awareness and more systemwide app actions.

What Apple says it can do

  • Find things across your messages, emails and photos, and act on them, such as adding ingredients from an email to your Reminders list.
  • Answer questions about what is on your screen, or take actions based on it.
  • Draft an email from scratch or edit and share a set of photos.
  • Use up-to-date information from the web for questions on almost any topic.
  • Work in apps you already use, such as texting a friend in WhatsApp or playing a book on Audible.

How you reach it

  • Say "Hey Siri", use the side button, or swipe down from the Dynamic Island on iPhone.
  • Apple says a new dedicated Siri app keeps your conversation history, synced privately through iCloud.

What you need

SoftwareiOS 27 (and matching iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS 27).
iPhoneApple says Apple Intelligence runs on iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro Max. Siri AI needs an Apple Intelligence-enabled device.
LanguageBeta in English. The 14 September announcement says French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish would come the following month. We did not separately verify their rollout. No Indian languages are listed for Siri AI.

Limits to know

  • It is a beta. Check important answers.
  • Some features, such as the most expressive voices and advanced dictation, need newer devices than Apple Intelligence itself.
  • Apple says Siri AI is not initially available in the EU on iPhone.
  • Apple says server-side features, including Siri AI, have daily usage limits that can vary by feature and request. Expanded access will be available for a fee in the future; no amount is stated.
  • Siri AI is not available to users under 13. Apple also says the new features are unavailable in China while it works through regulatory requirements.
  • We did not test it ourselves.
AiCoN checkRead Apple India's newsroom post on 9 October 2026, including its footnotes on devices and languages. The post does not list India-specific availability or Hindi support for Siri AI, so check your iPhone's Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new iPhone?

Apple says it needs an Apple Intelligence-enabled device. That list starts at iPhone 15 Pro.

Does it work in Hindi?

Apple's footnote lists English for the beta. It does not list Hindi for Siri AI.

Where does my data go?

Apple says requests use on-device processing and Private Cloud Compute, and that personal data is not stored or made accessible to Apple when Private Cloud Compute handles a request. This is Apple's claim.
