StoryWeaver: free children's stories in many languages

By AiCoN | Published 17 October 2026 | Sources checked 17 October 2026

StoryWeaver from Pratham Books is a free library of open-licensed children's stories, with downloads and offline reading. Its page metadata says 380+ languages.

What it is

StoryWeaver is a digital library of children's stories from Pratham Books, the publisher behind it. Its About page calls it "a digital gateway to thousands of richly illustrated, open-licensed children's stories in mother tongue languages". Its page metadata says "Read Stories in 380+ languages". You can read stories, download them, keep them for offline reading, and also write or translate stories with its tools. It is not an AI tool. We included it as a free reading resource for children and parents.

Is it free?

Yes, per the site. The About page says StoryWeaver "provides a free gateway to a never-ending collection of open source stories for children", lets you "download an endless stream of free stories", and says "All tools for translation and formatting are absolutely free". It also says all content is openly licensed under Creative Commons 4.0. We saw no price or paid plan.

What you can do with it

  • Read. Stories for early readers and more fluent ones, in languages from India and around the world, covering topics like maths, science, the environment and history, per the About page.
  • Download. Each story comes as a web-resolution PDF, an A4 print-ready PDF, and an ePub, per the About page.
  • Read offline. The About page says you can add stories to your Offline Library and read them without internet.
  • Create and translate. You can write a story with openly licensed illustrations, or translate one into another language. To request a language that is missing, the page gives the address storyweaver@prathambooks.org.

What to watch out for

  • Account. We did not check what needs a sign-in. The privacy policy mentions registration and email, and the Offline Library may need an account. We did not test it.
  • Children's data. The privacy policy says StoryWeaver and Pratham Books do not knowingly collect data from children under 16 without a parent or guardian's permission.
  • Not tested. We did not read a story, download a file or make an account.

Privacy and data

  • The privacy policy says it does not publish, sell or rent your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes without your consent.
  • It says it may use cookies, including third-party cookies, to personalize content and improve the site, and may use service providers.
  • It says your data may be stored in countries other than India, in compliance with applicable laws.
  • You can ask for your data to be deleted by emailing contact@storyweaver.org.in, though deletion may cancel your registration, per the policy.

India

  • The site is run from India and its policy refers to Indian IT law. The site is free, so no rupee price is involved. We did not test individual languages.
