Abhyas AI: free NTA mock tests for JEE, NEET and UGC NET

Practise a paper, then use the performance report to decide what to revise. The current app listings describe free tests and analysis, without a subscription or ads.

By Muhammed Salman | Published 24 November 2026 | Sources checked 24 November 2026 | Source-based overview

What AiCoN checked

We verified the app's identity through NTA's official Abhyas page and checked the current app-store descriptions. We also opened the web sign-in and blank registration screens. We did not create an account, install the mobile apps, take a mock test, or generate a performance report. This is a source-verified overview, not a hands-on review of test quality or AI accuracy.

What is it?

Abhyas AI is NTA's exam-practice platform. Its app listings describe full-length mock tests, subject tests, chapter practice and question-wise performance analysis. The aim is to help you spot errors and time-management problems instead of looking only at a total score.

This is an updated app, not a platform launched for the first time in November 2026. NTA's original National Test Abhyas launch notice is dated 19 May 2020. The current stores use the Abhyas AI name; Apple's version history records UGC NET among the additions in version 2.0.

Who can use it?

The current Android listing names JEE (Main) Paper 1 and Paper 2, NEET (UG), and UGC NET Paper 1 and Paper 2. Check the available tests for your own subject after signing in. We have not checked the live test catalogue or confirmed that every UGC NET subject has a paper.

The listings describe English and Hindi support, not Malayalam. The Android description specifically confirms both languages for JEE (Main) and NEET (UG). Kerala students can use the service, but should not expect Malayalam questions from these sources.

Apple's listing requires iOS 15.6 or later, or iPadOS 15.6 or later. Check the store on your own device for Android compatibility rather than relying on an older web FAQ.

Why might it be useful?

  • Practise with a timer: the listings describe full papers and shorter subject or chapter practice.
  • Review individual answers: the Android description lists your response, the correct answer, a solution and the time spent.
  • Separate speed from accuracy: performance reports describe correct and incorrect answers alongside whether you went over the suggested time.
  • Choose the next revision task: use the analysis to find a topic to revisit, rather than treating a report as a prediction of your final rank.

These are features described by NTA and its store listings. AiCoN has not independently measured the quality of the questions, explanations or personalised suggestions.

What does it cost?

The current Android and Apple descriptions say the app is completely free, with no subscription, paywall or ads. Mobile-data charges still depend on your network plan. A free app is not the same as an anonymous app: account details and practice activity are involved.

How to start

  1. Use NTA's own download page. Open nta.ac.in/abhyas and follow its Google Play or App Store badge. Both point to the app IDs in the direct links below.
  2. Check the publisher. Look for NTA Mobile Apps on the current Android listing and National Testing Agency on Apple's listing. Avoid look-alike apps or APK download sites.
  3. Read the terms and privacy policy. If you are under 18, NTA's policy requires your parent or guardian to read and accept it for you.
  4. Register or sign in yourself. The public web registration screen asks for a name and email address or phone number, then offers a verification code. The sign-in screen also offers OTP and Meri Pehchaan options. Do not share your password or verification code with anyone.
  5. Choose your examination and inspect the available tests. Check the paper, subject, language, instructions and marking rules before you begin. The steps inside the signed-in app have not been checked by AiCoN.
  6. Review mistakes after the test. Compare the explanation with your notes or textbooks. Revisit doubtful answers with a teacher rather than assuming every explanation is correct.

How offline practice is described

The Android listing says you first download a test while connected to the internet. You then turn off Wi-Fi or enable flight mode to attempt it without distractions. Responses are stored on the device; reconnect after finishing so answers can sync and the score and analysis can be generated.

Offline practice does not mean the entire service works without internet. Downloading, registration, syncing and generating the final analysis need connectivity. We did not test this download-and-sync flow, and do not assume that the web version has the same offline features.

Privacy: don't read "free" as "no data"

The official policy linked from the live sign-in screen describes account information, technical information and practice activity, including time spent answering questions. Apple's developer disclosure lists contact information, identifiers, usage data and diagnostics as categories that may be collected.

Google Play's displayed data-safety declaration differs from that policy and Apple's disclosure. Do not treat a store badge as proof that no information is processed. Read the linked policy before registering and provide only the details you are comfortable sharing.

What this cannot promise

Practice scores do not guarantee an entrance-exam result, rank or admission. The Android listing itself warns that mock-test content, interface and analysis may not exactly match the actual examination. Follow the latest official Information Bulletin for your exam's format, syllabus and marking scheme.

We are not claiming a particular AI model, error-free advice, guaranteed improvement, or full subject coverage. Account creation, test availability, offline operation and generated analysis have not been checked by us.
