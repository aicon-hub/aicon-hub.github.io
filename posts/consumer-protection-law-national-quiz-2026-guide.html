National Quiz on Consumer Protection Law 2026: win up to ₹10,000, ends 25 November

By AiCoN | Published 8 October 2026 | Sources checked 8 October 2026

The Chair on Consumer Law at National Law University Delhi, set up by the Department of Consumer Affairs, is running a National Quiz Competition on Consumer Protection Law 2026 with MyGov. It is open to all Indian citizens, has cash prizes with ₹10,000 for first place, and runs until 25 November 2026, 11:45 PM.

Check before you playWhen we first checked, the MyGov quiz page showed a "Closed" label without logging in. Re-checked on 19 October 2026: the page now shows both login options and the end date 25 November 2026, 11:45 PM, so it appears open. Once you click Play Quiz the timer starts, and entries cannot be withdrawn.

What does the quiz cover?

According to the official page, the quiz aims to build consumer awareness and legal literacy, especially among young consumers. Topics include the Consumer Protection Act 2019 and its rules, the 2019 Act compared with the 1986 Act, dispute-redressal machinery, e-commerce, dark patterns, consumer mediation, advertising norms, related laws on legal metrology, food safety and essential commodities, and landmark judgments. It also covers the Central Consumer Protection Authority, the National Consumer Helpline, e-Jagriti and the Bureau of Indian Standards.

Rules from the official page

WhoAll Indian citizens. Employees of NLU Delhi and MyGov, and certain family members, are not eligible.
FormatUp to 15 questions within 300 seconds. The quiz starts when you click Play Quiz.
MarkingNo negative marking.
EntriesOne entry per participant. Submitted entries cannot be withdrawn.
Dates25 September 2026, 2:00 PM to 25 November 2026, 11:45 PM.

What are the prizes?

  • First prize: ₹10,000
  • Second prize: ₹7,000
  • Third prize: ₹3,000
  • 10 consolation prizes: ₹1,000 each
  • E-certificate for all participants

Per the terms, winners are picked by an automated random draw from a pool of the highest scorers who submit within the time limit. Scoring high gets you into the pool but does not decide the prize tier. Entering does not guarantee a prize. Unfair means, impersonation or double participation make an entry void.

How to play: step by step

Checked on the live MyGov quiz pages on 19 October 2026.

  1. Open the official quiz page: quiz.mygov.in/quiz/national-quiz-competition-on-consumer-protection-law-2026. The page shows 15 questions, 300 seconds and the end date (25 November 2026, 11:45 PM).
  2. Tap Login with MyGov AUTH to Play Quiz or Login with OTP to Play Quiz.
  3. New to MyGov: register with MyGov Auth or with DigiLocker (MeriPehchaan single sign-on). Already registered: enter your email or mobile number and tap Request OTP.
  4. Enter the OTP sent to your email/mobile to log in.
  5. Read the terms, fill your profile details if asked, then click Play Quiz - the 300-second timer starts immediately.
  6. Answer the 15 questions and submit. Prizes are per the official page (see above) and every participant gets an e-certificate.

Steps 5-6 happen inside your own login, so they are not shown as screenshots.
