Colgate Keep India Smiling Scholarship 2026-27: Rs 75,000 for BDS and MDS, apply by 30 November

By AiCoN | Published 25 October 2026 | Sources rechecked 15 November 2026

Colgate-Palmolive (India)'s Keep India Smiling programme offers one-time support of Rs 75,000 for dental students. The live Buddy4Study listing now shows 30 November 2026 for both BDS and MDS applications, with Apply Now controls. It is a selection-based corporate scholarship, not a government scheme.

Deadline update, 15 November 2026

The actual browser page now shows 30 November for both courses and an active programme banner. This supersedes the 1 November BDS and 19 October MDS dates in our earlier version. A text-only copy still returned those old dates, so we used the current rendered application-partner page. We did not log in, fill a form or submit an application.

Who can apply

  • Indian nationals enrolled in BDS (any year) or MDS at a government or private college or institute in India.
  • At least 65% in Class 12. BDS students in years 2, 3 and 4 need at least 60% in their last semester; MDS students need above 60% overall in BDS.
  • Annual family income below Rs 8 lakh.
  • Children of employees of Buddy4Study and Colgate India are not eligible.
  • Applicants from Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh get preference. This is not a restriction to those states.

How to apply

  1. Open the official application-partner listing on Buddy4Study.
  2. Choose the BDS or MDS section that matches your course. Check its 30-Nov-2026 date and read View Details.
  3. Use Apply Now and sign in or register in your own account. Read the form's current eligibility, document and declaration requirements before entering personal details.
  4. Review everything before submission and keep the confirmation. We have not verified the form behind login.

Stay safe

There is no application fee. Never pay anyone who promises selection. Use the application partner linked by Colgate, not a lookalike page or an agent. This is a one-time award and selection is not guaranteed.

Sources: Colgate's programme page and Buddy4Study, its application partner. Current rendered listing checked 15 November 2026. Independent AiCoN guide, not a Colgate publication.
