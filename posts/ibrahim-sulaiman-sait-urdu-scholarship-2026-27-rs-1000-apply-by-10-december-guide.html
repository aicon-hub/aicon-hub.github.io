Ibrahim Sulaiman Sait Urdu Scholarship 2026-27: Rs 1,000 for A+ in Urdu, apply online by 10 December

By Muhammed Salman | Published 11 November 2026 | Sources checked 11 November 2026

Kerala's Minority Welfare Department has opened the Ibrahim Sulaiman Sait Urdu Scholarship Award 2026-27. It pays a one-time Rs 1,000 to students who scored A+ in all subjects with Urdu in the SSLC or Higher Secondary examinations. Apply online by 10 December 2026.

Good to knowThe dates differ slightly between documents: the official guideline sets 10 December 2026 as the last date for online application, while the portal's scheme window shows the row closing 12 December 2026, 11:59:59 PM. Treat 10 December as your deadline. After applying, give the printout and documents to your school head by 12 December; the head approves online by 15 December.

Who can apply

  • Students who are permanent residents of Kerala
  • Students who secured A+ in all subjects in the SSLC examination with Urdu as the First Language
  • Students who secured A+ in all subjects in the Higher Secondary examination with Urdu as the Second Language
  • Students receiving other scholarships are also eligible

What you get

  • Rs 1,000 as a one-time award

How to apply: step by step

From the official English guideline (MWD/428/2026-S), read on 11 November 2026. No registration or application was tested.

  1. Open www.mwdscholarship.kerala.gov.in and click One-time registration.
  2. Register with the student's name, Aadhaar and phone number, then log in.
  3. Complete the profile: personal details, bank account details and the studying institution's details. Then add educational qualifications through Add Qualification.
  4. Click Apply For Scholarship and select this scholarship.
  5. Upload the photo, signature, SSLC certificate and bank passbook, then submit the application.
  6. After submitting, track the application under Submitted Applications. Give the printed application and documents to your school head by 12 December 2026.

Documents to keep ready

  • Printed copy of the online application registration form
  • Attested copy of the mark list (SSLC or Higher Secondary / Plus Two)
  • Copy of the first page of the bank passbook in the applicant's own name, clearly showing the applicant's name, account number, branch code, IFSC code and branch address

Dates in the guideline

  • Online application submission: 10 December 2026
  • Printout and documents to the Head of Institution: 12 December 2026
  • Head of Institution's online scrutiny and approval: 15 December 2026

The portal's scheme table lists this scholarship as open from 10 November 2026, 10:00 AM to 12 December 2026, 11:59:59 PM. The school head must verify documents and bank details and approve online in time; the guideline says the department is not responsible if a wrong bank detail gets the amount rejected or delayed. Verified hard copies stay at the institution for inspection by the Directorate of Minority Welfare. Bank and personal records should go only through the official portal or authorised institution staff.
