Chrome Music Lab: music experiments in your browser, no account needed

By AiCoN | Published 20 October 2026 | Sources checked 20 October 2026

Chrome Music Lab says you can open any experiment and start playing, with no account. It is built to make learning music more accessible.

What it is

Chrome Music Lab is a website of music experiments. Its About page says it "makes learning music more accessible through fun, hands-on experiments". The site's own description says "Music is for everyone. Play with simple experiments that let anyone, of any age, explore how music works." It runs in a web browser. It is not an AI tool. The Chrome Music Lab site is by Google, per its GitHub project and its Chrome Experiments address.

Is it free?

No page we read says the word "free" about using the experiments. What the About page says is "Do I need to make an account? Nope. Just open any experiment and start playing." We saw no price and no sign-in step. So we can say it needs no account, and we saw no charge, but we cannot quote the site saying "free".

What the About page says

  • Teachers use it to explore music and its connections to science, math, art and more, and combine it with dance and live instruments.
  • The Song Maker experiment lets you "make and share your own songs".
  • The experiments are built with web technology such as the Web Audio API, WebMIDI and Tone.js.
  • The code for many of the experiments is open source on GitHub. The chrome-music-lab project lists the Apache-2.0 licence.
  • You can use it on "phones, tablets, laptops" in a web browser such as Chrome. The page also says "For the best experience, view it on Chrome".

Our advice

  • Good for children and for anyone curious about how sound and music work, with no setup. Try Song Maker first, as it is the one the About page names.
  • If an experiment does not load, try Chrome, as the page suggests.

Privacy and data

  • We read no privacy statement on the pages we checked and make no claim on what data it collects. The site loads a Google Tag Manager script, so visits may be tracked. We did not check how shared songs are stored.

India

  • No region limit appears on the pages we read. We did not test it from India. There is no price to convert.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No, per the About page. You open an experiment and start playing.

Can I make my own songs?

Yes, with Song Maker, per the About page.

Does it work on a phone?

The page says phones, tablets and laptops work through a web browser. We did not test it.
