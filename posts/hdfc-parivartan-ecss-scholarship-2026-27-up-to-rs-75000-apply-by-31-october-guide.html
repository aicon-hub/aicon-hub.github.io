HDFC Parivartan ECSS Scholarship 2026-27: up to Rs 75,000 for Class 1 to PG students, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 22 October 2026 | Sources checked 22 October 2026

Students from Class 1 to postgraduate level can apply for the Parivartan Educational Crisis Scholarship Support (ECSS) Programme 2026-27, a merit-cum-need scholarship by HDFC Bank under its CSR programme Parivartan. Support ranges from Rs 15,000 to Rs 75,000, with preference for students whose families have faced a personal or financial crisis in the past three years. Applications close on Saturday, 31 October 2026 on the programme's official site.

Who can apply

  • Indian nationals studying in Classes 1 to 12, or in diploma, ITI, polytechnic, undergraduate or postgraduate courses in recognized institutions in India. For diploma courses, only students pursuing a diploma after Class 12 are eligible.
  • At least 55% marks in the previous qualifying examination.
  • Annual family income of Rs 2,50,000 or less.
  • Preference is given to applicants facing a personal or family crisis in the past three years - such as loss of an earning family member, job loss, self-employment failure, a medical emergency or a natural disaster - that puts them at risk of dropping out.

Amount by course level

  • Classes 1 to 6: Rs 15,000.
  • Classes 7 to 12, diploma, ITI and polytechnic: Rs 18,000.
  • General undergraduate courses (BCom, BSc, BA and similar): Rs 30,000.
  • Professional undergraduate courses (BTech, MBBS, LLB, B.Arch, nursing and similar): Rs 50,000.
  • General postgraduate courses (MCom, MA and similar): Rs 35,000.
  • Professional postgraduate courses (MTech, MBA and similar): Rs 75,000.

Key dates

  • Applications for 2026-27 are open now on the official programme site.
  • Deadline: Saturday, 31 October 2026, for the school, undergraduate and postgraduate categories.

Before you apply: crisis proof

Crisis proof is optional but strengthens the application. The programme accepts a death certificate for loss of an earning family member, an official employer letter for job loss, a notarised affidavit for self-employment failure, a medical report or prescription from a certified doctor for illness or medical emergency, or an official government document confirming impact of a natural disaster.

How to apply: step by step

From the official programme page and the live application page, checked 22 October 2026.

  1. Start from the official programme site, parivartanecss.com, and click "Apply Now". The button routes to the programme's official application page, run with its application partner Buddy4Study.
  2. Register on Buddy4Study with your email, mobile number or Google account, or log in if you already have an account.
  3. Open "Start Application" for the Parivartan ECSS Scholarship Programme 2026-27 and fill the online form with accurate details.
  4. Upload the documents: a passport-size photograph, previous year's marksheets, a government-issued identity proof, current-year admission proof (fee receipt, admission letter, institution ID card or bonafide certificate), bank account details (copy of passbook or cancelled cheque), income proof (from the Gram Panchayat, Ward Counsellor or Sarpanch, or from the SDM, DM, CO or Tehsildar, or an affidavit), and crisis proof where applicable. For school students, the bank account can be in the applicant's or a parent's name.
  5. Accept the terms and conditions, check the Preview carefully, then click Submit.

Registration and the application form sit behind a Buddy4Study login, so we could not screenshot the form's inner pages. The steps above follow the sponsor's published application process.
