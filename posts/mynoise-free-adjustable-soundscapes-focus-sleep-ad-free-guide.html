myNoise: free adjustable soundscapes for focus and sleep

By AiCoN | Published 17 October 2026 | Sources checked 17 October 2026

myNoise is an ad-free site of adjustable soundscapes for focus and sleep. Its home page says an extensive library is free and the site runs on donations.

What it is

myNoise is a website of adjustable soundscapes for focus, relaxation and sleep. Its home page says it gives you "adjustable soundscapes that adapt to your space and your needs", including nature sounds, ambient music and noise textures. It says the website is ad-free and respects user privacy. It is run by one person, sound engineer Stéphane Pigeon, per the site. Its FAQ says the audio does not use AI: nature sounds are recorded in the field and musical soundscapes are played by real musicians. We included it as a free way to mask noise or wind down.

Is it free?

Mostly yes, per the site. The home page says there is an "extensive library available for free" and that the site "operates entirely on donations". It also asks for support: a recurring $5 a month (about Rs 484) or a one-time donation from $10 to $30 (about Rs 968 to Rs 2,905), which gives lifetime all-access membership. The page also says patrons can access their favorite soundscapes from a Favorites section. The site does not list in what other ways free access differs from patron access, so we cannot say exactly what a free user misses.

What you can do with it

  • Focus. Sounds such as "Irish Coast" and "Cafe Restaurant", described on the home page as masking distractions.
  • Relax and sleep. The home page has sections for meditation and relaxation, and sleep.
  • Adjust the sound. The home page says the soundscapes are adjustable. Press H on a generator page for a help pop-up, per the FAQ.
  • Browse the library. The home page points to a Full Index page for the other sounds available for free.

What to watch out for

  • Personal use only. The FAQ says the sound generators are for personal and private use only, and cannot be used in your own projects without licensing.
  • Free vs patron. Favorites need a patron login, per the home page. We did not check other differences.
  • Not a medical tool. The site pitches focus, relaxation and sleep, not treatment. We did not verify any health claims.
  • Not tested. We did not listen to any soundscape or log in.

Privacy and data

  • The home page says the site is ad-free and respects user privacy.
  • The FAQ says it anonymously shares connection statistics with Clicky, a GDPR-compliant alternative to Google Analytics.
  • It says myNoise never uses your patron email address for marketing or shares it with others.
  • It says some references on myNoise are Amazon affiliate links, so the site earns a commission if you buy through them.

India

  • Donations are in dollars. We converted at about Rs 96.83 per US dollar. The site mentions no country limits. We did not test it from India.
