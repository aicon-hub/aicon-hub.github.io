CryptPad: encrypted shared documents you can use without an account

By AiCoN | Published 9 November 2026 | Sources checked 9 November 2026

CryptPad is a set of online tools for working together on documents. Its site describes it as an encrypted, open source collaboration suite. It is an option for group assignments or shared teaching material when you do not want to depend on a big company account.

Quick facts

  • What it is: "Collaboration suite, encrypted and open-source", as the CryptPad site title says. The page asks you to turn on JavaScript because the encryption runs in your browser.
  • Guest use: the documentation says guests, who are not registered, get access to all applications and can share and collaborate on documents.
  • Guest limit: storage is limited to 3 months of inactivity, counted per document. File storage for images, videos and PDFs is not available to guests, and calendar access is limited.
  • Free account: the documentation says registering needs only a username and a password, no personal information. Logged-in users get permanent personal storage, file storage, folders and tags, teams, calendars, and more control over documents such as passwords and expiry dates.
  • Premium: the documentation says there is a premium option on cryptpad.fr only, with more storage and a higher upload limit. We did not find the prices or the free storage quota on the pages we read.
  • Checked by AiCoN: we read the CryptPad site and its user account documentation on 9 November 2026. We did not create an account or a document.

Who is it for?

Students working on a group assignment, or teachers who want to share notes or forms with people who do not have a particular company account.

How to start

  1. Open cryptpad.fr and pick an app, such as a document or a spreadsheet, as a guest.
  2. Share the link with your group.
  3. If you want permanent storage, register with a username and a password. Write the password down: for an encrypted service, a lost password may mean lost documents. Check the CryptPad help for how recovery works.

Limits and things to know

  • AiCoN did not use CryptPad, so we cannot say how it compares in speed or features with other editors.
  • A guest document can expire after 3 months of inactivity. Keep a copy of anything important.
  • Anyone with the link can usually open a shared document, so share links with care.
  • The free storage quota is not confirmed. Check the CryptPad site before you store large files.

Sources: CryptPad · CryptPad user account documentation. Independent guide by AiCoN - not affiliated with CryptPad. Checked 9 November 2026. Details can change; check the official source before you act.
