Veer Gatha 6.0 (2026-27): schools upload best entries by 31 October, national winners get Rs 10,000

By AiCoN | Published 23 October 2026 | Sources checked 23 October 2026

Submissions for Veer Gatha Project 6.0 (2026-27) are open on the MyGov portal until Saturday, 31 October 2026. The Ministry of Education and Ministry of Defence project asks school students from Grade 3 to Grade 12 to create poems, paragraphs, essays, paintings, drawings, multimedia presentations or short videos about gallantry award winners and the warriors of India. Students do not submit directly: each school conducts the activities, shortlists its best entries and uploads up to four entries by the deadline. National winners - the Super 100 - receive a cash prize of Rs 10,000 each from the Ministry of Defence, with district- and state-level winners too.

Who can take part

  • Students of all schools in India, in three categories: Category 1 - Preparatory Stage (Grades 3 to 5); Category 2 - Middle Stage (Grades 6 to 8); Category 3 - Secondary Stage (Grades 9 to 12, judged separately for Grades 9-10 and Grades 11-12).
  • Students of CBSE-affiliated schools located abroad also take part - their entries are uploaded online after evaluation at the school level.
  • Entries reach the portal through schools, not individual registrations: the school conducts the activities, evaluates them and uploads the best entries to the MyGov portal.

What to create

  • Category 1 (Grades 3-5): a poem, a paragraph of up to 150 words, or a painting/drawing.
  • Category 2 (Grades 6-8): a poem, a paragraph of up to 300 words, a painting/drawing, or a multi-media presentation / short video (anchoring, storytelling or reporting of war).
  • Category 3 (Grades 9-12): a poem, an essay of up to 750 words, a painting/drawing, or a multi-media presentation / short video.
  • Suggestive topics include: a gallantry award winner as your role model and the values learnt from their life; how you would keep the memory of a gallantry award winner who made the supreme sacrifice alive; and the spirit and military strategies of warriors of India such as Prithviraj Chauhan, King Kharavela of Kalinga, Shivaji, or the warriors of the 1857 Mutiny and tribal uprisings.
  • The official page points schools to gallantryawards.gov.in for the stories of the brave-hearts, and to NCERT's book on Paramvir Chakra awardees (PDF).

How it runs, and key dates

  • Schools conduct the activities at school level and shortlist one best entry per category, up to four entries per school (Category 3 has separate slots for Grades 9-10 and 11-12; smaller schools follow the breakup given on the official page).
  • School uploads on the MyGov portal: 9 October to 31 October 2026. The official note asks schools not to wait for the last date - upload as soon as the best entries are shortlisted.
  • District-level evaluation: 4 to 20 November 2026.
  • State/UT-level evaluation: 21 November to 7 December 2026.
  • National-level evaluation: 8 to 28 December 2026; results reach the Ministry of Defence by 30 December 2026.

Winners and prizes

  • National level: 100 winners (Super 100) - 25 from Category 1, 25 from Category 2, and 50 from Category 3 (25 each for Grades 9-10 and 11-12). Each national winner gets a cash prize of Rs 10,000 from the Ministry of Defence and a certificate given jointly by the Ministry of Defence and the Ministry of Education. National winners of earlier Veer Gatha editions are not included in the Super 100.
  • State/UT level: 8 winners per State/UT (two from each category), excluding Super 100 students, felicitated by the State/UT.
  • District level: 4 winners per district (one from each category), felicitated by the district.

How to take part

  1. Students: do the project through your school now. The school-level activities and shortlisting happen before the portal deadline, so finish your poem, paragraph, essay, painting, drawing or video this week and give it to your teacher. There is no individual upload on the portal.
  2. Schools: after shortlisting the best entry in each category, upload them (up to four entries) on the MyGov portal at innovateindia.mygov.in/veer-gatha-6 by 31 October 2026. The page carries the "Submit your Entry" option and the evaluation rubrics (Annexure I) and state-wise entry numbers (Annexure II).
