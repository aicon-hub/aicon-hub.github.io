Janjatiya Gaurav Divas Quiz 2026 on MyGov: e-certificate, ends 15 November

By AiCoN | Published 16 October 2026 | Sources checked 16 October 2026

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs and MyGov list a Quiz on Janjatiya Gaurav Divas 2026, open to all Indian citizens. The listed reward is an e-certificate for every participant. The listed dates are 16 October to 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM.

Availability warningThere is no cash prize listed, only the e-certificate. The MyGov page showed "Closed" when we opened it without logging in, so confirm that it opens for you. The ministry and MyGov can amend or cancel the quiz.

Quiz details

  • Dates: 16 October 2026, 12:00 AM to 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM
  • 10 questions in 300 seconds. The timer starts when you click Play Quiz
  • Topics: India's tribal communities, their cultural traditions, history, indigenous knowledge, art forms, festivals and tribal freedom fighters
  • Reward: e-certificate for all participants

Rules to read

  • Open to all Indian citizens. One entry per person
  • Keep your MyGov profile accurate. The ministry uses it to contact you, and missing details can void the entry
  • Staff connected with organising the quiz, and their immediate family, are not eligible
  • Unfair means, impersonation or double participation make the entry void. The ministry's decision is final

How to play

  1. Open the quiz page on quiz.mygov.in
  2. Follow the login instructions shown on MyGov. Do not share your login code with anyone.
  3. Click Play Quiz and answer within 5 minutes

We read the quiz page and all 13 terms on 16 October 2026. We did not take the quiz.
