Janjatiya Gaurav Divas Quiz 2026 on MyGov: e-certificate, ends 15 November

By AiCoN | Published 16 October 2026 | Sources checked 16 October 2026

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs and MyGov list a Quiz on Janjatiya Gaurav Divas 2026, open to all Indian citizens. The listed reward is an e-certificate for every participant. The listed dates are 16 October to 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM.

Availability warningThere is no cash prize listed, only the e-certificate. When we first checked, the MyGov page showed "Closed" without logging in; re-checked on 19 October 2026, the page now shows both login options and the end date 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM, so it appears open. The ministry and MyGov can amend or cancel the quiz.

Quiz details

  • Dates: 16 October 2026, 12:00 AM to 15 November 2026, 11:45 PM
  • 10 questions in 300 seconds. The timer starts when you click Play Quiz
  • Topics: India's tribal communities, their cultural traditions, history, indigenous knowledge, art forms, festivals and tribal freedom fighters
  • Reward: e-certificate for all participants

Rules to read

  • Open to all Indian citizens. One entry per person
  • Keep your MyGov profile accurate. The ministry uses it to contact you, and missing details can void the entry
  • Staff connected with organising the quiz, and their immediate family, are not eligible
  • Unfair means, impersonation or double participation make the entry void. The ministry's decision is final

How to play: step by step

Checked on the live MyGov quiz pages on 19 October 2026.

  1. Open the official quiz page: quiz.mygov.in/quiz/quiz-on-janjatiya-gaurav-divas-2026. The page shows 10 questions, 300 seconds and the end date (15 November 2026, 11:45 PM).
  2. Tap Login with MyGov AUTH to Play Quiz or Login with OTP to Play Quiz.
  3. New to MyGov: register with MyGov Auth or with DigiLocker (MeriPehchaan single sign-on). Already registered: enter your email or mobile number and tap Request OTP. Do not share your OTP with anyone.
  4. Enter the OTP sent to your email/mobile to log in.
  5. Read the terms, fill your profile details if asked, then click Play Quiz - the 300-second timer starts immediately.
  6. Answer the 10 questions and submit. Everyone gets an e-certificate in their MyGov dashboard; there is no cash prize for this quiz.

Steps 5-6 happen inside your own login, so they are not shown as screenshots.
