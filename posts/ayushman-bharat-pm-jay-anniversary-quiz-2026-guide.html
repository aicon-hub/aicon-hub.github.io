Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY anniversary quiz: win up to ₹10,000, ends 15 October

By AiCoN | Published 8 October 2026 | Sources checked 8 October 2026

The National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, and MyGov are running the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Anniversary Quiz 2026 to mark eight years of the scheme. It is a 10-question, 300-second quiz open to all Indian citizens. First prize is ₹10,000. The quiz ends on 15 October 2026 at 11:59 PM.

Check before you playThe MyGov quiz page we read from a logged-out browser showed a "Closed" label next to the dates, even though the quiz list and the dates (1 to 15 October 2026) show it as running. Open the quiz page yourself and confirm it lets you start. The quiz is timed and allows one attempt per mobile number or email ID.

What is the quiz?

AB PM-JAY was launched in September 2018 as a health assurance scheme. To commemorate its 8th anniversary, NHA invites citizens to test what they know about the scheme, its key features and benefits, its impact on healthcare delivery and its role in access to affordable healthcare.

Quiz rules from the official page

WhoAll Indian citizens.
Format10 questions in 300 seconds, randomly picked from a question bank.
MarkingNo negative marking.
RegistrationValid mobile number or email ID. The same number or email cannot be used more than once.
Dates1 October 2026, 12:00 AM to 15 October 2026, 11:59 PM.

Keep your MyGov profile accurate, since it is used for further communication.

What are the prizes?

  • First prize: ₹10,000
  • Second prize: ₹7,500
  • Third prize: ₹5,000
  • Next 10 appreciation prizes: ₹1,000 each

NHA will disburse the prize money to the selected winners. Entering does not guarantee a prize.

A careful checklist

  1. Open quiz.mygov.in and find the quiz yourself.
  2. Read the Terms and Conditions on the quiz page, including how winners are chosen.
  3. Sign in, update your MyGov profile, and start only when you have 5 uninterrupted minutes.
  4. Finish before 15 October 2026, 11:59 PM.

We did not log in or play the quiz. The prize selection method is in the full Terms and Conditions, which we only read in part.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an entry fee?

The page we read does not mention one.

Can I play more than once?

The terms say the same mobile number or email ID cannot be used more than once.
