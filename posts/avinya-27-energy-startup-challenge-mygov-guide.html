Avinya'27 Energy Startup Challenge: prizes up to ₹3 lakh, apply by 15 October

By AiCoN | Published 9 October 2026 | Sources checked 9 October 2026

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas is running Avinya'27, an energy startup challenge on MyGov's Innovate India portal. The organiser is the Federation of Indian Petroleum Industry (FIPI). The top three winners get cash prizes of ₹3 lakh, ₹2 lakh and ₹1 lakh. Applications close on 15 October 2026 at 11:59 PM IST.

Check before you applyOnly DPIIT-recognised startups can apply, and a startup must be at most 10 years old (as of 1 January 2027) with turnover under ₹100 crore. Apply on either Innovate India or Startup India, not both. The organisers may change the rules, prizes and timelines at their discretion.

What is Avinya'27?

Avinya is a startup competition for the energy sector. The official page says it follows three earlier editions. Winning startups present at India Energy Week 2027, scheduled for January 2027.

Themes

  • Hydrocarbon value chain: oil and gas (upstream, midstream, downstream) and coal gasification
  • Alternative fuels: biofuels, green hydrogen, green ammonia
  • Low carbon technology: CCUS, e-mobility, batteries, energy storage
  • Renewable energy: solar, hydro, wind, geothermal
  • Functional areas: operations, HSE, digital, AI and ML, trading, energy efficiency, capability building, sustainable financing, Industry 4.0

Eligibility from the official page

WhoCitizens of India. Ideation and early-stage startups (including bootstrapped, pre-seed and Series A to C).
RecognitionDPIIT-recognised under Startup India.
Age and sizeAt most 10 years from incorporation (as of 1 January 2027) and turnover below ₹100 crore.
Not eligibleAn entity formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business.
EntriesOne per participant, on one platform only.
Deadline15 October 2026, 11:59 PM IST. Late or incomplete entries may be disqualified.

What do winners get?

Cash prizes for the top 3: ₹3 lakh, ₹2 lakh and ₹1 lakh. The top 5 winners also get air travel and accommodation support for India Energy Week 2027, felicitation, an exhibition space and other non-cash benefits listed on the page, some of them "potential". The top 10 startups get certificates, visibility through FIPI channels and a jury interaction.

How are winners chosen?

Round 1: all applications are screened on set evaluation criteria and the top 10 go to a pitch round. Round 2: those 10 present to a jury, which selects the top 5 winners. The page does not publish the evaluation criteria or say how the top 3 prize order is decided.

Update: applications are closed

Checked 19 October 2026: the official page now shows SUBMISSION CLOSED - applications ran 21 August to 15 October 2026 (11:59 PM IST) and no new entries are possible. The competition itself continues with evaluation and winner selection; the timeline below stays for those who applied. This guide is kept for reference.

A careful checklist

  1. Confirm your DPIIT recognition and incorporation date.
  2. Pick one theme and read the full page and terms.
  3. Apply on only one portal, Innovate India or Startup India.
  4. Submit before 15 October 2026, 11:59 PM IST, and use an email you check, since all correspondence goes there.

We did not apply or log in. We read the full challenge page and terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is a finished product needed?

The page says concept-note stage ideas are welcome.

What if I cannot be reached after winning?

The terms say a winner who cannot be contacted within three working days of the announcement may forfeit the prize.
