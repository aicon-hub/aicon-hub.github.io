Kerala ration card online: new card, corrections and member changes on the citizen portal

By AiCoN | Published 29 October 2026 | Sources checked 29 October 2026

Most ration card work in Kerala no longer needs an office visit. The Civil Supplies Department's e-Citizen portal lets you register once and then apply for a new ration card, add or remove family members, correct details, order a duplicate card, or transfer your card across taluks and states - from your phone or computer. Card details and application status can be checked without even logging in. We verified the portal on 29 October 2026.

Quick facts

  • The portal: civilsupplieskerala.gov.in is the Department of Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs' official site; the citizen e-services run at ecitizen.civilsupplieskerala.gov.in (Ration Card Management System, built by NIC), available statewide since 4 August 2018.
  • Scale: the department's home page shows about 96 lakh ration cards, 13,927 ration shops and over 1.78 crore e-service accounts, across five card types including AAY and priority (PHH).
  • What you can do after login: per the department's application forms page - new ration card, inclusion of new members, corrections, duplicate card, removing members, changing the card owner, and transferring the card or its members to another taluk or state. A separate "Give-up ration" link handles surrendering a card online.
  • Without login: Rationcard Details and Application Status are public checks on etso.civilsupplieskerala.gov.in - just a captcha, no account. Status messages and print passwords also arrive through the Sandes mobile app.
  • On your phone: the official "Ente Ration Card" app (Android and iOS) is linked from the department home page.
  • Prefer assisted service: the same system has an Akshaya login, so any Akshaya centre can file these for you.
  • Grievances: a dedicated portal at pg.civilsupplieskerala.gov.in, plus help desk 0471-2322155 and toll-free 1967, or rcmskerala@gmail.com.
  • Keep buying: the department publishes a list of AAY/priority cards moved to non-priority for not buying ration for three straight months - and ration shops currently work 9 am to 12 noon and 4 pm to 7 pm.

How to start

  1. Open ecitizen.civilsupplieskerala.gov.in and choose Citizen, then "Create an Account" to register.
  2. Sign in with your User ID and password and pick the service you need - new card, member changes, corrections, duplicate or transfer.
  3. Track everything without logging in: use Application Status on etso.civilsupplieskerala.gov.in, or the Sandes app for status messages and print passwords.
  4. For problems, file at pg.civilsupplieskerala.gov.in or call 1967 (toll-free) / 0471-2322155.

The site works in Malayalam and English - the language toggle is at the top of civilsupplieskerala.gov.in.
