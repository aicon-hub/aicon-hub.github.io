Kaggle Learn: free short courses in Python, data and AI

By AiCoN | Published 17 October 2026 | Sources checked 17 October 2026

Kaggle Learn lists short courses in Python, machine learning and AI ethics. Its page says the courses are provided at no cost and that you can now earn certificates.

What it is

Kaggle Learn is a set of short courses on programming, data science and AI, run by Kaggle. Its Learn page says: "We pare down complex topics to their key practical components, so you gain usable skills in a few hours (instead of weeks or months)." The page lists courses such as Intro to Programming (Python, "if you have no coding experience"), Intro to Machine Learning, Pandas, Data Visualization, Intro to SQL, Intro to Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Data Cleaning and Intro to AI Ethics. It also lists guides, including 5-day courses on generative AI and AI agents "in partnership with Google". This is an AI and data skills resource, not an AI tool you chat with.

Is it free?

Yes, per the page: "The courses are provided at no cost to you, and you can now earn certificates." The page does not say whether certificates cost anything or what is needed to earn one, and we did not find that on the pages we read.

What you can do with it

  • Start from zero. Intro to Programming is for people with no coding experience, per its description.
  • Learn AI basics. Intro to Machine Learning, Intro to Deep Learning, Computer Vision and Intro to AI Ethics are on the list.
  • Pick a short guide. The Guides section lists self-paced collections, including generative AI and AI agents courses in partnership with Google.

What to watch out for

  • It is aimed at people who want to code. Most courses use Python, SQL, TensorFlow or R. They are not for casual app use.
  • Account. Kaggle's Terms say you "may be required to sign up for an account". We did not check whether the courses can be opened without signing in, and we did not take a course.
  • Certificates. The page says you can earn them, but gives no details. Check the course page before counting on one.

Privacy and data

  • Kaggle's privacy policy says that when you create an account you provide your name and a password, and that you may provide an address, phone number or other personal information. It says that when you are not signed in, it still stores information tied to your browser, app or device.
  • The Learn page says Kaggle uses cookies from Google to deliver its services and to analyze traffic.
  • The Terms say children who are not of legal age to enter a contract should read the children's privacy section and contact Kaggle Support about parental consent.

India

  • We found no region limit on the pages we read, and no rupee price is involved. We did not test access from India.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kaggle Learn free?

Yes, per its page: the courses are provided at no cost.

Do I need to know coding first?

Not for Intro to Programming, which says it is for people with no coding experience.

Can I get a certificate?

The page says you can now earn certificates. It gives no further details.
