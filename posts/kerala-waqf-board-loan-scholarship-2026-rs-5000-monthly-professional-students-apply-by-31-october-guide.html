Kerala Waqf Board Loan Scholarship 2026: Rs 5,000 a month interest-free, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

Scoped recheck: 24 October 2026. Current official eligibility, places, repayment wording and surety conditions checked. Application is offline; no submission tested.

The Kerala State Waqf Board is inviting applications for its interest-free Loan Scholarship under the Revolving Loan Scholarship Scheme. The current 2026-27 instructions limit applications to first-year professional-course students, require at least 80% in Plus Two or an equivalent grade and annual family income below Rs 2.5 lakh, and provide Rs 5,000 a month to 200 students selected on merit. The filled offline form must reach the Board's office in Kaloor, Kochi by 31 October 2026.

Good to knowThis is a loan, not a grant. It is interest-free if repaid as required, but the documents include a 12% default penalty and obligations for two sureties. The form must physically reach the Kochi office by 31 October 2026. The official PDF has three pages: the notice, current 2026-27 instructions, and the blank form. Its first two pages confirm the 80% mark threshold, income below Rs 2.5 lakh and 200 merit places. Our earlier text incorrectly called these press-only figures. The Board's separate scheme page still shows much older rates; use the current cycle's instructions.

Who can apply

The notice and 2026-27 instructions in the official application PDF say:

  • Muslim students studying in the first year of a professional course in 2026-27. This is not an all-years application call.
  • At least 80% marks in Plus Two / Class 12, or an equivalent grade.
  • Annual family income below Rs 2,50,000.
  • A copy of the Plus Two / Class 12 mark list must accompany the application.

What you get

The current instructions state Rs 5,000 per month and selection of 200 students on merit. This is an interest-free loan scholarship, not money you keep without repayment.

Repayment and surety

The current 2026-27 instructions require a Rs 200 stamp-paper agreement and two sureties after selection. All pages must be signed by the student and sureties.

  • The current instructions say repayment starts six months after completing the course and must finish within the following 24 months, without ordinary interest.
  • The separately linked agreement still contains 2025 dates. It instead starts repayment within three months of employment or engaging in a trade/profession, or six months after course completion, whichever is earlier. Confirm the agreement you are asked to sign with the Board; do not assume the later date applies.
  • The current instructions describe 12% compensation on sums not repaid within the stipulated period, subject to the Board's assessment. The older agreement says 12% penal interest on repayment default.
  • The current instructions describe an employed surety in government, a public-sector/autonomous body or a co-operative bank/society, with an alternative of an earning father, mother, brother or sister of the borrower. Two sureties are still required. The older sanction template describes employed sureties more narrowly, so ask the Board to confirm the combination it will accept. The general pack excludes an employed surety retiring within two years of the agreement period. Use the required employment/surety forms and confirm acceptance before arranging signatures.
  • The older agreement makes the sureties jointly and severally responsible. It allows the Board to demand the whole balance after three consecutive missed instalments, and immediate repayment if the award is forfeited, for example for false information or leaving the course.
  • The employment certificate includes consent to recover from a surety's salary, retirement/terminal benefits and other benefits. These are real financial obligations; read the final documents before signing.
  • The general pack asks witnesses to be the president, secretary or treasurer of the concerned Jama'ath, with their name, address, role and Jama'ath seal.

The sample sanction letter in that pack is dated 5 January 2026 for a 2025 award. Its 21 January 2026 attendance appointment and document checklist are not the current application's deadline or a new appointment. Follow your own sanction letter if selected.

How to apply: step by step

The Forms & Applications page and all three pages of the current application PDF were rechecked on 24 October 2026. No application was submitted.

  1. Open the Board's Forms & Applications page: keralastatewakfboard.in/forms.html. This public download page uses http. The application route is offline; do not enter personal information into a website claiming to be an online application for this call.
  2. Click "Loan scholarship - Application invited - Last date: 2026 October 31" to download the three-page notice, instructions and application PDF. Read the first two pages before filling the form on page 3.
  3. Print and fill the form: student and guardian details, annual income, qualifying exam marks, and the institution and course you joined. Attach a copy of your Plus Two / Class 12 mark list (the form asks for it).
  4. The student and the parent or guardian must both sign the declaration on the form.
  5. Post or deliver the form to: The Chief Executive Officer, Kerala State Waqf Board, V.I.P. Road, Kaloor, Kochi - 682017, so that it reaches by 31 October 2026.
  6. For questions, contact the Board: 0484-2342485 or ceokl@wakf.gov.in (from the Board's official letterhead).
