Kerala Waqf Board Loan Scholarship 2026: Rs 5,000 a month interest-free, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

The Kerala State Waqf Board is inviting applications for its interest-free Loan Scholarship under the Revolving Loan Scholarship Scheme. First-year Muslim students admitted to professional courses in 2026-27 get Rs 5,000 a month. The filled offline form must reach the Board's office in Kaloor, Kochi by 31 October 2026.

Good to knowThis is a loan scholarship, not a grant: you repay it without interest after your course. Repayment starts 6 months after course completion and must finish within 24 months. The application is fully offline and must physically REACH the Kochi office by 31 October 2026, so post it several days early. The Board's website still shows older monthly rates on its scheme page; the Rs 5,000 a month figure and this year's invitation were reported by Malayala Manorama and CIGI on 20 October 2026.

Who can apply

  • Muslim students who are Kerala residents
  • Admitted in 2026-27 to the first year of a professional course (engineering, medicine, BSc nursing, BPharm, MBA, MCA and similar programmes)
  • At least 80% marks or equivalent grade in Plus Two / Class 12
  • Annual family income below Rs 2.5 lakh

What you get

  • Rs 5,000 per month as an interest-free loan scholarship for the course period
  • 200 students are selected statewide, on merit, from the applications received

Repayment and surety

  • No interest. Repayment in instalments starts 6 months after you finish the course and must be completed within 24 months
  • Selected students sign an agreement on Rs 200 stamp paper with two sureties
  • One surety must be a person working in a government, semi-government, public sector, local self-government institution, co-operative bank or society; or an earning father, mother, brother or sister of the student

How to apply: step by step

Checked on the live Kerala State Waqf Board website on 21 October 2026.

  1. Open the Board's Forms & Applications page: keralastatewakfboard.in/forms.html. The site opens on http, not https - that is normal for this Board site.
  2. Click "Loan scholarship - Application invited - Last date: 2026 October 31" to download the application form (PDF).
  3. Print and fill the form: student and guardian details, annual income, qualifying exam marks, and the institution and course you joined. Attach a copy of your Plus Two / Class 12 mark list.
  4. The student and the parent or guardian must both sign the declaration on the form.
  5. Post or deliver the form to: The Chief Executive Officer, Kerala State Waqf Board, V.I.P. Road (near International Stadium), Kaloor, Kochi - 682017, so that it reaches by 31 October 2026. Late applications are not considered.
  6. For questions, call the Board: 0484-2101485 (also 0484-2342485, 0484-2346091).
