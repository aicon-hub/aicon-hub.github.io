Kerala Waqf Board Loan Scholarship 2026: Rs 5,000 a month interest-free, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 21 October 2026 | Sources checked 21 October 2026

The Kerala State Waqf Board is inviting applications for its interest-free Loan Scholarship under the Revolving Loan Scholarship Scheme. Muslim students admitted to professional courses get Rs 5,000 a month during the course period, under a Board decision dated 17 December 2025. The filled offline form must reach the Board's office in Kaloor, Kochi by 31 October 2026.

Good to knowThis is a loan scholarship, not a grant: you repay it without interest after your course. The application is fully offline and must physically REACH the Kochi office by 31 October 2026, so post it several days early. A note on figures: the Board's scheme page still shows older rates of Rs 1,500/1,250 a month marked under review, but the Board's current instruction pack, linked on the same forms page, carries a sanction-letter template and loan agreement stating Rs 5,000 a month under its decision of 17 December 2025. Press reports (Malayala Manorama and CIGI, 20 October 2026) mention 80% Plus Two marks, a Rs 2.5 lakh income limit and 200 seats; these thresholds do not appear anywhere on the Board's website, so treat them as reported, not confirmed. The official form does ask for your marks and annual income.

Who can apply

From the Board's website and official documents:

  • Muslim students; the scheme page describes the scheme as assistance to "poor and able students" and the agreement says "deserving Muslim students"
  • Students admitted to a professional course such as engineering, medicine, BSc nursing, BPharm, MBA, MCA or similar programmes
  • The application form asks for your qualifying examination marks or percentage and your annual family income

Reported by press on 20 October 2026 (Malayala Manorama and CIGI), but NOT stated anywhere on the Board's website:

  • At least 80% marks or equivalent grade in Plus Two / Class 12
  • Annual family income below Rs 2.5 lakh

What you get

  • Rs 5,000 per month as an interest-free loan scholarship for the course period, under the Board's decision dated 17 December 2025 (stated in the Board's own sanction-letter template and loan agreement)
  • Press reports say 200 students are selected statewide on merit; the Board's website does not state a seat count

Repayment and surety

From the Board's instruction pack and scholarship loan agreement:

  • No interest: the Board's scheme page says scholars "repay the amount without any interest by instalments"
  • Repay in 24 equal monthly instalments, starting within 3 months of getting employment or 6 months after completing the course, whichever is earlier
  • Default after the agreement period draws 12% penal interest on the amount due
  • Selected students sign an agreement on Rs 200 non-judicial stamp paper, with two sureties
  • Sureties must be employees of the Central or State Government, PSUs, statutory or autonomous bodies, co-operative banks or societies (per the Board's sanction-letter template)
  • Witnesses must be the president, secretary or treasurer of the concerned Jama'ath, with the Jama'ath seal

How to apply: step by step

Checked on the live Kerala State Waqf Board website on 21 October 2026.

  1. Open the Board's Forms & Applications page: keralastatewakfboard.in/forms.html. The site opens on http, not https - that is normal for this Board site.
  2. Click "Loan scholarship - Application invited - Last date: 2026 October 31" to download the application form (PDF).
  3. Print and fill the form: student and guardian details, annual income, qualifying exam marks, and the institution and course you joined. Attach a copy of your Plus Two / Class 12 mark list (the form asks for it).
  4. The student and the parent or guardian must both sign the declaration on the form.
  5. Post or deliver the form to: The Chief Executive Officer, Kerala State Waqf Board, V.I.P. Road, Kaloor, Kochi - 682017, so that it reaches by 31 October 2026.
  6. For questions, contact the Board: 0484-2342485 or ceokl@wakf.gov.in (from the Board's official letterhead).
