Evergreen.ai: free beta planning and India eligibility limits

By AiCoN | Published 8 October 2026 | Sources checked 8 October 2026

Evergreen.ai offers AI financial planning using information you provide and, optionally, linked financial accounts. Its current site promises free access through January 1, 2028 for people who register during the public beta. That offer is not proof of Indian account support or free human investment management.

Do not connect bank accounts just to try itAccount linking is optional. Start with general questions and review legal terms, supported institutions and data controls before sharing a real financial picture.

What is the service?

The named product is evergreen.ai, a financial-planning service whose site describes analysis of cash, accounts, spending, holdings and goals. It can help organise a question around your situation rather than answering only from generic assumptions.

That still depends on correct data and a clear question. Old balances, missing liabilities or wrong tax assumptions can change the answer. A confident AI response is not a guarantee that a decision is suitable.

The free offer has a condition

The current homepage says everyone who registers during the public beta gets free access through January 1, 2028. It says no credit card is required to start. This is a dated beta offer, not a promise of permanent free service or an unlimited allowance for every future feature.

The site separately says human investment-advisor services and investment management through Evergreen Wealth Advisors cost money. Ask for the exact fee and agreement before using those services. This guide did not register, inspect a personal account or verify post-beta pricing.

Optional account linking

The official security page says Plaid connects external financial institutions and that bank credentials go to Plaid or the bank, not to Evergreen's AI. It says linking is optional and users can begin with questions instead.

Optional does not mean risk-free. Confirm the scope of data access, how to disconnect, what remains stored and whether the specific bank is supported. Never paste bank passwords or verification codes into a planning conversation.

What privacy claims were checked?

The company says financial information is encrypted, not sold and not used to train public AI models. It describes isolated sessions, screened messages and activity records. Those are the company's current commitments, not an independent security audit by AiCoN.

The phrase public AI models does not settle every internal use, processor or retention period. Its linked privacy-policy article redirected to sign-in, and the readable Terms page pointed to a disclosure that was not available in the fetched text. Detailed legal and retention terms remain unverified here.

India and advice limits

The material is US-oriented and describes Plaid and US investment-advisor services. The checked pages do not establish Indian resident eligibility, Indian bank coverage or India-specific tax advice. Do not assume US account concepts or tax calculations transfer to India.

The site says it provides education, calculators and possibly personalised advice when given enough information. It expressly excludes legal, tax, insurance and estate-planning counsel and says it is not a recommendation to buy or sell a security. Get qualified help for consequential decisions.

A careful trial route

  1. Check the exact evergreen.ai domain; similarly named sites can be unrelated.
  2. Confirm eligibility and current beta terms through official support.
  3. Begin with a hypothetical or non-sensitive question, without linking accounts.
  4. Check the assumptions, calculations and dates in the answer.
  5. Read accessible privacy and legal documents before adding personal information.
  6. Before any paid human service or money movement, review the separate agreement and costs.

No account was created, no bank was connected and no financial transaction was made. This is a product guide, not financial advice or a tested recommendation.
