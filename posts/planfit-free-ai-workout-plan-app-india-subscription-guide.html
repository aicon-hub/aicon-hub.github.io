Planfit: a free AI workout plan, and what to check before subscribing

By AiCoN | Published 10 October 2026 | Sources checked 10 October 2026

Planfit is a workout app whose AI builds a plan around your goals and equipment. Planfit says the plan, guides and logging are free. A paid subscription exists, so check before you tap.

What it is

Planfit is a workout app for iPhone and Android. Planfit says its AI builds a plan from your fitness level, goals, schedule and the equipment you have, including bodyweight-only plans for home. It says the plan adjusts as you log workouts, and the app has exercise guides and videos.

Is it free

Yes. Planfit's page says the personalized plan is "completely free", with no credit card or payment needed to start. Planfit also says on its App Store listing that core features such as workout plans, guides and logging "will always remain free". The app does sell a subscription as an in-app purchase. The India App Store description lists premium real-time AI coaching, muscle recovery and exercise performance analysis, and Apple Watch integration, with a 7-day trial. Several in-app purchase entries show ₹199 monthly and ₹1,199 annually; another annual promotion shows ₹9,900. These are listed offers, not a verified checkout quote. Check the exact plan, trial eligibility, renewal date and final price in the app.

Is it available in India

Planfit has a live India App Store listing (Free, with in-app purchases) and a Google Play listing. We did not test it.

How to try it

  1. Install Planfit from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Answer the questions on your fitness level, goals, schedule and equipment.
  3. Start the first workout and log your sets.

Limits to know

  • Core features are free. A paid subscription exists, so read the screen carefully before you tap subscribe.
  • If you do subscribe, manage and cancel it through the store that billed you, not by uninstalling the app. The iOS listing says to cancel at least 24 hours before the subscription period ends and says payment starts after the trial. Check your renewal date and cancellation confirmation. Google Play also says uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
  • This is not medical advice. If you have an injury, a health condition or pain, talk to a doctor or a qualified trainer first.
  • Start light and check your form with the exercise guides.
  • The App Store privacy label says identifiers may be used to track you, and health/fitness information and other data may be linked to you. Review permissions before connecting health data. This is the developer disclosure, not an independent audit.
  • We did not test it ourselves.
