KERALA SCHOLARSHIP NOTICE

OBC medical and allied-course girls: official notice announces e-grantz reopening until 10 December

By Muhammed Salman | Published 20 November 2026 | Public access route checked 20 November 2026

Kerala's Information and Public Relations Department has announced a fresh application opportunity for the special financial assistance scheme for OBC girl students studying medical or medical-allied courses who have lost their mother, father or both parents. Its notice dated 19 November 2026 says e-grantz has been reopened until 10 December 2026. Previous beneficiaries are asked to renew through e-grantz.

This is an announcement and access guide, not a completed application test. We checked the official PRD notice, the public e-grantz notice board and the route to the blank student-login page. We did not sign in, create a student record, check a particular student's eligible schemes or submit an application. In-account availability was not checked by us.

Who does the new notice describe?

  • Girl students belonging to Kerala's OBC category.
  • Studying medical or medical-allied courses in government or government-aided institutions.
  • Having lost either parent or both parents. The notice uses an orphan-student description, but its stated condition includes losing only one parent.

This is not a general scholarship for every medical student or every OBC student. The brief reopening notice does not list every approved course, community, income condition, exclusion or document. Check the department's current scheme instructions and your institution before treating yourself as eligible.

Why do two different dates appear?

The newer top entry on the live e-grantz board, checked on 20 November, explicitly identifies the Special Scholarship 2026-27, says the portal is being reopened and gives 10 December 2026 as the last date. The older linked call lower down still displays 15 September. That older entry has not disappeared.

The PRD notice and the newer e-grantz entry now agree on 10 December. Read the newer reopening announcement rather than mistaking the old September date for the current announcement. This confirms what the department has announced, not whether a particular student can see or submit the scheme after signing in. No closing time was stated in the reopening notice we checked.

How to reach the official access route

  1. Read the 19 November PRD reopening notice and confirm the current announcement on the official e-grantz homepage.
  2. If you already have an e-grantz student account, use Student Login on that homepage. In our check, that button opened the blank student-login page, with email/Aadhaar and password fields. We stopped there.
  3. New applicants can review One Time Registration from the homepage. The public registration instructions describe completing registration, the five-part profile and qualifications, then viewing eligible schemes under the scholarship-application section. These are the portal's instructions, not steps we completed.
  4. Check the special scholarship's availability and requirements in your own account, or ask your institution or the department for help if it is absent. Do not pick a similarly named scheme merely to submit something.
  5. For previous beneficiaries, the PRD notice specifically says to renew through e-grantz. Confirm the correct renewal entry before proceeding, and retain the portal's acknowledgement if submission succeeds.

Before sharing personal information

The public registration page includes consent for Aadhaar-based identity verification and scholarship processing, including sharing with NSP and PFMS for disbursement. Review that consent and use the official portal. Do not send Aadhaar copies, passwords, parent death certificates or bank details to AiCoN or post them in a group.

Ask the department about the current document list before uploading. The older circular linked from the board is dated 2023 even though it appears under a 2026-27 notice. We have not treated its older benefit amounts or detailed conditions as a newly verified 2026-27 award promise.

What remains unverified?

  • The scheme list and application or renewal form visible inside an eligible student's account.
  • The exact award amount, current full eligibility rules and complete document list for this reopening.
  • Successful submission, acknowledgement, institutional verification or payment of assistance.
  • Any service charges if an applicant chooses a third-party assisted-application centre.

The PRD notice lists 0484-2983130 for enquiries. Ask the institution or the department if the login does not show the announced scheme. No fee, benefit or approval is promised by this guide.

Sources and scope

  • Kerala I&PRD, 19 November 2026, 20:41: reopening to 10 December, parent-loss condition, government/aided institution scope and renewal instruction.
  • e-grantz public notice board: newer 2026-27 reopening entry and the older September entry, checked 20 November.
  • Public one-time-registration instructions: general access steps and consent.
