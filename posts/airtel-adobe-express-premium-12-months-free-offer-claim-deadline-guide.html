Airtel offer: 12 months of Adobe Express Premium free, claim by 27 January 2027

By AiCoN | Published 11 October 2026 | Sources checked 11 October 2026

Airtel says active users get 12 months of Adobe Express Premium free. Adobe India lists redemption until 27 January 2027, through the Airtel Thanks app.

What it is

Adobe Express is a design app for social posts, videos, flyers, invitations and QR codes, with templates and Adobe Firefly AI features. Airtel is offering 12 months of its Premium plan to its customers. Airtel's page says Premium adds 250 monthly generative AI credits, unlimited premium templates, Adobe Stock assets, the full Adobe Fonts library, video background removal and one-click resizing.

Is it free

Yes, the promotional subscription is free for eligible active Airtel customers, but your Airtel service and internet use can still cost money. Airtel's page says Adobe Express Premium, "worth ₹4000", is free for 12 months for all active Airtel users, including prepaid, postpaid, Wi-Fi and DTH. Adobe's own India pricing page says the same offer runs for redemption from 28 January 2026 to 27 January 2027, and that your free year runs for 12 months from the day you activate it. The 27 January 2027 date is the activation deadline, not the end date for every subscriber. We did not confirm whether this promotion needs a payment method or renews after the free year. Adobe's pricing page has a general warning that ordinary free trials begin billing and subscriptions renew until cancelled; that is not proof of the Airtel promotion's renewal rule. Read the actual activation terms and check the displayed price, end date and cancellation route before confirming. Do not accidentally take a standard paid trial instead. Airtel's page says that if you lose Premium you keep your projects but some premium features and assets may become unavailable, and you can keep using the free version of Adobe Express.

Is it available in India

Yes, the public offer is for Airtel India users, including active prepaid, postpaid, Wi-Fi/broadband and DTH customers. Both official pages describe it. We did not verify eligibility in an individual Airtel account. Do not buy or change a telecom plan solely on this article; first check that your own benefits page shows the offer.

How to claim it

  1. Open the Airtel Thanks app and log in with your Airtel number.
  2. Open benefits, "Claim OTTs and more", Rewards or the "Discover Adobe Express Premium Offer" banner. Check the details shown for your account.
  3. Choose the Adobe account you want to keep using, sign in or create one, and follow the offer activation steps. Check that it is the complimentary 12-month offer and review any payment, renewal and cancellation terms before confirming.
  4. Install the Adobe Express app on Android or iOS, or use it in a desktop browser.

Limits and privacy to know

  • Generative credits reset monthly. Once used up, Airtel's page says you wait for the reset or buy more directly from Adobe.
  • Adobe says its current Firefly models were trained on licensed content, including Adobe Stock, and public domain content. Its official AI approach says it does not train Firefly on customer content and says customer data and content are not used to train generative AI models. This is not a promise of no cloud processing, no retention or no other data use. Check privacy and sharing settings, including the terms of any partner model you select.
  • The mobile app needs Android 10 or later, or iOS 17 or later. These are minimum OS versions, not a guarantee for every device. Airtel points unsupported devices to the desktop web version; some supported Android devices can also use a mobile browser.
  • AiCoN advice: do not upload private IDs or documents to a design app.
  • We did not sign in to Airtel or Adobe, redeem the offer, submit payment details, upload content or generate/export a design. These instructions are based on the official pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is Adobe Express Premium free with Airtel?

Yes, for 12 months from activation, for active Airtel users. You must activate it by 27 January 2027, per Adobe's India page.

What if I already pay for Express Premium?

Airtel's FAQ says existing Premium customers are eligible and the complimentary subscription is added alongside the existing one. It does not explain how your existing bill changes. Do not assume your paid plan stops or is refunded; verify the account and billing details before redemption.

Is Premium the full Creative Cloud suite?

No. It is Adobe Express Premium. It does not give you standalone Photoshop or Illustrator subscriptions. Premium assets have licence limits, and commercial-ready AI is not a guarantee that every output or use is legally risk-free. Check asset and feature terms before sharing client work.

Are 250 credits 250 finished designs?

No. They are a monthly allowance for generative features, not a fixed count of finished designs. Consumption depends on the feature. Airtel says you can wait for the monthly reset or buy more after the allowance runs out. We did not test usage or rollover.
