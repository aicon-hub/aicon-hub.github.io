Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Quiz Competition 2026: free e-certificate on MyGov, open till 14 April 2027

By AiCoN | Published 25 October 2026 | Sources checked 25 October 2026

The Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's Life and Contributions Quiz Competition 2026 is a free online quiz organised by the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre (DAIC) under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and hosted on MyGov. It has 10 multiple-choice questions to be answered in 300 seconds, questions are picked randomly from a question bank, there is no negative marking, and every participant receives an e-certificate of participation. It is open to all Indian citizens and runs until 14 April 2027, so there is no hurry: you can take it any day this year.

At a glance

  • What: free online quiz on the life and contributions of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
  • Who: open to all Indian citizens
  • Format: 10 multiple-choice questions in 300 seconds, no negative marking
  • Reward: e-certificate of participation for every participant
  • Window: 14 April 2026 to 14 April 2027 (11:45 pm)
  • Where: quiz.mygov.in, run by the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment; no fee

How to take the quiz

Checked on the live quiz page on 25 October 2026. Playing the quiz needs a MyGov login; we did not log in or play, so the steps below describe the route the page presents, not a completed test.

  1. Open the quiz page: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's Life and Contributions Quiz Competition 2026 on MyGov.
  2. Choose Login with MyGov AUTH to Play Quiz or Login with OTP to Play Quiz and sign in or create a free MyGov account.
  3. Update your MyGov profile (full name, address, contact details) before playing. The terms make an accurate profile mandatory for qualifying for the winner list.
  4. Start the quiz: 10 multiple-choice questions, 300 seconds in total, no negative marking. Questions are drawn randomly from a question bank, so retakes can differ.
  5. Download your e-certificate of participation after submitting.

Rules that matter

  • Open to all Indian citizens; free.
  • Using unfair means, impersonation or double participation gets the entry rejected.
  • Employees of the associated ministries or organisations, and their immediate family members, are not eligible to participate.
  • The organisers can amend the terms or cancel the competition; disputes fall under Delhi jurisdiction.

Stay safe

  • The quiz is free. Never pay anyone who promises a certificate, a prize or a winning slot.
  • Play only on quiz.mygov.in, the official MyGov quiz platform. Lookalike quiz pages are common.
  • The e-certificate goes to every participant; treat "guaranteed winner" claims as fraud.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a deadline soon? No. The quiz opened on 14 April 2026 and runs until 14 April 2027. You can take it any day.

Is there a cash prize? The page promises an e-certificate of participation to all. It mentions a winner list tied to an updated MyGov profile but states no cash prize in the sections we read.

Can I retake the quiz? The terms do not promise a retake; questions are randomly picked per attempt, and double participation is listed as grounds for rejection. Treat it as one attempt.

Do I need an account? Yes. Playing requires logging in with MyGov AUTH or OTP; account creation is free.
