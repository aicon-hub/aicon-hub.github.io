iPhone Visual Intelligence: point the camera, learn about what you see, and which iPhones get it

By AiCoN | Published 12 October 2026 | Sources checked 12 October 2026

Apple says Visual Intelligence lets your iPhone camera identify things, translate text and look up places. It needs an iPhone 15 Pro or 15 Pro Max, an iPhone 16 model or later, or iPhone Air. Apple says usage limits may apply.

What it is

Visual Intelligence is an Apple Intelligence feature on iPhone. Apple's support page says you point the iPhone camera at what is in front of you and it can search for visually similar images online, show details about a restaurant or business, translate or summarise text, identify plants and animals, and add an event to your calendar from a poster or ticket.

Is it free?

Apple lists no current Visual Intelligence charge; limits apply, and Apple says expanded access to certain AI features will cost extra in future. Its current pages say some server-side Apple Intelligence features have daily limits that vary by feature, request complexity and other factors. They do not publish a numeric Visual Intelligence allowance or a price for that future expanded access. This is not a promise of unlimited free use. A compatible iPhone is required.

Which iPhones

  • Apple says Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 works on iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later and iPhone Air.
  • Your Siri and device language must match the same supported language. English is on the list.
  • The current support pages describe iOS 27. Apple's 31 March 2025 India announcement also lists earlier Visual Intelligence support: iOS 18.2 or later on iPhone 16/16 Plus/16 Pro/16 Pro Max, iOS 18.3 or later on iPhone 16e, and iOS 18.4 or later on iPhone 15 Pro/15 Pro Max. Apple Intelligence must be on.
  • Apple says some features may not be available in every region or language.

How to start it

  • Camera Control: click and hold it, on models that have it.
  • Action button: set it to Visual Intelligence in settings.
  • Lock Screen or Control Center: add the Visual Intelligence button.
  • Camera app: if you have Siri AI (beta) turned on, open Camera and tap Siri.

After opening it, point the camera at the subject. Tap Search for similar images, the business name for place details, or the result for a plant or animal. For a poster or ticket, tap Add to Calendar, then Create Event. For text, choose the available action at the bottom. Close results with the close button, then swipe up from the bottom to leave Visual Intelligence.

The Camera > Siri route requires Siri AI (beta) on iOS 27 or later. Apple says it starts in English and is not initially available in the EU on iOS.

What to know about data

  • The image search option says it is subject to your search provider's terms and privacy policies, per Apple.
  • Apple says you can turn on a ChatGPT extension to ask ChatGPT about what you are looking at. That is optional and sends the question outside Apple's own feature. AiCoN advice: read the prompt on screen before you use it, and do not point it at private documents.
  • We did not find a plain statement on Apple's Visual Intelligence page about what is stored. We are not saying anything beyond what Apple's page says.

India

Apple's India support page lists English among supported languages and says features are available in most regions. Apple's 31 March 2025 India newsroom announcement, "Apple Intelligence features are now available in India", includes Visual Intelligence and localised English for India. Some features may differ by region, so check on your own iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay Apple for it?

Apple lists no current Visual Intelligence charge. Usage limits may apply, and expanded access to certain Apple Intelligence features will cost extra in future. You need a compatible iPhone.

Does it work on older iPhones?

Apple lists iPhone 15 Pro and 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, and iPhone Air. Ordinary iPhone 15 and 15 Plus are not listed.

Is ChatGPT required?

No. The ChatGPT option is an extension you choose to turn on, per Apple.
