AICTE Pragati Scholarship 2026-27: ₹50,000 a year for girl students, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 24 October 2026 | Sources checked 24 October 2026

The AICTE Pragati Scholarship pays ₹50,000 a year to girl students studying in the first year of an AICTE-approved technical degree or diploma, or in the second year through lateral entry. Applications for 2026-27 close on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) on Saturday, 31 October 2026 - one week away. There are 5,000 degree and 5,000 diploma seats, and selection is on your qualifying-exam marks, not a test.

Key dates on NSP right now

  • Scheme opened for applications: 1 June 2026.
  • Last date for students to apply: Saturday, 31 October 2026.
  • Defective-application correction and institute verification: until 15 November 2026.
  • District, state and ministry-level verification: until 30 November 2026.

These dates are from the live National Scholarship Portal scheme listing for "AICTE - Pragati Scholarship Scheme For Girl Students (Technical Degree)" and the parallel "(Technical Diploma)" listing, checked today.

Who can apply

  • Girl students admitted to the first year of an AICTE-approved degree or diploma course in 2026-27, or to the second year through lateral entry.
  • Family income from all sources not more than ₹8 lakh a year; a valid income certificate issued by the State or UT government is required.
  • Maximum two girl children per family can hold Pragati.
  • The gap between passing the qualifying exam and joining the course must be two years or less.
  • You must not be receiving any other scholarship, stipend, salary or emolument. If another grant is found, Pragati is discontinued and the amount must be returned to AICTE.

Amount and seats

₹50,000 a year as a lump sum towards college fees, a computer, books, stationery, equipment and software - for up to four years (three years for lateral-entry students). No extra grant for hostel or medical costs. The scheme earmarks 5,000 degree scholarships and 5,000 diploma scholarships a year, distributed state and UT-wise (Kerala's degree share is 196). Unfilled degree seats move to the diploma list. Separately, every eligible girl applicant from the 13 Union Territories and North-Eastern states listed in the guidelines receives the scholarship.

How selection works

Selection is purely on merit in the qualifying examination (10+2 or equivalent for degree, 10th for diploma), with state and UT-wise merit lists. Ties are broken by 10th-standard marks, then by age (older candidate ranks higher). Reservation follows Government of India norms; vacant reserved-category seats move to the general category.

How to apply on NSP

  1. Open scholarships.gov.in and complete One Time Registration (OTR) if you are new - a 14-digit number issued against Aadhaar that stays valid for your whole academic career.
  2. Log in and open the application form; select the Pragati degree or diploma scheme as applicable.
  3. Fill the compulsory fields and upload documents (income certificate, marksheets, institute admission proof); personal and educational credentials are verified through DigiLocker.
  4. Use "Save as Draft" if needed, but press "Final Submit" before the portal closes on 31 October 2026.
  5. After submission, your institute must verify the form by 15 November - tell your scholarship cell to expect it.
  6. Renewal happens on NSP each year with your marksheet and a letter from the head of your institution; failing to get promoted forfeits the scholarship.
  7. Payment status can be tracked later under "Know Your Payment" on the PFMS portal.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not wait for the last evening - NSP slows near deadlines, and your institute still needs days to verify. Do not apply while holding another scholarship, and check that your income certificate is current and issued by the State or UT authority. Diploma students must pick the diploma listing, not the degree one. And if you joined directly into the second year, you qualify only if that was through lateral entry.

Frequently asked questions

Can two sisters in one family both get Pragati?

Yes. A maximum of two girl children per family are eligible.

Is there an entrance test for Pragati?

No. Selection is on qualifying-examination marks through state and UT-wise merit lists.

What if my application is marked defective?

Corrections can be made until 15 November 2026, but do not rely on the defective window - a clean first submission is safer.
