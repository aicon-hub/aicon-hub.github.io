Industrial R&D for Viksit Bharat 2047 Quiz: MyGov e-certificate, ends 20 November

By AiCoN | Published 25 October 2026 | Sources checked 25 October 2026

The Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Ministry of Science and Technology, and MyGov are running the Quiz on Industrial R&D for Viksit Bharat 2047. It is open to all Indian citizens until 20 November 2026, 11:45 PM. Every participant gets a certificate of participation. There is no cash prize.

Good to knowThe reward is a participation certificate only. You need a MyGov login to play: the quiz page shows login options, and the quiz starts only after you sign in and click Play Quiz.

At a glance

  • Organiser: Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Ministry of Science and Technology, with MyGov
  • Dates: 21 October 2026 to 20 November 2026, 11:45 PM
  • Format: 10 questions in 300 seconds (5 minutes)
  • Who: all Indian citizens
  • Reward: certificate of participation for all participants

Rules from the terms

  • The quiz starts as soon as you click Play Quiz, and all questions must be answered within the time limit
  • One person can take the quiz only once; multiple entries are not accepted
  • Entries using unfair means, impersonation or double participation are declared null and void
  • Employees of DSIR, the organising department and MyGov are not eligible to participate
  • Organisers are not responsible for lost, late or incomplete entries, and proof of submission is not proof of receipt
  • DSIR can amend, postpone or cancel the quiz, and its decision is final

How to take the quiz: step by step

Checked on the live MyGov quiz page on 25 October 2026.

  1. Open the official quiz page: quiz.mygov.in/quiz/quiz-on-industrial-rd-for-viksit-bharat-2047. The page shows the quiz banner, the end date (20 November 2026, 11:45 PM), 10 questions and 300 seconds.
  2. Tap Login with MyGov AUTH to Play Quiz or Login with OTP to Play Quiz. The page does not open the quiz until you sign in.
  3. New to MyGov: register with MyGov Auth or with DigiLocker (MeriPehchaan single sign-on). Already registered: enter your email or mobile number and request the OTP.
  4. Enter the OTP sent to your email/mobile to log in.
  5. Come back to the quiz page, read the Terms and Conditions, then click Play Quiz - the 5-minute timer starts the moment you click.
  6. Answer the 10 questions and submit. You get only one attempt.
  7. After you finish, download your certificate of participation.

Steps 3 to 7 happen inside your own login, so they are not shown as screenshots.

Why this quiz

Industrial R&D for Viksit Bharat 2047 is about developing indigenous technologies, advanced manufacturing and innovative solutions for a developed India. It promotes collaboration among industries, startups, academia and government. Key areas include AI, semiconductors, biotechnology, clean energy, defence and smart infrastructure. DSIR runs this quiz to spread awareness of India's industrial research ecosystem.

We read the MyGov quiz page and its terms on 25 October 2026. The page was live with both login-to-play options visible. We did not take the quiz.
