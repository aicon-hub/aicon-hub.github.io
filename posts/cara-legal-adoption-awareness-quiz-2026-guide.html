CARA legal adoption awareness quiz: top 10 win ₹2,000 each, ends 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 8 October 2026 | Sources checked 8 October 2026

The Central Adoption Resource Authority (CARA) and MyGov are running the "No Shortcuts to Parenthood" Legal Adoption Awareness Quiz. It has 10 questions in 300 seconds, is open to all Indian citizens, and the top 10 winners get ₹2,000 each. Every participant gets an e-certificate. It runs until 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

Check before you playThe MyGov quiz page showed a "Closed" label when we opened it without logging in, although the dates run to 31 October 2026 and the quiz list shows it as running. Open the page yourself and confirm it lets you start. Entries cannot be withdrawn once submitted.

What is the quiz about?

The official page says the quiz aims to improve understanding of the legal provisions under the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015, to highlight the risks of illegal adoption, and to promote adoption through the Mission Vatsalya Portal as the only lawful pathway.

Rules from the official page

WhoAll Indian citizens.
Format10 questions in 300 seconds, picked randomly from a question bank.
MarkingNo negative marking.
Dates22 June 2026, 11:00 AM to 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.
DetailsYou give your name, email and phone number. Impersonation or double participation voids an entry.

What are the prizes?

  • Top 10 winners: ₹2,000 each
  • E-certificate of participation for every participant

Entering does not guarantee a cash prize.

A careful checklist

  1. Open quiz.mygov.in and find the quiz yourself.
  2. Read the Terms and Conditions on the page.
  3. Start when you have 5 uninterrupted minutes. The timer runs for 300 seconds.
  4. Finish before 31 October 2026, 11:45 PM.

We did not log in or play. We read the prizes and the first seven rules, not every clause.

Related CARA and MyGov contests

The same campaign also has a poster competition (top 15 win ₹3,000 each) and a Myth vs Reality infographic contest (top 10 win ₹2,000 each), both ending 31 October 2026. They are separate entries on MyGov.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an entry fee?

The page we read does not mention one.

Is this about adopting a child?

No. It is an awareness quiz. The page names the Mission Vatsalya Portal as the lawful route for adoption.
