Figma for Education free for students: Professional tier with education verification

By AiCoN | Published 20 October 2026 | Sources checked 20 October 2026

Figma, the collaborative design tool used by product teams worldwide, says on its education page that Figma and FigJam are "100% free for students, educators and schools", with "no freemium or hidden upgrades, ever". Verified students and educators get the Professional tier free, K-12 school districts can get the Enterprise tier free, and there is no deadline - it is an ongoing programme.

Good to knowYou need to be 13 or older and part of a structured classroom (school, university, trade school, online course, bootcamp or academy) to apply on your own. Figma verifies your education status during signup and can re-check it later. Children under 13 get access only when their school signs up for the free Enterprise tier. Figma's education page also says eligible education users get "full access to Figma's AI toolkit, including Figma Make and AI-powered tools on the canvas".

Who can apply

  • Students and educators aged 13 or older in a structured classroom
  • Classrooms can be virtual or in-person: Figma lists schools, universities, trade schools, online courses, bootcamps and academies as examples
  • A free Figma account, created during signup
  • K-12 school districts and institutions can instead get the free Enterprise tier, which is also the route for students under 13

What you get (checked 20 October 2026)

  • Figma Professional tier free once your education status is verified
  • Figma Design, FigJam, Figma Slides, Figma Draw and Figma Motion
  • Dev Mode and the MCP server to bridge design and code
  • Figma's AI toolkit, including Figma Make and AI-powered tools on the canvas (Figma's own wording on the education page)
  • K-12 districts: free access to the Enterprise tier

How to apply: step by step

  1. Create a free Figma account, or log in to your existing account.
  1. Fill in the education verification form with your school or institution details and submit it. Figma reviews and verifies your education status.
  2. Once verified, create an Education team in Figma - your workspace now runs on the free Professional tier.
