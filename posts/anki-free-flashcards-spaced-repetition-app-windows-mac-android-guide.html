Anki: free flashcard app with spaced repetition for exam revision

By AiCoN | Published 18 October 2026 | Sources checked 18 October 2026

Anki is a free, open-source flashcard app that uses spaced repetition: it shows you a card again just before you are likely to forget it. The computer version (Windows, Mac, Linux) and the Android app (AnkiDroid) are free. Only the iPhone/iPad app (AnkiMobile) is paid, and that money funds Anki's development.

Good to knowAnkiDroid on Android is free and made by volunteers; AnkiMobile on iOS is the only paid app, sold by the Anki team to fund development. Free sync across devices runs through AnkiWeb, which needs a free account. Shared decks made by other users vary in quality - check a deck before you trust it for an exam.

Why students like it

  • Spaced repetition: cards you find hard come back sooner, easy ones later, so revision time goes to weak spots
  • Works offline after setup; sync across phone and computer with free AnkiWeb
  • Cards can hold audio, images, video and scientific notation
  • Handles very large decks (100,000+ cards)
  • Thousands of free shared decks to download, plus add-ons to extend it

Platforms and cost

  • Windows 10+, macOS 13+, Linux: free (current version 26.09.3)
  • Android: AnkiDroid, free, developed by contributors
  • iPhone/iPad: AnkiMobile, the only paid app; purchases fund Anki's development

How to start

  1. Download Anki for your platform from apps.ankiweb.net
  2. Create a deck and add cards, or grab a free shared deck
  3. Review daily - rate each card honestly and Anki schedules the next review
  4. Make a free AnkiWeb account to sync between devices
