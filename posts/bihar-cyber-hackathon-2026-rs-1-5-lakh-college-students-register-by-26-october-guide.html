Bihar Cyber Hackathon 2026: ₹1.5 lakh first prize, college students, register by 26 October

By AiCoN | Published 15 October 2026 | Sources checked 15 October 2026

C-DAC Patna and the Cyber Crime and Security Unit of Bihar Police are running the Cyber Hackathon 2026. It is open to college students across India and is free to enter. The top three teams win ₹1.5 lakh (about US$1,800), ₹1 lakh (about US$1,200) and ₹50,000 (about US$600). Registration and submission both close on 26 October 2026, 11:59 PM.

Good to knowSome news reports give 28 November as the results date. The official guidelines list the shortlist on 20 November and the online Grand Final on 27 November 2026. We follow the official site. Prizes are for teams, and the site says awards are subject to the organisers' terms.

Who can enter

  • Students currently enrolled in a recognised college or university in India
  • Teams of 3 to 4. At least one woman member is encouraged, not required
  • One team per person and one problem statement per team. The roster and problem choice lock once confirmed
  • Every member uploads a valid college ID. An ID for the team leader alone is not enough

The five problem statements

  • Mule bank account detection and financial fraud investigation
  • Android app security and forensic analysis
  • Detecting coordinated disinformation on social media
  • Tracking and prioritising public grievances raised on social media
  • Checking digital evidence (images, audio, video, CCTV) for tampering

Pick one on the problem statements page. Any language, framework or tool is allowed.

Prizes

  • First: ₹1.5 lakh
  • Second: ₹1 lakh
  • Third: ₹50,000
  • Certificate and internship consideration are listed too. Internships are at the organisers' sole discretion

How it works

  1. Register on the portal, verify emails, and add 3 to 4 members with college IDs and GitHub handles
  2. The team leader creates a private GitHub repository named after the team and adds every member
  3. Build and commit as you go. A single last-minute code dump is grounds for disqualification, and so is any commit after 26 October
  4. Record an 8 to 10 minute explainer and demo video. Upload it to your own Google Drive, share it as Viewer with the email on the guidelines page, and submit the link on the portal
  5. Experts screen the videos and shortlist 25 teams, published 20 November 2026
  6. Shortlisted teams hand over repository ownership to the organisers. The online Grand Final is on 27 November 2026, with live demos, questions from judges and possibly a new dataset

Rules to read

  • No registration or entry fee
  • Plagiarism, fake IDs and multiple team registrations lead to disqualification
  • All team members must attend the final with a webcam, microphone and stable internet
  • The organising committee's decisions are final

We read the official site, its guidelines, FAQ and problem statements pages on 15 October 2026. We did not register. Start early: the site asks teams to submit before the last day in case of server load.
