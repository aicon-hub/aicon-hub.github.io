DeepL Translator free version: 50,000 characters a month, and what it does with your text

By AiCoN | Published 13 October 2026 | Sources checked 13 October 2026

DeepL has a free version with 50,000 characters a month. DeepL says free-version text is used to train its models, so keep personal data out.

Official DeepL logo

No official video is used.

What it is

DeepL Translator is a machine translation service. You paste text or upload a file, pick a language, and get a translation. DeepL's language page lists Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, Nepali and Assamese among many others.

Is it free?

Yes, there is a free version. DeepL's terms say private and business use of DeepL Translator (free) is free of charge. DeepL's plan table lists the free version at 50,000 characters per month, 1 file translation per month (up to 5 MB), 1 glossary with 5 entries per language pair, and 1 saved translation. DeepL's help center says file translation needs a free DeepL account. DeepL also says it can change, reduce or expand the free services at its discretion. Paid Pro plans are separate.

India availability

DeepL's country page lists India under where paid plans can be bought. That page is about paid plans only. We could not find a DeepL page that states whether the free version is offered in India, so treat that as not confirmed by DeepL. The DeepL SE Google Play listing says the Android app is free to download, with some features needing a paid plan. That is not a confirmation of free-service access from India.

Privacy: read this first

  • DeepL's privacy policy says that with the free version, it processes your text and translations for a limited period to train and improve its neural networks.
  • DeepL's terms say the free services may not be used for content containing confidential or personal data of any kind. That is only allowed on a Pro subscription.
  • DeepL's Pro page lists "no data training" as a Pro feature.
  • AiCoN advice: on the free version, do not paste ID numbers, medical or bank details, or private messages.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, a free version exists, with monthly limits of 50,000 characters and 1 file.

Does it train on my text?

DeepL says the free version does. It lists no data training under Pro.

Does it support Indian languages?

DeepL's language list includes Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, Nepali and Assamese.

What changed since this post?

DeepL sources rechecked on 13 October 2026. The plan table still lists the stated free limits. Its terms allow free services to change and prohibit confidential or personal data; its privacy policy says free content is processed to train and improve networks. File translation needs an account; the document help page says files translated without a subscription are not editable. Feature support varies by language. India free access remains unconfirmed; no translation or free-account test was performed.
