Free coaching for JEE, NEET, UPSC and more: govt scheme with Rs 4,000 monthly stipend, apply by 31 October

By AiCoN | Published 18 October 2026 | Sources checked 18 October 2026

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment runs a free coaching scheme for competitive exams for SC and OBC students and PM CARES Children beneficiaries. Coaching happens at empanelled Central Universities, the course fee is fully paid, and students also get a stipend of Rs 4,000 a month for up to 12 months. Applications for 2026-27 are on the National Scholarship Portal and close on 31 October 2026.

Good to knowOnly 3,500 students are selected each year, and each Central University selects its own students on merit. 30% of slots are reserved for women. You need Aadhaar-based biometric attendance; repeated unexplained absence can cancel the benefit. SC and OBC students need a family income of Rs 8 lakh or less; PM CARES Children beneficiaries have no income or caste conditions and are exempt from the selection process.

Exams covered

  • UPSC, SSC and Railway (RRB) Group A and B exams, and State Public Service Commission exams
  • Officer-grade exams of banks, insurance companies and PSUs
  • Entrance exams: JEE (engineering), NEET (medical), CAT (management), CLAT (law)
  • SAT, GRE, GMAT, IELTS and TOEFL; NDA and CDS entrance

Who can apply

  • SC and OBC students with total family income up to Rs 8 lakh a year
  • Beneficiaries of the PM CARES Children Scheme (with the Central Government certificate) - no income or caste conditions
  • SC:OBC ratio is roughly 70:30 within each university's slots

What you get

  • Course fee paid in full under the scheme
  • Stipend of Rs 4,000 per month for the full course duration, up to 12 months
  • Rs 15,000 incentive for interview coaching if you clear the mains of Central or State Civil Services (Group A/B) exams
  • All payments by Direct Benefit Transfer to an Aadhaar-linked bank account

How to apply

  1. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) on the National Scholarship Portal
  2. Apply under "Free Coaching for SCs OBCs and Beneficiaries of PM CARES Children Scheme"
  3. Watch for the empanelled Central University's selection and joining instructions

Dates on the NSP scheme list (checked 18 October 2026): student applications close 31 October 2026; institute verification 15 November 2026; state/nodal verification 30 November 2026. Benefits and rules are from the scheme guidelines published on NSP (revised from 2023-24).
