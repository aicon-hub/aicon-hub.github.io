Resourify: find digital deals, then verify them

By AiCoN | 6 October 2026 | 5 min read

Resourify is a directory of software discounts, trials and credits. Use it to discover leads, not as proof that a free offer is still available or applies to your Indian account.

What is a deals directory?

A directory collects links and descriptions from different companies. Resourify presents offers across software and digital services, including student and developer categories. It is not the vendor delivering every subscription. A timestamp or a verified label on a listing does not replace the original company's current terms.

Browsing versus claiming an offer

The public directory can be read without claiming a subscription. Each vendor decides what is needed to redeem its offer. That may include an account, school-issued email, identity check, card or a country restriction. Browsing a listing and completing a vendor checkout are separate actions with different privacy and billing consequences.

How to use it, step by step

  1. Start at resourify.com and browse a relevant category. Write down the exact product and the offer that interests you.
  2. Read the listing carefully for expiry, region, eligibility and whether it is a trial, credit or permanent free plan.
  3. Open the company's own offer page through an official route. Compare its current terms with the directory text.
  4. Check India availability. A US trial or a select-country education programme may not apply to a Kerala student.
  5. Confirm the verification method. Do not upload student identification to an unfamiliar form merely because a directory linked it.
  6. Check whether a card is required, whether billing begins automatically and how to cancel. Record the renewal amount, not only the first charge.
  7. For credits, confirm eligible services, expiry and what happens when the credit runs out. Free credits can coexist with usage charges.
  8. Claim only after the official page and final checkout agree with your intent. Save the terms or confirmation for later reference.

What does free actually mean?

A free plan may have usage limits. A trial ends after a period. A promotional credit offsets some eligible spending. A student discount lowers a price but may still cost money. A zero first-year price does not necessarily mean the next year is free. Do not combine these terms or assume an offer covers every add-on.

For students in India

Indian institutions do not all use .edu addresses. Follow the actual vendor's accepted verification rules rather than the directory's broad shorthand. Some offers require a recognised institution or specific course, not just an email address. Educational licences can restrict commercial use. Check these limits before using the tool for paid client work.

Currency, cards and renewal risk

A USD price is not a fixed INR charge. Taxes, exchange rates and card-provider charges can change the total. Annual billing may require the whole year upfront despite displaying a monthly equivalent. Put the end date and cancellation deadline somewhere you will see them. Deleting an app or stopping use may not cancel a subscription.

How to assess a stale or doubtful listing

The checked directory includes expired entries alongside other offers. That is a reason to verify, not a reason to treat every entry as false. If the company no longer mentions an offer, do not infer it is hidden or ask a stranger to activate it. Avoid paying an intermediary to unlock a student deal. Contact the vendor through its official support channel if the terms remain unclear.

Sharing deals responsibly

When passing an offer to classmates, include the official vendor link, region and any limits you verified. Do not forward an untested headline promising a free year. A deal that worked for one person can fail for another because of country, account history or eligibility. Keep personal verification documents out of group chats.

Frequently asked questions

Does a listing guarantee I can claim it?

No. Vendor eligibility and current terms decide.

Are all listed trials available in India?

No. Check country restrictions for each offer.

Do credits mean there will be no bill?

No. Check what they cover, their expiry and overage charges.

Should I send student ID to the directory?

Use the verified official vendor's required process and share only what is necessary.
